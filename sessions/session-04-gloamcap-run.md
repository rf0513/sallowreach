# Set Piece — The Gloamcap Run

*Leavy's spotlight: "Persona Non Grata" + the Calling. **Slot: Session 4 or 5**, whenever the table turns toward Orna's errand. Mostly a solo-in-town beat with tag-along jobs for the others. Quest origin: [[orna-tessant#The Light Quest — "Persona Non Grata"|Orna's file]] (Lorne, the Lastlight ransom — all still true; this file makes it runnable). Arc context: [[leavy-arc]].*

---

## GM Quick Reference

- **Goal on paper:** fetch a dram of **gloamcap distillate** from the Alchemists' Consortium. Orna's banned.
- **Actually delivering:** the arc's opening clue volley + **Seven's arrival.**
- **The rolls** *(compress Beat 5 to one roll if the night runs long; difficulties legal as-is — the SRD sets no fixed bands, GM-set 5–30, [[STATUS]] SRD Facts)*:

| Roll | Trait | Diff | Stakes |
|---|---|:--:|---|
| Front of house | Presence | ~11 *(waive for the form bit)* | delay, escalation, a supervisor |
| Demonstration: the Base | Knowledge | ~11 | Lorne's respect; the dram |
| Demonstration: the Turn | Finesse | ~13 *(adv. if the hands lead)* | the fold is seen either way; Fear = memory bleed + Stress |
| The pen scene | Presence | ~13 *(or pure RP)* | Lastlight written vs. the debt; Orna's panic glimpsed |
| The assist: keeping up | Knowledge | ~13 | success unlocks the panacea read |
| The assist: the hold | Finesse | ~13 | both successes unlock the notebook read |
- **Clues out tonight:** the fold (shown + said) · **Orna's truth — all-cure, self-testing, the conversion (shown + said, Beat 5)** · the old bond (shown + said) · the rue sprig (**shown only — hold the say**) · first ghost-ink (shown; Orna half-says it).
- **Pacing valve:** this is a full night. If it's dragging, Beat 5 slides cleanly to the next morning — the Calling (Beat 6) only needs dusk, and the ghost-ink button (Beat 7) can ride on any later gloamcap drop.
- **Props:** Form 11-C as a real handout · a pressed sprig (real dried herb if you can get it) · the ghost-page (white crayon + wash, if you want the craft project).
- **NPCs:** [[orna-tessant|Orna]] · **Magister Auguste Lorne** (stores-keeper, per Orna's file) · a front-desk clerk (grab a name from [[improv-ammo]] — Cosette Vasseur works) · **[[seven|Seven]]** (full sheet + phrasebook: [[seven]]).
- **Fear spends:** another form appears · a supervisor is summoned · Lorne's attention sharpens · memory bleed on the brew (mark a Stress, give the table the fragment — [[leavy-arc#Costs & Gifts|arc rules]]).
- **Tag-alongs:** Tenson hears "regulatory body" and materializes (he redlines, Leavy files — let them duet). Fletcher is the bird-spotter at the Calling. Isotta gets one quiet cross-spark (below).

## Beat 1 — Orna's Send-Off *(cold open, comedy)*

*Orna supplies zero credentials and total confidence. Play her mid-thought, as always.*

> Leavy — Orna's got your shopping list ready. It's written on the back of a seed packet. It says, and I quote: "Gloamcap. THE GOOD DRAM. Tell them it's for the garden. Do not mention the fire."
>
> You ask about the fire. She waves a hand. "Boring story. Old story. *Their* story, if anyone asks." She's already walking away. Then she stops. "Oh — they'll know you're mine the moment you talk shop. That's either a problem or a password. Depends which alchemist answers the bell."

**How to play:** she will not explain the ban. She genuinely believes this errand is simple. The carrot stands: gloamcap first, familiar after — *tonight*, if they hurry back before dusk.

## Beat 2 — The Chapter House, Front of House

**Three aspects:** brass nameplates polished daily by someone who resents it · air scrubbed of every reagent smell (a building this clean is hiding its own stains) · a numbered-chit queue system with no queue in it.

**Form 11-C — Declaration of Intended Use.** Hand the player the actual form. A super-literal clank will complete it perfectly, in full, in order. **Reward the bit:** the clerk gets quietly emotional — *"Nobody has ever filled in Section F."* If the form is played to the hilt, waive the roll; front-of-house simply *works* for the first applicant in Consortium history to respect the process.

🎲 Otherwise: **Presence ~11** to navigate the chit system and clerk politics.

**Saying "Tessant":** the room drops two degrees. A passing junior stops walking. Contempt, curiosity, or grief — pick per NPC (Orna's file has the range).

## Beat 3 — Magister Lorne & the Demonstration

Lorne per [[orna-tessant|Orna's file]]: keeper of the stores, decides who touches restricted stock, respects exactly one thing — documented work. He reads the formulary in silence for a long time. Too long. Then:

> "Anyone can carry a book." He slides a burner across the bench. "The bench is there. Show me a Third Stabilization — your hands, my glass."

🎲 **The Base — Knowledge ~11.** 🎲 **The Turn — Finesse ~13**, advantage if Leavy lets the hands lead.

**Whatever the dice say, the fold happens:** Leavy's hands, unbidden, fold the ash *inward.* On a roll with Fear, add the memory bleed — one beat of someone else's cadence out of Leavy's own mouth: *"Nine measures, not seven—"* — and it's gone. Mark a Stress. *(Legal GM move on a Fear roll — making a PC mark Stress is on the SRD's move list.)*

**The recognition** *(the clue, said aloud — this is the line the session exists for):*

> Lorne goes still. Not offended-still. *Careful*-still.
>
> "Where did you train?"
>
> *(and Leavy can't answer that, can they — sit in it)*
>
> "That fold. Ash folded in, not out. That's old-coven work. Hedge-order. Northern. The Nine Hedges did it that way — I watched one of them do exactly that at a symposium, years back." A beat. "Odd, is all. Because as I heard it… those hands are dead."

**How to play:** Lorne is not a threat and doesn't pursue it — but he's a correspondent, and he *will* mention this in a letter to a northern colleague. (A thread, never a clock — [[leavy-arc#Bindweed — guardrails|guardrails]].)

**The price:** he releases the dram against the ransom from Orna's file — bring back **Lastlight, written down.** His grief-line about Orna lands here (*"Best I ever saw… you can't help someone who won't be helped"*), plus the second-source plant of the lost miracle.

## Beat 4 — The Pen and the Genius *(back at the Margin)*

Don't run this as persuasion against a wall. Orna isn't *opposed* to writing Lastlight down — she's never met a reason that outranked the next experiment. **Leavy is the reason,** if the player finds the move. The winning play is the documentarian's own gesture: the immaculate formulary open on the bench, a fresh page, the pen held out. *"I will hold the page still."*

🎲 **Presence ~13**, or waive it for good RP.

- **Success:** Lastlight, in Orna's hand — the first recipe she has ever recorded. The Consortium relationship banks (standing access; Lorne pays out and hates how pleased he is).
- **On Fear or failure:** she gets three lines in, stops, and stares at her own handwriting like it's a stranger's — and has to leave the room. *(The panic under the "not caring" — her file's secret, glimpsed once.)* The dram can still be released on a promise, with the formulary as collateral — Lorne's choice, and a debt that humanizes him.

## Beat 5 — "A Little Assistance" *(afternoon, the Verdant Margin — the Orna reveal)*

*Leavy's back with the dram. This is the scene where Orna's file stops being a secret: the all-cure, the self-testing, and what it's costing her — revealed not by a speech, but by a failed brew and a Witch sharp enough to read the wreckage. The brew is doomed no matter what the dice say. The rolls decide how much Leavy* understands *when it dies.*

**Three aspects:** a bench actually *cleared* — you didn't know the wood was green · a row of vessels, smallest to biggest, arranged with terrifying care by someone whose every other surface is chaos · a chair. Set out. For Leavy.

> You get back to the Margin and Orna's waiting. "Oh good. You're here." She takes the dram like it's nothing — like you didn't spend a whole morning on it — and holds it up to the light. "Right. Before your bird. I need a *little* assistance. Tiny thing. Ten minutes. I need a third pair of hands."
>
> You have two hands. She has two hands. …You do the math on that.

**How to play:** she will not explain what they're brewing. She gives instructions like *"the thing with the salt — no, the* thing, *you know the thing"* and is genuinely startled every time Leavy needs more than that. The understatement is the runner: *"tiny thing,"* she says, wheeling out a burner the size of a dog.

🎲 **Keeping up — Knowledge ~13** (her instructions are garbage; success means Leavy translates genius-shorthand in real time). 🎲 **The hold — Finesse ~13** (a two-hand stage where a third hand is genuinely needed — and Orna solves it by plunging her *barnacled* hand straight into the boiling glass, without flinching, without noticing she did it. **Show it. Don't comment.**)

**Fear spends here:** a vessel cracks and she keeps going · the vapor makes Leavy's own hands start an old fold uninvited (cross-clue, mark a Stress) · Orna tastes a *mid-stage* — "calibration, dear."

**The failure** *(scripted — no roll touches this):*

> The last stage comes together and for about four seconds it's beautiful. Gold. Clear. It looks like *medicine* — like the idea of medicine. And then it turns. Grey-green, from the bottom up, like something underwater closing over it.
>
> Orna watches it die. "Hm," she says. That's it. That's the eulogy. She writes nothing down.
>
> And then she pours a measure into a cup — and drinks it.

**The conversion, shown** *(the clue the whole scene exists for):*

> Leavy — you're a mixologist. You watch it hit. The color *moves* under her skin — up the wrist, into that barnacle crust on her forearm. And the crust **grows.** Not much. A fingernail's worth. Right there, while you watch.
>
> She looks at it the way you'd look at an interesting weather front. "Fascinating," she says. And the worst part is — she means it.

**What the rolls bought** *(failure never wastes the scene — it grades the read):*
- **Either roll succeeded — the panacea read:** *"You don't even need to taste the residue. The nose does it. That wasn't a tonic. That was aimed at* everything *at once — rot, wound, poison, fever. A shape of recipe that isn't supposed to exist. There's a word for it nobody serious says out loud: an all-cure."*
- **Both succeeded — the notebook read** *(the jewel — deliver the evidence, let the* player *say the conclusion):* *"And here's the thing you can't unsee: the crust isn't random. You can* read *it. That ridge line — salamander-base series, has to be. The green filament at her temple — moth-fern work, badly fixed. Every growth on her is a* result." *Then wait. If the player lands it — she DOES write everything down; she's the notebook — that's the table's line, not yours. (Fallback: Delphine says it plainly in a later session.)*
- **Both failed:** Leavy still saw the self-dose and the crust advance. The questions are free (below). The reads wait for another visit.

**If Leavy just asks** *(and the literal automaton will)* — Orna answers everything factual, cheerfully, no shame:
- *"You're testing it on yourself."* — **"Only honest lab I've got. Everything else lies politely."**
- *"It's converting you."* — **"Mm. Into what — that's the interesting part. Data all the way down, dear."**
- *"An all-cure. That's what you're chasing."* — **"An all-cure. Yes."** *(flat, plain, no drama — the confirmation IS the drama)*
- *"Why?"* — this is the one she can't do. **"Because it's possible. I've—"** *(stop. a beat too long.)* **"…Because it's possible."** And she's already moving: *"Now. You were promised something with opinions. Let's go call your bird."* *(Straight into Beat 6 — the deflection is the segue.)*
- If Leavy presses the cost — *"you are erasing yourself"* — she says it light as anything: **"Yes, well. It's mine to spend, dear."** And that line should sit wrong in a clank's chest for a week — because Leavy's self was *stolen*, and Orna's is being *spent on purpose*, and nobody at the bench says the mirror out loud. Don't help. Let it sit.

**Reveal / withhold at this scene:** revealed — the all-cure goal, the deliberate self-testing, the transformation *is* the experiments (shown by the dose, said in her own words). Withheld — the lost miracle and the racing panic underneath (*"I've—"* is the whole crack; Lorne planted the miracle this morning, and connecting the two is the player's job, maybe weeks from now), and who the cured person is ([[orna-tessant]] — still unnamed).

## Beat 6 — The Calling *(dusk, the Verdant Margin)*

**Three aspects:** chalk circle on the potting bench · a bowl of rainwater going gold in the last light · the bioluminescent beds waking up early, as if interested.

> Orna sets it up with terrifying confidence. "Circle's chalk, bowl's rainwater, the moth-fern does the actual work — don't ask. You sit in the middle. Something out in the Mire hears the invitation, and if you're lucky, something answers. Might take all night. Might take days. Might be a toad, might be a—"
>
> There's a bird on the rail.
>
> Black and white. And Fletcher — you clocked this bird *three streets back.* Same one from the roof this morning, you'd swear it. Magpies don't do that.
>
> It doesn't wait for the circle. It drops onto the bench, walks straight through the chalk like it isn't there, and starts *moving Leavy's kit.* Vial rack — two spaces left. Stirring rod — across the cloth. Everything ends up exactly… huh. Exactly where you'd want it. If you were about to brew.
>
> Orna has gone very, very quiet. Then: "That's not an answer to my ritual, dear. A new familiar doesn't know your bench. That bond is *old.* That bird knows you."

**Fletcher's channel** *(hand the clue to the PC whose skill spots it):* cue the Ranger — the bird's been shadowing Leavy for days; it's not wild, it's not local, and it looks *travel-worn* — feathers gone ragged the way only distance does. If the player hesitates, Orna is the fallback voice.

**Isotta cross-spark** *(optional, one line, no comment):* the magpie tracks *her reflection*, not her. Say it and move on.

**The gift:**

> It works something loose from under its wing feathers — something it has carried a long, long way. Sets it on the bench in front of Leavy. Steps back. Waits.
>
> A sprig. Pressed flat. Dried. Old. Small grey-green leaves.
>
> Leavy — Plant Encyclopedia, no roll needed, you just *know:* it's rue. The herb of grief. The old folk name is "herb-of-grace."
>
> The bird is looking at you like it's waiting for something.

**Do NOT explain.** The sprig is shown-only tonight; its say-aloud channel (the plant-name pattern, the rhyme) fires in later sessions per the [[leavy-arc#Clue cadence|ladder]]. If Leavy names the bird something devastatingly literal, every NPC adopts it on the spot — Orna first.

## Beat 7 — Button: the Ghost-Ink *(cliffhanger — end the night here)*

Orna decants the gloamcap — and, being Orna, flicks a drop at Leavy's open formulary: *"Hold still. I've had half a theory about your book since the day you walked in."*

> The drop soaks into a blank page. One that's *always* been blank.
>
> And lines come up. Faint. Rising like a bruise. Handwriting.
>
> Leavy — it's *your* handwriting. Yours, but older. Surer. And it says:
>
> *"—for the Cup. F. says nine measures. I say seven. We compromised on nine. (She is right. She is always right.)"*
>
> And that's where we end.

Orna's half-said clue, if the table needs a nudge before the lights go out: *"…your book is older than your notes, dear. Considerably."*

## Threads Opened

- **Lorne's letter north** — soft pressure, never a clock ([[leavy-arc#Bindweed — guardrails|guardrails]]).
- **Lastlight, written** (or the debt if the scene broke) — Consortium standing access; the Orna bond deepened either way.
- **Gloamcap = the revelator.** Every ghost-page costs a controlled reagent Orna also needs — the dosage dilemma, and a reason to keep the Consortium warm.
- **Seven's hoard** — next gifts queued: the nine-leafed pin, the snapped wand, the teacup shard, the empty frame ([[leavy-arc#Seven — the magpie|arc file]]).
- **"—for the Cup"** — the phrase Leavy will chase for the rest of the campaign.
- **Orna's elixir, in the open** — Leavy now knows what she's doing and what it costs her. The *why* (the lost miracle, this morning's Lorne plant) is still the player's to connect — and **who did Orna cure?** is now a live question worth an answer someday ([[orna-tessant]]: still needs a name).
- **The mirror, armed** — "It's mine to spend, dear." A stolen self watching a spent one. This pays off every future Leavy–Orna scene; never say it out loud for them.
- **Familiar mechanics** — settle with the player between sessions. *(Not an SRD check — the Witch class is playtest content, so the rules come from the table's build. Fallback chassis: the SRD Ranger's Beastbound companion framework.)*
- **The Tessant Cauldron follows within a session** ([[items/loot-ledger#⭐ The Tessant Cauldron|ledger]]) — Orna's gift, zero ceremony, ideally the next time Leavy's at the Margin with Seven reorganizing her kit: *"You stir like it matters. Things that are stirred like it matters remember. Take it before I dissolve enough to get sentimental."* The new bird deserves a bench worth organizing.
