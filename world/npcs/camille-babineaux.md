# Camille Babineaux

**Role:** Front-of-house hostess at [[the-pale-room|the Pale Room]] — the first face every guest meets, and the reason [[Marcel Dray]] opens every meeting already knowing your name
**Location:** [[the-pale-room|The Pale Room]]; family in Thornside
**Faction:** Marcel's operation (front of house); Thornside edge families by blood
**Tier:** 1

## Appearance

Sixteen, and the youngest face on the floor by twenty years — which is exactly why it works. Boardwalk-thin, dark hair pinned up too neatly for her age, an apron with pockets she keeps her hands near. She moves through the room like water finding level: never hurried, never still. Guests remember her smile. They do not remember how much they told her.

## Personality & Mannerisms

Warm, quick, and professionally unflappable — the genuine article, not a performance, which is what makes her disarming. She remembers *everything*: names, drinks, debts, the order people came in and who they looked at first. She survived a locked cage in a boat's bow by memorizing every detail that reached her through the slats, and the Pale Room pays her for the same skill with the lights on. Hypervigilance, rebranded as hospitality.

**Mannerisms to play:** she takes your coat like it's an honor; she names returning guests *by name on the second visit* (warm and quietly hair-raising); she never writes anything down in front of you.

## What She Wants

To clear the Babineaux family debt — legitimately, on wages, one week at a time — and keep her two little brothers off the night water for good. And underneath that: to never be cargo again. The Pale Room is the one place in Sallowreach where nobody can put her in a cage, because everyone alive is afraid of her employer. She chose the safest room in the city, and she knows exactly what that choice says about the city.

## What She's Hiding

Two things. **First:** she is Marcel's front-line intelligence — every name, every want, every tell on that floor is filed with her, and she reports the floor to the ledger every night. When Marcel opens with your name, that was Camille. **Second:** her family doesn't know any of it. Thornside believes she found kitchen work. She has never told them about the cage, the smuggler, or the cold men who opened the door — and she keeps the cage's broken padlock in her apron pocket, the way other people keep a saint's medal.

## The Cage *(GM — the vacancy's living witness; see [[Marcel Dray#The Vacancy|Marcel's file]])*

She was Verrin Stagg's freelance side-cargo, taken against her family's debt for an upriver "labor broker." Marcel's people opened the cage, and one of them gave her his coat because she was shaking; nobody said anything the whole way home. Weeks later she walked back to the Pale Room and asked for work. Marcel looked at her for a long moment and said only: *"You count things. Count for me."* He has never once mentioned the cage. Neither has she. It is the entire foundation of her loyalty, and neither of them has ever needed to say so.

**If the party asks her about the smuggler:** she will not say Marcel's name — ever — but she is a true channel: *"The cold men opened the cage. One of them gave me his coat. Nobody said anything the whole way home."* Warmth and dread in one breath; that's the tone the whole Pale Room runs on.

**The boat** *(banked gut-punch — hold it until it can land)*: the party sails the boat she was caged in. If she ever sees it at the berths, or hears it described down to the bow — her floor-calm cracks, one visible second, the padlock hand going to the apron pocket. What she does next is yours: asks them, quietly, if they've repainted the bow yet — or simply arranges, without being asked, that their drinks are never charged again.

> **S4 — the fuse is lit from the other side:** Leavy spoke with the surviving cub (**Whimsy**), who remembers *"a girl, tied up"* in the same hull, and a bad man who smelled of smoke. The party now carries half of Camille's story with no name and no face. When they finally walk into the Pale Room, play the recognition from *their* side — the girl taking their coats is the girl from the cub's memory, and the drink-that-nobody-ordered scene is suddenly about the cage. *(Staged in [[set-piece-monsieur-dray-has-time]] Movement 1 — the beat that movement exists for.)*

## The Door *(how anyone meets Marcel — the protocol)*

Marcel meets no one by default. You play, you spend, you leave; the room reads you and that is the whole transaction. A meeting happens exactly three ways, and Camille is the mechanism for all of them:

1. **Ask her directly and plainly.** "We need to speak with Monsieur Dray about—" and the *about* matters; vague asks get a lovely evening and no door. Asking costs nothing but the asking (the house rule of Marcel's whole table).
2. **Be interesting.** The room notices nerve, candor, a fair game played well, a marker refused politely.
3. **Be weird.** The room *really* notices that.

When the answer is yes, nothing is announced — a drink arrives that nobody ordered, and Camille says, *"Monsieur Dray has time."* Present tense. He already knows why they've come.

## Key Relationships

- [[Marcel Dray]] — employer, and the man whose one hard line saved her life; the debt neither of them names
- Her family (Thornside, near [[fen|Fen's]] row) — believes she works a kitchen; her wages quietly service the debt that put her in the cage
- [[the-mooring|The Mooring]]'s night berths — she knows their rhythms from the wrong side of a hull, and files everything she hears about them

## Dialogue Hook

Taking a coat, first visit: *"Welcome to the Pale Room. House rules are short: the games are fair, the drinks are better, and everything you say here is worth something — so say what you mean to spend."*

And the line that tells the party what she is, second visit, before anyone's given a name: *"Your usual table, Master Tenson?"*

## Stat Block

None needed. Camille does not fight, and the room does not let fights near her. If the campaign ever needs numbers, she's a Tier 1 social presence with an Experience worth stealing: *Counts Everything +3.*
