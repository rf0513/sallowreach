# Session 6 — Recap (As Played): *Three Days Out*

*The as-played record of Session 6 (2026-08-25). **Canon.** The plan was [[session-06-run-sheet]] (*"Three Days"*); this is what landed. The Celebration is two nightfalls away. The spar, the report, and Basile's crossing carry to S7.*

## Previously, on Sallowreach

Last month!

Caspar floated away on a balloon and the whole city mourned a man who isn't dead. You told Remy he drowned. You told everyone he drowned. You held a funeral, and the only one of you who couldn't stomach it was Tenson — the auditor who swore he'd never lie.

You found the wizard Augury Vane's journal and it hit you like a wall: the same thing happened to him. Festival night. The black water. And he could never leave either.

You know the Celebration did this to you. You just don't know how.

Today! Adaline came for the museum keys. A heron knocked at Isotta's door. And Fletcher put an arrow across Gault's bow.

## The Beats (canon)

- **The museum audit — the mimic frame job** ([[set-piece-the-empty-shelves]], as played). Adaline came to take possession of the city-funded museum and found the collection thinning — years of gaps, cases emptying. Her conclusion: Caspar was robbing the city. The party, knowing the truth (Marcel's extortion) but unable to say it without naming a man they haven't met, got creative. They **framed the Mire-Wrought Mimic** — the dormant creature banked in Caspar's storage room since S5 — for the missing items. Killed it, it exploded (Disgorge), and Adaline rationalized the whole thing: the creature had been eating the collection for years. Case closed without anyone lying to Adaline's face, without naming Marcel, and without confessing Caspar's fake death. ⚠ **The real gaps remain** — the Tidebreaker (case seven, at Marcel's), the founder's bow (returned, see below), and years of Marcel's actual fencing are still unaccounted for. Adaline's satisfied *for now*.
- **The mystery key.** During the museum scene, the party found a **mysterious key with an upside-down P insignia**. It doesn't open anything in the museum. **Unresolved — carry to a future session.** *(Recommended: ties to the Pale Room / Marcel's mark. Decision deferred.)*
- **Fletcher returns the founder's bow.** Tenson pressured Fletcher into giving back the stolen founder's bow (taken in S5, planned to be painted over). Fletcher returned it. **New thread:** Fletcher will attempt to **craft his own bow** from good wood sourced in town. *(The stolen-relic consequence thread from Front 11 is closed before it fired — no townsperson will clock it now.)*
- **Leavy's familiar — Chip Chip.** Leavy received their familiar and named them **Chip Chip**. *(The Witch's companion is now in play.)*
- **The Tallykeeper's knock** ([[set-piece-convince-me-to-wait]], as played — the scheduled door). In the middle of the night, **time froze.** The inn went silent — no breathing, no settling wood, nothing. Then: knocking. Three slow knocks at the door. Isotta opened it. The Tallykeeper was there — its true form, tall and wrong-angled, the heron's silhouette stretched into something that almost passes for human but doesn't try very hard. ⚠ **CORRECTION (2026-09-01): the cord was never tied — the scene broke off before it.** The knock and the time-freeze fired; **no terms, no prop, nothing on her wrist.** And the **grace cord is now retired entirely** (GM decision — it duplicated the Slipping Clock). The terms land in S7 at Basile's crossing: [[set-piece-she-lets-me-win]] **Beat 4**. ⚠ **The Tallykeeper scene is open, not closed** — the terms negotiation carries.
- **Isotta's custom socks.** Isotta picked up custom-made socks to offer **Chauncy** as tribute. *(The small, human gesture — currency for the NPC relationships.)*
- **The Still Cup — Vael and Old Basile.** Isotta went to the Still Cup to ask Vael about other ghosts. Vael was already planning this conversation — **the Count is kicked off** ([[isotta-arc]] §The Standing Count). Vael introduced Isotta to **Old Basile**, the beloved café regular who is, in fact, a slipping spirit. They played dominoes. They connected. Basile is fading — losing the woman's name, the anchor that kept him here. **Isotta chose to help him cross.** They are walking to **the red house** (the woman's old home) to find her name. ⚠ **The crossing has not happened yet.** Mid-scene when the session ended. Carry to S7 open.
- **The boat — Gault, face to face** ([[set-piece-name-them-if-you-want]], partially played). **Fletcher and Leavy only** went out on the water (not the full rookery trip — Brin's count is still owed). They encountered **Gault** working the channels. This is the party's **first direct confrontation** with the campaign's villain — five sessions of distant cameos finally broken. Fletcher **shot an arrow at him.** Gault abandoned his traps and left. ⚠ **Gault was not stopped, not caught, and not hurt** — he chose to leave. His operation continues, minus whatever traps he left behind. The arrow established Fletcher's intent: this is personal now.
