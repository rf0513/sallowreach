# Handout — Caspar's Farewell Letter *(the Legacy Bundle's heart)*

> ⚠ **Not to be confused with [[caspar-letters-handout]]** (plural — *"The Unsent Letters"*): the forty years of love letters to [[delphine|Delphine]] the party found in S5. **This** file is the single farewell letter to the *party*, and it fires on his departure. Two different props, two different readers.

*The letter the encounter file orders written in advance ("don't improvise it at the table" — [[session-03-caspar-encounter]]). It fires **the night Caspar leaves**, if the Object-on-Loan deal lands, atop the Legacy Bundle ([[the-tidebreaker]] § the Caspar Fork): the old-words fair copy ([[old-words-handout]]), the Tidebreaker provenance file, the VANE folder, the purse. Print the boxed text; bracketed lines are strike-or-keep depending on how the table played it. **The two-stage plant in the middle is canon-locked verbatim** — it must survive every edit, because it detonates the day the tether won't cut.*

---

## The Prop *(print this — close, hurried curator's hand; three words inked out and rewritten; he wrote it the night before the boat)*

> To the four of you —
>
> (I began this letter three times with your names and honorifics and it read like a placard. You are not exhibits. To the four of you.)
>
> You will find with this letter the working core of forty years, catalogued properly because I do not know how to do anything else:
>
> *Item the first* — the fair copy of the old words. The only transcription in existence. Nobody alive can read it, including me, which I am told is not how transcription is supposed to work.
> *Item the second* — the provenance file on a certain object currently in the keeping of a man we both know. The pages are numbered. Return nothing.
> *Item the third* — the folder marked VANE. Six years of my failures, organized. May they save you a month.
> *Item the last* — the purse. Do not be sentimental about the purse. Consider it the exhibition fee, paid in advance, for the finest collection of impossibilities I ever handled: the four of you. Dead men keep no accounts.
>
> [**if Tenson wrote the false report:** *A private word for the auditor: I have read my own death certificate, which is a privilege very few curators are granted, and I want you to know it is a beautifully kept document. You wrote a dead man a very fine death. It is the only review of my life I have ever entirely approved.*]
>
> [**if the party is minding the museum / Delphine holds the archive:** *Be kind to Delphine. She has been being kind to me for forty years and I never once made it easy, and she came anyway. Some collections you do not deserve.*]
>
> On the museum: someone should keep this city's memory. I always said that. I said it at dinners, at auctions, over other people's grief, for forty years — and it took a barge ticket to understand that I was describing a job I never actually did. I kept the city's *things.* Its memory I sold, mislabeled, or filed where nobody would look, myself included. You are holding the parts I got right. It is a small stack. Keep it better than I did.
>
> On the spear — and read this part twice, because I know how you listen:
>
> **The papers tell you what the spear does. Read again what it *is.* A gift, carved by one great thing for another, never delivered. Forty years I owned that sentence and never understood why it was the one that mattered. If the breaking ever fails you — try the making.**
>
> I am aware that is cryptic. I have earned one cryptic sentence. It is the only thing in this bundle I cannot provenance, because I do not know yet if it is true. You will.
>
> — C. Renne
> *Curator (retired, thoroughly)*
>
> *P.S. — The folio, the old words: you are going to meet something older than they are, if you're half as stubborn as I've priced you. If it can speak to you — ask it to read my fairytales back to me.*

## GM Annotations

- **When it fires:** the night he leaves — slipped under Remy's door, left atop the bundle, or handed over at the water with the pathos line the encounter file already scripts (*"The papers stay. All of them."*). If the party never opens the bundle, Delphine delivers the letter within the week; she promised him.
- **The two-stage plant:** the bolded paragraph is the whole reason this letter exists. Stage one, tonight: cryptic, filed away, probably joked about. Stage two — **the day the spear cuts their personal curses and the tether doesn't move** ([[the-tidebreaker]] cut-that-shows) — someone at the table remembers the letter. Let them dig it out and re-read it themselves; the room going quiet is the payoff. **If nobody remembers:** Vael or Delphine, the said-channel — *"didn't the dead man leave you papers?"* — one nudge, no more.
- **Do not let the letter be smarter than Caspar.** He doesn't know about the binding, the Turn, or what's at the Heart. The gift-reframe is a *collector's instinct about an object*, not knowledge — that's why the line hedges ("I do not know yet if it is true"). Same calibration law as the journal and the old words: baffled, never right.
- **If Caspar STAYS** (the party talked him out of leaving): hold this handout unused — he delivers Step 4 in person, alive, and the "try the making" line becomes dialogue at the archive desk instead. The letter keeps in the drawer for whenever his story does end; it reads even harder posthumously.
- **The purse:** canon per [[coin-in-sallowreach]] (the Payday Map — "a dead man doesn't need his savings"); weigh it as *handfuls → a serious sum if he goes.* This is the S4/S5 coin beat if it fires; don't stack a second one.
