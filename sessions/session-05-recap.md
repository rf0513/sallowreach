# Session 5 — Recap (As Played): *Dead on Paper*

*The as-played record of Session 5 (2026-07-21). **Canon.** The plan was [[session-05-run-sheet]] (*"Dead Men & Deep Water"*); this is what landed. The planned boat day never happened — the whole night stayed on Caspar. Springboard into Session 6.*

---

## Previously, on Sallowreach 

Last month!

You sat in the candlelight of Caspar's museum and he explained the reason for his paranoia. The name he could barely say out loud: Marcel Dray. A crime boss who runs the underground, who's been bleeding Caspar dry.

The Empire *executed* Marcel twelve years ago, but somehow he's in Sallowreach, running his casino. Caspar is convinced he's something worse than a man now. 

So you helped the frightened Caspar die.

You took him out in the boat to the outreaches of the Mire. The deeper you went, the sicker and weaker you got, until you couldn't go a further. And there, at the edge of everything, Caspar took out a little magic balloon and floated off into the horizon to start a life somewhere else.

You told Remy he drowned. He believed you. He was heartbroken and disappointed you didn't wake him to captain the boat.

The next day the whole city mourned Caspar. An empty boat on the drillfield, story after story. Sinope stood up in front of the whole town and said Caspar loved puzzles.... and that he'd keep solving them for him.

You went back to the museum and found the wizard's journal. The wizard Augury who went under the black water on a festival night... and could never leave this city again. Just like you.

Today! Leavy is promised a familiar. Sable is promised a spar. Brin is promised a spar. A heron continues stalking... 

## The Beats (canon)

- **The truth, in full** ([[session-03-caspar-encounter]] §2–§3, as played). Caspar told them everything and named **Marcel Dray** — paranoid, terrified, sweating the name out. Established as *party knowledge*: Marcel **runs the underground**, has been **extorting Caspar**, holds **the curse-breaking relic**, is **at his casino**, and — per Caspar's belief — is **undead** (the Empire executed him twelve years ago). This is **Stage 1 of Marcel's reveal, done** ([[marcel-dray]] §What Isotta Sees). **Marcel was not met.** The party is now actively interested in meeting him — he has what they need.
- **Tenson's compromise: bend the truth, never lie.** Tenson refused to author a false report. The party negotiated a workaround with Caspar: the report will **not** state they saw him die — it will say **Caspar fell out of the boat and they don't know where he is.** True sentences, arranged to mislead. ⚠ **Tenson has not written the note yet** — "at some point." *Open thread; it still has to be filed, and it still feeds Caius's annexation file when it is.*
- **The fake death, as played — the balloon.** Not the run sheet's drop-off-and-report. The party took Caspar out in **their own boat**, at night, into the deep Mire. Approaching the tether edge they felt **cursed, sick, weak** — worsening the closer they got, until they could go no farther. There Caspar produced **a small magic balloon** and **floated away into the distance** to begin a new life. **Caspar is alive, gone, whereabouts unknown to everyone but the party.**
- **The night navigation (GM craft note).** The GM ran deep-Mire nav rolls — boat took damage, party took Stress — but felt it played **softer than the danger the table had been promised.** Flagged for future prep: the Mire needs teeth that *bite*, not just narration. (See STATUS craft note / the offered Mire-danger kit.)
- **Sinope caught them at the boat.** Before they collected Caspar, the party found **[[sinope|Sinope]]** crashing where their boat was stashed. He asked what they were up to; **they were dodgy**; he got *excited* — declared he loves puzzles and will solve this one. Next day at the funeral he gave a short speech: **Caspar loved puzzles, and he'll keep solving them in his honor.** *(The party knows they're being looked at, in the open, by a friend they can't threaten, buy, or lie to usefully. He does **not** suspect a faked death — he suspects* something*, which means he checks everything. Live thread; see [[sinope]] §Where He Is Now.)*
- **Remy waited up.** They came home late; Remy was up because their absence was strange. They told him **Caspar drowned.** Remy was **devastated and angry** — furious they hadn't woken him to operate the boat, convinced Caspar died for **lack of a proper captain.** He made them **promise to accept his help in future** Mire trips. *(He grieves a death that didn't happen and carries guilt for it. The party is lying to Remy too.)*
- **The funeral — the Setting-Out.** Next day, the whole town gathered at the **Drillfield.** An empty boat in the center; people came up one by one to share a story and lay an item; at the end the boat was pushed off into the water. **The whole party took part except Tenson** — sharing manipulated, half-true stories and leaving items. **Delphine was visibly devastated.**
- **Tenson's crisis.** Tenson was **deeply upset by the funeral** — the party had barely begun to sit with the fact of faking a death, and had not reckoned with **an entire city mourning a living man.** Refused to participate. *(Live arc thread — the auditor who wouldn't lie is now complicit in a civic-scale one.)*
- **The love story.** In the archive they found **Caspar's unsent love letters to [[Delphine]]** — a whole bittersweet lost-love story, folded and kept, never delivered. Paired with Delphine's raw grief at the funeral, the party now holds the shape of what these two never had. *(She does not know he's alive.)*
- **The journal found & read** ([[the-first-maintainers-journal]]). The party found **Augury Vane's field journal** and the GM read it aloud. *(The prop was rewritten to four entries in the GM's own prose, 2026-07-24 — that rewrite is now the canonical text.)* The intended recognition landed **hard**: the party is now **100% certain the Celebration and their curse are connected**, because it happened to Vane too (festival-night drowning, the boundary, never left). *(Note: this overshoots the journal's designed "one suspicion, then stop" — but it's the players' own deduction from recognition, which is exactly right. The **mechanism** — the binding, the Tree Spirit, the Celebration-as-cage — remains fully locked; they have the *connection*, not the *how*.)*

## Loot & Improvised Canon

- **Fletcher's founder's bow — STOLEN, and recognizable.** Fletcher took a bow from the museum that **belonged to one of the town's founders.** He rolled and **understands someone in town would recognize it as stolen** — and declared he'll **paint over it** to disguise it. ⚠ **Live consequence thread:** a disguised founder's relic on the town's hunter. Anyone who knew the piece (Caspar's circle, an old family, an archivist) could clock it. *(New canon: the founder's-bow item is now in play, off-book, carried by Fletcher.)*
- **Leavy's three "relics"** — she pocketed the three joke pieces: **the Weeping Candle**, **the brass spirit-detector frog** (the one that *gronks* when squeezed), and **"the Founder's Tooth"** (a pig's molar). All mundane, all charming, all Caspar's cheerful frauds. *(Comedy props now in Leavy's kit; the frog is a gift that keeps giving at the worst moments.)*
- **Isotta's wine — the poignant one.** She kept the **"very good wine"** — which is the bottle Caspar had tagged **"for when the journal comes home."** She took the bottle he was saving for exactly this day, without knowing it. *(A beautiful accidental irony to pay off whenever they finally open it.)*
- **The mimic did not fire.** The Mire-Wrought Mimic (banked in Caspar's storage room) was **not triggered** — they found the journal without opening the chest. **Still available** as a future surprise or quietly retired. *(Its Disgorge journal-save was moot; the journal surfaced on its own.)*
