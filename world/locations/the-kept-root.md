# The Kept Root

**Type:** Outdoor boardwalk plaza. No building. A giant old root grows up through a hole in the deck.
**Region:** [[Sallowreach]], near the city center — a few bridges from the [[the-hall-of-the-mire|Hall of the Mire]]
**Controlled By:** Nobody. An old man named **Prosper Doucet** sweeps it.

*This is the city's place of devotion — fills the "shrine" gap. Also hides clue **12b** ([[the-celebration-clue-map|clue map]]).*

---

## What You See

- A small open plaza, about 30 ft, where two boardwalks meet. Open air, black water below.
- In the middle: a **hole in the decking**. A huge black root-knee rises through it — ox-sized, polished shiny at hand height by 200 years of people touching it.
- Around the hole: a **ring of scrubbed pale planks** with offerings on it — a heel of bread, a cup of rum, a coin, a button, one crawfish a kid arranged very carefully.
- Old houses lean in on three sides. Probably an old man sweeping (Prosper).

The founders built the city *around* this root instead of cutting it. Locals call it "her" and can't tell you why.

**Smaller offering-roots exist all over the city** — anywhere old wood breaks the surface near a boardwalk, the boards are cut back and a clean ring is kept. Families also keep a shelf by the door with a full guest-cup: the "family altar."

---

## The Etiquette (how devotion works here — any local can teach it)

1. **Never ask for anything.** No prayers, no requests. Guests don't get handed chores. A newcomer who kneels and *asks* gets gently corrected — it's the one real faux pas.
2. **Never name, never count.** The guests, the offerings, any of it.
3. **Leave firsts.** First coin of a new venture, first cup of a new batch, first slice of a wedding loaf.
4. **Don't watch it go.** Set it down, touch the root or don't, walk away. Watching is rude.
5. **Anyone hungry may take.** Never said out loud, known by everyone. Any stranger might be a spirit wearing a face ([[cosmology]]) — so feeding the hungry counts as feeding the guests.

---

## Prosper Doucet

Retired piling-setter, 70s. Square build, rope-scarred hands, a folding rule in his belt. Sweeps the plaza dawn and dusk. Not a priest — there are no priests. Just the guy who keeps the boards good.

**Voice tic:** talks about everything like an engineering problem — loads, spans, give.

- *"You don't ask her for things. She's a guest, not a winch."*
- *"Nothing gets* took *here. Things get received. Mind your words on this deck."*
- *"Quarter-inch a year, toward the middle of town. Forty years of my own marks say so. You don't lay a stringer in front of something that's walking. You give way."*

**Wants:** The Accord condemned the plaza decking. The repair plan runs a beam straight through the root's gap — she's marked *"obstruction, to clear."* He wants the route bent around her. Nobody with a seal will listen to a retired tradesman. **A PC can fix this in an afternoon** — [[tenson|Tenson's]] seal, or [[Théo Rouchard|Théo's]] guild weight.

**Hiding:** 40 years ago his crew was ordered to cut her. One saw-cut, a hand deep. By morning it had **closed** — seamless, like the saw went through water. No one was hurt. Nothing happened at all — she didn't even *mind*, and that scale is what converted him. The foreman quit the trade. Prosper started sweeping. The saw's still in the silt. He's never told anyone.

## The Clue (12b — GM)

**The root is slowly moving toward the heart of the city.** Quarter-inch a year, 200 years running. Same direction as the inward verse (#12) and the Tallykeeper's no-fly zone (#13).

| Shown | Said | Never say |
|---|---|---|
| Look down: three generations of plank-ring in the deck, each older ring offset a hand-width further out. The migration is visible. | Prosper's "quarter-inch a year" speech — to him it's a carpentry fact | What she's moving *toward* |

**What she is:** a piece of the true Mire that was already inside when the salt ward went up. She feels the same pull Vane measured from outside ([[the-first-maintainers-journal]]). Don't define it further — what matters is she *points*.

**Offerings that vanish:** mostly gulls, kids, and the hungry. But if a PC stakes it out (rude — the plaza will let them feel it), some vanish with no taker. Never explain this.

## Scenes If You Need One (d8)

1. Mother teaching a toddler: set it down, touch the wood, eyes front.
2. A hungry docker takes a meal off the ring, unhurried, with a nod. Staring at him gets *you* stared at.
3. Prosper vs. the repair foreman, drawings out, politest argument in the city.
4. [[Delphine]] leaves something small. Lately it faces southwest. She hasn't noticed.
5. An offering untouched for three days. The quarter is quietly rattled — what does it mean when a guest *declines*?
6. A guild outsider measuring the root for a report. The plaza has frozen him out; he hasn't noticed.
7. Wedding party, half-drunk, totally sincere, leaves the first slice. Old women cry.
8. Celebration morning: the ring dressed like a feast table. Guests eat before the city does.

## Hooks

- **The Stringer:** reroute the repair beam = one afternoon of PC effort, big goodwill with the old quarter, and Prosper hands over his 40 years of marks — **that's how clue 12b enters play as a reward.**
- **Someone will price her eventually.** Old-growth Mire wood, unregulated, in a public plaza. [[Caius Maren|Caius]] would pave her politely. Gault wouldn't bother being polite. If a front needs a desecration beat, it's standing right here.
- **Free cosmology lesson:** everything anyone says here about guests and welcome is also true of the Tree Spirit. Nobody knows that, including the person saying it.

## Secrets

- The saw-cut that closed overnight (Prosper's silence; the saw's still down there).
- The lean — clue 12b.
- The vanishing remainder — never explained, ever.
- If she's ever cut through (endgame only): 200 years of offerings grown into her rings like pearls, founder-era stuff at the core. A toy, not a plot.
- **GM ONLY (canon 2026-07-18):** she is not a root *near* the guest. She is the guest's **farthest fingertip** — the drowned tree under [[the-hall-of-the-mire|the Hall]] surfacing, once, in a public square ([[set-piece-the-honored-guest]]). The offerings arrive. Every one. Divers find the recent ones resting on the roots below, the old ones grown into the bark. The city has been lovingly feeding its prisoner for two hundred years, and neither of them would call it that.

## Dungeon Alchemist Notes

Small boardwalk plaza over black water, ~30 ft: central hole in decking with a big black root-knee through it, pale plank ring with small offerings, three visibly different generations of decking, a stack of pale imported lumber with drawings on it, lantern posts, stilt-houses on three sides. Dusk, warm lanterns vs. black water.
