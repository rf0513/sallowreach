# Session 4 — The Ranging House *(Fletcher's thread — light on purpose)*

## Movement 1 — The Collision *(walk-in; no rolls)*

The party arrives at [[The Ranging House]] to collect the snake intel. The map room already has a visitor. Read:

> You hear it before you're through the door. Not shouting — the opposite. Two voices, low and flat. It's the kind of quiet that makes you check where the exits are.
>
> Brin's at her map table. And the man across from her — you know him. The port tavern, day one. The man with the sealed jars, and the glowing thing dying in the glass. Lean. Scarred hands. Dark clothes, nothing that catches. He hasn't raised his voice once this whole time. Somehow that's worse.

The tail of the argument, scripted *(Gault: plain, unhurried, states things once. Brin: precise, colder with every line — she has never needed volume)*:

> **Gault:** "Four skins in your drawer. Southwest junctions. Name a price."
> **Brin:** "There's no number."
> **Gault:** "There's always a number. Yours is just sentimental."
> **Brin:** "Get out of my guild hall."

**The exit.** He goes — unhurried, unbothered, the specific quality of departure that says *I was never worried.* On his way past the party he stops, half a beat, at Fletcher. Read:

> He stops next to you, Fletcher. Doesn't say anything. Just looks at the journal under your arm. And it's not a curious look. There's nothing personal in it at all. It's how a man looks at freight.
>
> Then you catch his eyes in the lantern light. And the light... stays in them. Half a second too long.
>
> He says, to Brin, not to you: *"You'll sell eventually. Everyone in this city is one bad season from a number."* And at the door, without turning around: *"And if your bounty boys stumble on it first — nothing that punctures the skin."*
>
> Then he's gone.

**Brin's non-explanation** *(the only one she will ever volunteer — say it and move on)*:

> "That man costs more than he pays. That's all you'll get from me about it."

*(If someone pushes: she goes quiet and starts pulling maps. The wound — [[brin-sevarre|her file]] — stays hers. Remy can add the S1 callback later if asked: "That's Gault. We don't do business with Gault.")*

## Movement 2 — The Map Scene *(the promise paid; 0–2 rolls)*

Door barely shut behind him, Brin opens the drawer. Read:

> She lays them out without a word. Four shed skins, pressed flat, each one tagged and dated. Then she looks at your journal, Fletcher. And waits. She's not grabbing. She's asking.
>
> And when your castings go down next to hers — they match. Same two-headed pattern. Same scale run. She starts pinning them across her channel map, yours and hers together, oldest to newest. And Fletcher — the line they make. You've been walking that line for three years, one shed skin at a time. You've never seen it from above. Until right now.

**Let Fletcher make the read** — this is his competence scene. Optional roll: **Knowledge or Instinct + *Swamp Specialist*, Difficulty 12** `[VERIFY]` — on any hit, *he* gets to say what's wrong with the line before Brin does (hand the insight to the player; let him deliver it to the table). Brin confirms it in plain words either way *(clue rule — said aloud, unmissable)*:

> "Animals wander. This thing traveled. That's a freight road."

**The second clue, shown then said.** She nods at the yard, where the party's boat is tied *(or at the broken cage if they brought pieces)*:

> "One more thing you should sit with. The cage your owlbear cub broke out of, and the crates on that boat of yours — same maker's stamps. I've seen those stamps before, on crates cached out in the deep channels. Whoever built that snake's road built your boat's road too."

*(Optional second roll, only if a player goes looking: **Instinct, Difficulty 12** `[VERIFY]` to recall that Gault's sealed containers — just now, under his arm — carried the same stamps. Don't force it; it's a teaser, not a payload.)*

**Her warning, flat and serious** *(canon, from her file — deliver as written)*:

> "Two hunters went past the Double Knot after it and didn't come back. I won't go alone, and I won't send you alone. That's not caution. That's arithmetic."

## The Commission — Side-Quest 1 lands

She rolls the map up and makes the offer — to the *party*, not the frog:

> "So here's what I've got, and what I want. There's a shedding hollow in the middle channels — it sheds there regular, and I've never had a boat that could reach it. You do now. Bring me one fresh casting, whole. And on the way there's a rookery I need counted — it's been feeding there, and I need to know how bad.
>
> Guild coin, fair rate, for all four of you. Four sets of eyes. It's killed people who went out there in ones and twos. Bring me the skin and the count, and I'll pay you like the Guild pays people it intends to hire again."

*(That's [[fletcher-arc#Side-Quest 1 — "The Wrong Skin"|"The Wrong Skin"]] — the anthology boat trip with a reason aboard for every PC: Leavy's reagent water, the lost hunters' camp for Isotta, the survey-audit for Tenson. Pitch those hooks when the party plans the trip, not tonight.)*

## Soft Button — The Cub *(1–2 minutes, warm; run whenever it fits)*

Before Fletcher leaves, Brin jerks her chin at the back room. The cub — his arrow, his bandage — is healing. Read:

> It's bigger already. Bandage half shredded because it will not stop chewing it. It sees you and does a thing that's half growl, half... hope? Brin says it eats like a landslide and hates everyone. It doesn't seem to hate you, which she finds professionally interesting.
>
> She hesitates. Then: "One thing I can't fix. Same hour every night, it goes dead still, looks out at the dark — southwest — and cries. Just a few minutes. Then it stops." She looks at you. "Something out there is crying back. You know that, right?"

*(Front 9 tell — [[world-in-motion]], the mother is coming. Plant it and move on. If Fletcher brought smoked eels: the cub takes them from his hand and only his. Brin logs it in the tally-book.)*
