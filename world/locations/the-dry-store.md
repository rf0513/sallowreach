# The Dry Store

**Type:** Shop — outfitter, general goods, dry storage
**Region:** [[Sallowreach]] — off the market row, two boardwalks back from the cook-stalls
**Controlled By:** Odette Arceneaux (independent; guild-friendly, guild-free)

**In plain words:** Sallowreach doesn't really have shops — the city feeds and houses everyone for free, so there's nothing to sell that matters. Except one thing: *dryness.* Everything in a swamp city rots, molds, and swells, and the one shop that can keep things truly dry is where you buy expedition gear that survives the deep swamp — and where families pay to store the things they cannot bear to lose. The owner is an old woman who remembers every sale she's ever made, which accidentally makes her stock book the best record in town of who went into the Mire, with what, and whether they came back. This file closes the campaign's last unbuilt shop function: buying, selling, and the shopkeeper.

---

## What You See

Indoors, narrow, deep — a shotgun shop three rooms long, and the air changes at the threshold: **dry.** Actually dry, the first dry air you've breathed since the wreck. In view:

- Walls of **sealed jars, waxed cloth, and coiled rope**, sorted by some system with no labels — just small burnt marks on the shelf-edges.
- A counter of black cypress worn pale where three generations of elbows have rested, and behind it **Odette**, on a high stool, mending an oilskin.
- The **iron stove** that runs day and night, every day of the year — the town joke is that the Dry Store is the only place in Sallowreach where winter is illegal.
- At the back, a heavy door with a wax-sealed frame: **the Dry Room.**

*Say to the table: "It smells like wax, cedar, and warm rope. Somewhere behind the counter a kettle's on. An old woman looks up, takes all four of you in at one glance — gear, boots, posture — and says: 'Going out, or coming back?'"*

## Odette Arceneaux

**Role:** Outfitter; keeper of the Dry Room · **Tier:** 1 · **Faction:** Independent

- **Appearance:** small, dense, seventy-something; forearms like a poler; a magnifier on a cord she never uses in front of customers. Hands always working — wax, thread, rope.
- **Manner:** unhurried, exact, kind without softness. She *reads* customers the way [[brin-sevarre|Brin]] reads hunters — for whether they'll come back.
- **What she wants:** **pilings for the Dry Room's expansion.** The waiting list for lockers is two years long, families trust her with their dearest things, and the Woodworkers say the market row can't bear new pilings ([[world-in-motion|Front 3]] arithmetic — same stringer problem [[the-kept-root|Prosper]] fights). Someone with standing, a seal, or guild credit could move it. She'll say it plainly if asked what she needs. Nobody asks shopkeepers that.
- **What she's hiding:** **the quiet account.** For three years, a standing order — jar-wax by the crate, cage wire, sealed lamp-oil in bulk — collected by a different hired hand each season, paid in clean coin, no name. She files it under a mark that means *money with no face*, and she doesn't like it, and she keeps taking it, and she knows both of those things about herself. *(It's [[gault|Gault]]'s supply line. She has no idea — but her marks can date every order.)*
- **Also true:** she can't read. The famous stock book is burnt marks and memory — her whole ledger only exists *in her*. You can't steal it, buy it, or subpoena it. You can only be someone she decides to tell.
- **Dialogue hook:** *"Going out, or coming back? Don't shrug at me. The kit's different."*
- **Tell:** she prices by looking at your boots, not your purse — and when she's decided you won't come back, she starts recommending *less*.

## Buying — what coin is for here *(doctrine: [[coin-in-sallowreach]] — the floor is free; coin only buys moves)*

The Homecoming draws cover living. Nobody buys bread, bedding, or a working coat — the city provides. What Odette sells is **going somewhere the city's floor doesn't reach**, weighed not priced:

| Kit | Costs about | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Town kit** | a handful, or free with a favor | patched oilskin, boots re-waxed, a sound lantern. She barely charges; this is neighbor-work. |
| **Mire kit** (day trips, middle water) | a purse | full oilskins, sealed lanterns, salt, waxed food-wrap, rot-proof rope, a repair roll. What [[remy|Remy]] quietly checks the party has before he'll pole them anywhere. |
| **The deep kit** | a Bag — a real decision | everything above but *expedition grade*: double-sealed everything, waxed-page journals, a month of dry. She has outfitted every deep-Mire attempt for thirty years. This is a coin *beat*, not a transaction — see the Refusal. |

**Selling:** she buys honest salvage — rope, tools, hardware, unbroken jars — at fair weight, no questions *about goods*. (Relics and anything with a story go to [[Caspar Renne|Caspar]]'s side business, not here; she'll say so: *"That's not stock, that's trouble. Museum's four bridges east."*)

**⚠ The Refusal** *(the shop's one scene — don't waste it on a shopping trip)*: she will not sell a full deep kit to people she's decided aren't coming back. Not at any weight. The party outfitting for [[set-piece-the-delivery|the Delivery]] or [[set-piece-double-knot|the Double Knot]] must *pass* her — not a roll, a conversation: who's your guide, who's your anchor, who knows your route. Convince her and she adds things they didn't buy, off her own shelf, no charge. *(She and Brin have never once compared methods. They're the same method.)*

## The Dry Room *(the back door — the shop's heart)*

Locker shelves floor to ceiling: the one truly dry room in Sallowreach, rented by the year for what families cannot bear to lose. Marriage papers. A grandmother's letters. Drum skins. A christening gown that has outlived three floods. What a family chooses to keep dry is the truest thing you can know about them — and Odette knows every locker's contents and never says one word about any of it.

- **Three lockers are unclaimed** — renters dead, no kin come forward. She keeps paying their space out of her own till. Won't discuss it. *(If [[lantern-docket|Isotta]] ever asks what's in them, that's a knock waiting to happen — GM's discretion, one case someday; the docket queue is full for now.)*
- **Tenson** will notice the marks-ledger is *flawless bookkeeping in a script that isn't writing* — and she'll enjoy being audited by the first person in decades who appreciates the system. Access to what her memory holds goes through exactly this kind of respect.

## Tensions & Hooks

- **The quiet account** — the party hunting [[gault|Gault]]'s supply line ends up at her counter; her marks can date three years of orders, *including when they stop.* **Optional extra authorship clue for [[set-piece-the-bad-season]]** *(GM's call — the built clue-set already runs easy-mode)*: the quiet account went silent two weeks before the raid. He didn't need to buy anymore. He was done stocking.
- **The pilings ask** — her expansion is a ready-made coin sink with a face (a person, per doctrine), and it braids with Prosper's stringer and Front 3's rehousing arithmetic: one guild work-order could serve all three. Paperwork with three beneficiaries is a *Tenson* quest shape.
- **Fletcher's journal** — she stocks waxed-page journals, and she has watched him handle his. Next visit, there's one set aside on the counter, his size, no comment. *(If the arc's S7 beat ever spends the journal, this is where the replacement was already waiting.)*
- **Leavy and the jars** — Consortium seal-jars are her wax, her supply. She can authenticate, re-seal, or quietly note that somebody's been buying her jar-wax *by the crate* for years.

## Secrets

- The quiet account is Gault's (above) — she can date it; she cannot name it.
- The ledger is memory; the marks are hers alone. Rob the store and you get rope. The intelligence walks home on a stool-legged old woman.
- One unclaimed locker holds deep-kit gear tagged with marks she made **thirty-one years ago** — an expedition that never came back for it. She re-waxes the seals every spring anyway. *(It was the **Cuvier survey** — two guild surveyors charting the deep currents. Their punt, and their chart, wait in [[set-piece-the-long-anchorage|the Long Anchorage]], Room 5.)*

## Dungeon Alchemist Notes

Interior, narrow 3-room shotgun layout (~8×24): retail room (shelving walls, counter, stove), workroom (workbench, hanging oilskins, rope coils), Dry Room (locker shelving, single reinforced door). Warm palette, one light source per room, no windows in the back room.
