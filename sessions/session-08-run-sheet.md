## 🧭 In Plain Words *(the whole session — no jargon)*

One full day in town — the day before the big festival. After tonight: Long Rest → the Celebration.

It opens small: the half-ghost left a gift of socks for the little house spirit at the inn, and in the morning there's a gift back — a warm stone, and a biscuit she can actually taste. Then the whole party goes down to the docks, where the giant fish they killed last session has drawn a crowd. Their captain is getting drinks bought for him. A stranger in the crowd says something friendly about the dead museum curator that doesn't quite add up. And the imperial envoy strolls up, admires the fish, and mentions that official mail leaves on the lumber barges the day after tomorrow.

Then they split. The half-ghost goes to the town hall to ask what the festival is really for — and watches the town's dead start walking out of the city ahead of it. The auditor gets a suspiciously tidy revised expense report from the salt-wall wizard, then has tea with the envoy, who wants the auditor's signature on a report before the barges go — and the auditor wants the envoy's signature on a report of his own. The ranger plans the snake hunt with the hunters' guild steward, finishes his new bow, and finds his own arrow stuck in his owlbear cub's pen with a shipping tag tied to it: the poacher he shot at wants revenge. The witch brews antivenom for the snake hunt out of the snakeskins in the ranger's journal.

Everyone comes home. The city is getting ready. End on the eve.

## 🎲 Dice First

*The reflex: the moment a PC tries to* get *something — read a person, find a thing, push past a "no" — call the roll* **before** *you narrate the result.*

**Action roll** = 2d12 (Hope die + Fear die) + trait vs **Difficulty.** Hope die high → PC gains **Hope.** Fear die high → **you** gain **Fear.** Matching dice = **Crit** (auto-succeed, gain Hope, clear Stress).

- ⭐ **Spend a Hope → +2 from a relevant Experience.** Every roll below names which one. **Say it out loud when you call the roll.**
- **Help an Ally** → helper spends 1 Hope, rolls +d6 (highest d6 counts).
- **Advantage** → +1d6. The Hall gives it for respect (Slot 2a); Leavy's hands give it at the Turn (Slot 2d).
- **Gate the cost, not the fun.** They always learn *something*; the die sets the price.

**Four answers, not two:** success-with-Hope · success-with-Fear · failure-with-Hope · failure-with-Fear.

**Diff ladder:** 11 baseline · 13–14 hard · 15 a real reach.

### 🧪 This session's one new mechanic: the Project countdown *(say this to the table)*

> "Leavy, this is your antivenom. Four boxes. Every time you work on it, you roll, and you cross off boxes — two on a success with Hope, one with Fear, three on a crit. When all four are gone, it's done. At the snake, it stops the venom cold."

That's the whole rule. It's the same tick system as the SRD's dynamic countdowns ([[STATUS]] §SRD Facts). **Why it matters:** the snake fight ([[set-piece-double-knot]]) has a venom countdown built in — a bitten PC is dying in four rolls, and **the antivenom ends it instantly.** No antivenom, no brake. If you've never run a countdown out loud: [[set-piece-the-empty-shelves]] §Teaching the Countdown has the verbatim script.

**Every counter tonight is visible:** Antivenom (4) · Bow (2 left) · Isotta's Slipping Clock (6) · her tally sheet.
