# Set Piece — Meat That Talks *(The Tessak Camp)*

*Encounter 2 of the [[lantern-rays]] thread. The party sails into the camp of the clan that ambushed them in Session 1, to talk a deal-holder out of a deal that's feeding its children. Fight-optional the hard way: violence is always one wrong register away. Stats: [[sallowreach-roster#Tessak Diver|the roster]].*

> 🔁 **The reprise door** *(canon 2026-07-19)*: if [[set-piece-the-bad-season]] has played, the Weighing runs a second time — on the party's terms. The raid failing IS the proof of *tessavrak*; the elders have reasserted; the party brings the night's own count (losses taken vs. meat brought in — Gault's lure against the clan's own law). Same machine, same registers, Sresh present. Win it and the war the raid started ends at this water's edge.

## Cold-Open Box

**What happens:** Party lands at a Tessak camp. **Drevath** (a Tessak Diver, speaks for the clan) meets them at the water's edge. A safe-conduct roll gets them heard, then a 3/3 countdown ("the Weighing") where three arguments (season-count, spent détente, the Season-Eater precedent) tick Progress and register errors (pity, threats, performing) tick Consequence. Win it: Gault loses his workforce, they get a prow-tooth. Lose it: door shuts, they're meat forever after. No warmth is possible either way — best case is *wary respect between predators.*

| Roll | What | Diff |
|---|---|:--:|
| Safe-conduct (opening) | Presence (adv. if boat-marks disowned / wreck-survival shown) | 13 |
| Each Weighing lever (×3) | Presence/Knowledge (adv. with evidence in hand; auto-succeed with written tallies) | 14 |
| Clock Sresh (who she is) | Instinct (Fletcher free) | 12 |
| Composure vs. the racks (Fear spend) | Presence Reaction | 12 |

**Countdown:** linked Progress 3 / Consequence 3. Consequence ticks need no roll — pity, threats, performing, smiling, reaching for a weapon.
**Party jobs:** Fletcher = professional read + lever 3 + Sresh · Tenson = lever 2 (the paper argument) · Leavy = the oil-trade testimony (Orna's stake) · Isotta = one free beat — no restless dead here, the kills cross clean.
**Guardrails:** no pity lane, ever — this is arithmetic, not moral mush · Drevath never raises their voice or negotiates against themself · Remy stays with the boat · the far racks stay implied, never inventoried · "sixty adults" gets said once, never again.

## What the Party Can Win

- **Gault loses his workforce** — the extraction collapses to one stubborn man.
- **The Tessak prow-tooth** ([[items/loot-ledger#Tessak prow-tooth|ledger]]) — earned deep-channel passage.
- **A clan that remembers them as trade, not food.**

What they can't win: warmth. There's no befriending the Tessak.

## Before They Go — Remy's Briefing

He's met the Tessak twice — once tied up as their catch. If asked, he says:

> "Right. Three things, and I mean them. One — you can't outswim them, so don't plan anything that ends in swimming. Two — don't smile. Showing teeth means something different down there, and what it means isn't friendly. Three — and this is the one that'll save you — **don't waste their time.** They'll listen exactly as long as you're saying something real. The moment you start performing, you're not a negotiator anymore. You're an animal making noise."

Remy stays with the boat. His choice, no apology. The party goes to the water's edge alone.

## The Approach

> The channel narrows, and the first thing you notice is what's missing. No birds. No frog-chorus. Not scared-quiet — *settled*-quiet, like a house where the owner's home.
>
> Then the camp opens up out of the reeds: a low island, hide shelters staked down against the wind, smoke off a fire that doesn't flicker — somebody feeds it exactly right. Gault's waterproofed containers stand stacked at the waterline in neat rows, stenciled, waiting for a barge. And strung between the shelters there are drying racks. Long ones. You can see ray-hide stretched on the near ones.
>
> You don't look too hard at the far ones.
>
> And there are wakes in the water around your boat. Count them. Now count again — there are more than there were.

**The pipeline-marks wrinkle:** the party's boat is Gault's old boat. The stamps read as *his people* from a distance — buys unmolested approach, poisons the opening if unaddressed. Disowning the marks honestly ("we took this boat from your supplier") = advantage on safe-conduct. Getting caught trading on them = a free Consequence tick before the Weighing starts.

## The Water's Edge — Drevath

They meet the party before anyone lands.

> One of the wakes stands up. That's the only way to describe it — the water sheds off a lizardfolk the size of Tenson, and they were *right there*, an oar's length off your bow, the whole time you were counting the others. Grey-green, harpoon across the back, eyes that don't blink and don't leave you.
>
> They look at each of you in turn. Slowly. It's not a greeting. It's the way you look at a market stall.
>
> "Meat that talks. And it sailed here on its own." A pause you could land a boat in. "Say the real thing first."

**Drevath's tic:** long pauses that are never hesitation — the math running. No contractions. Drops their voice instead of raising it. Prices everything out loud: *"That is worth hearing." / "That cost you nothing to say, so it is worth nothing." / "Closer."*

**Safe-conduct roll (Presence 13):** success = the party is *trade* for the scene; wakes back off to Far. Failure = *undecided* — the Weighing runs anyway, wakes stay Close, first Consequence tick is free.

## The Weighing *(the countdown — the scene's engine)*

Same grammar as [[set-piece-the-mother-arrives|the owlbear's Calm-Down]], colder register. **Linked countdown, Progress 3 / Consequence 3.** Say the rule out loud when the first PC argues — let the table watch the loops tighten.

**Progress ticks (a roll per lever, Presence/Knowledge 14, adv. with evidence in hand):**

1. **The season-count** — Remy's tallies, the emptying shallows. Written counts auto-succeed on a good speech. *Sresh's lever.*
2. **The spent détente** — Tenson's lane: staged wrecks make survivors, survivors make imperial paper, paper ends two hundred years of nobody looking. *"You are eating the arrangement that keeps the city blind."*
3. **The Season-Eater precedent** — the thing past the Double Knot is what taking-beyond-the-season makes. *"He is doing to your channels what its makers did to theirs."* Lands hard — Drevath goes stiller than the whole scene.

**Consequence ticks (no roll):** appeals to mercy/friendship/the rays' beauty · smiling too wide, looming, reaching for a weapon · performing (flattery, bluster) · caught trading on the boat-marks · any threat (Drevath: *"Closer to food."*).

**Perform the ticks:**
- **Progress 1:** "Drevath's head tilts. The pause is longer. 'That is worth hearing.'"
- **Progress 2:** "An old Tessak comes down to the waterline — bone-grey, scarred, leaning on a harpoon gone green with age. Drevath does not look at her. Everything about them says they know she's there."
- **Progress 3 — verdict.** See Outcomes.
- **Consequence 3:** *"You have said all your real things. The rest is noise."* Wakes come back to Close. One clean exit — Drevath won't pursue a failed trade, that's bad math too. Door shut, deal stands.

### Sresh — the side-channel (don't skip her)

The bone-grey elder is **Sresh**, keeper of the tessavrak count — the reason this can work. Drevath won't reverse for outsiders, but can lose to the clan's own law. **Instinct 12** to clock her (Fletcher free). Address the count *to her* and the clan gets a face-saving door: Drevath doesn't fold to meat, Drevath defers to law. Skipped entirely: the final verdict costs one extra good argument.

**Her line, if spoken to:** "The count does not care who brings it. Bring it."

## Fear Menu *(the scene is a held breath — spend to squeeze)*

- **1 Fear — the wakes multiply:** "Count the water around your boat again. Two more than before. No splash when they arrive. There never was."
- **1 Fear — the young one:** "A Tessak the size of a ten-year-old surfaces and just watches. Head tilted. Not menace — *study*. It's learning that food negotiates now." *(The deal's real danger, made visible.)*
- **1 Fear — the racks:** wind turns, brings the far racks with it. **Presence Reaction 12**, everyone marks a Stress or keeps their face. Losing composure isn't a Consequence tick — reaching for a weapon is.
- **2 Fear — Gault arrives** (if present — he moves constantly): watches from the water, says nothing, doesn't intervene. Doubles the weight of every register error — the clan won't look uncertain in front of the supplier. If the party wins with him watching: the first time Gault has ever looked *surprised.*
- **2 Fear — Sound the Channel** (combat only — Scout's move): the trill through the water, gar boil up at the blood.

## If It Breaks — The Fight

*Takes a drawn weapon, a raid on the containers, or Consequence 3 plus insistence.*

- **Forces:** Drevath (Tessak Diver + *Speaks for the Clan* +2) · 3× Tessak Diver · 1× Tessak Scout in reserve. Sixty adults live here; say the number once.
- **The water is their arena** — *Of the Deep Channels* gives every Tessak advantage on nearly everything wet. The party fights in the kitchen of the thing that eats it.
- **The containers sink** — Gault's rig fires first exchange; evidence goes into the mud. The fight costs the investigation, win or lose.
- **They don't fight to the death.** The clan submerges the moment the math goes bad — *Slip the Net*, all of them, gone.
- **Afterward:** the party is food-that-threatens, permanently. The détente cracks — Drevath hardens, Gault keeps his workforce *and* a grievance. Deep-Mire water stays hostile the rest of the campaign. Survivable. Expensive.

## Outcomes

1. **The Weighing lands (Progress 3).** Longest pause of the scene, then: *"The count holds. The deal with the oil-man ends when the barge does not come back for his boxes. You will not see us end it. You will simply stop finding nets."* Coda: *"You negotiated. That was good math, meat. Carry this so the channels know it."* **The prow-tooth**, pressed into a palm, cold from the water.
2. **Weighed and pending (Progress 2 at time-out).** *"Bring the count. We will weigh it."* One return trip with better evidence reopens it. The rays keep dying while they wait.
3. **The Weighing fails (Consequence 3).** Clean exit, shut door, meat forever after. The deal still dies later when the shallows empty — slower, no credit, no tooth.
4. **The fight.** Above. Worst outcome for everyone except Gault.
