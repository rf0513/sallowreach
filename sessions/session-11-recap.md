## ⭐ New Canon (GM, decided at wrap)

- **Caius paid Gault to sink the ship.** Motive: make Sallowreach look dangerous, unstable, and in need of the Empire. **Marcel is no longer part of this.**
- **Tenson was the target**, because he's **Magistra Silence's favorite.** Her favorite dead in Mire water makes the annexation an easy sell.
- **The Tessak eat what's left.** That's why Gault's wrecks leave no witnesses.
- **Caius's undoing: Bigsby transcribes everything for him, even his malfeasance.** The payment to Gault is in the record ([[bigsby]]).
