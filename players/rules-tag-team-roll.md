# Player Reference — Tag Team Roll

*A rules reminder card, not an in-fiction prop. Post it in Roll20 so it's there if you want it — nobody's required to use it.*

## What it is

Once per session, you can team up with a willing ally mid-action.

**How it works:**
1. Spend **3 Hope**.
2. Describe with your ally how your two actions combine into one move.
3. You both roll.
4. Take the **better** of the two results.
5. If it's an attack: **both of you roll damage, and it adds together.**

That's it. One PC, one ally, one roll, better result wins — and on a hit, double damage dice.

## When it feels good to call it

- A fight's climax — the moment that should feel like the finishing blow.
- Two characters whose skills obviously combine (one holds something steady, the other strikes; one distracts, the other closes).
- Any time the table's energy says "this is the big swing" and you've got the Hope to spend.

Nobody has to use this. It's here so it's not forgotten when the moment's right.
