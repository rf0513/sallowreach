# Session 4–5 Set Piece — The Redline Meeting

*The scene the table has already scheduled itself: Tenson returns [[incident-report-handout|the incident report]] marked up, and [[caius-maren|Caius]] receives the edits. A fencing match conducted entirely in ink — and the delivery vehicle for the arc's most important plant: **[[bigsby|Bigsby]] demonstrates, on screen, that the Embassy contains an unsteered record.** The players will never invent "subpoena the furniture" on their own; this scene shows it to them three times at three brightnesses. Fires whenever the party goes back to the Embassy with the report — S4 or S5, it keeps.*

---

## What Caius wants from this meeting *(GM compass)*

Signatures. An amended, signed report is still **document fourteen** — he genuinely does not care about most of the wording, and that indifference is the scene's central clue. He will concede edit after edit with real grace, because he's playing a longer game than sentences. The horror, for a careful reader: **Tenson can win every battle and the document still points the same direction.** Rung 0 of [[tenson-arc]], taught a second time, at a higher level: you can change every sentence except where it's going.

## The Fencing Match — how Caius handles each redline

*(Run this as genuine back-and-forth over the player's actual markups — he redlined a real handout; honor every edit he fought for. The patterns:)*

**He concedes instantly, and makes the concession serve him:**
- **Remy's skill restored?** *"Of course. 'A guide of exceptional personal skill' — better, in fact. It underscores how much depended on one man."* *(The compliment is real. The frame — one irreplaceable man, no system — still feeds the file.)*
- **The city's warmth, the rescue, the welcome?** He'll take it as color anywhere Tenson likes — introduction, appendix — *"it does the city credit."* He resists it only in the **findings**: *"Charity is not infrastructure. I can't file kindness as a safety provision — I wish I could."*
- **The attributed quote struck** (*"no party responsible"*)? Gone without a fight. He apologizes for it, and means it: *"Clumsy of my office. A synthesis reached too far."* *(Note the word he uses. It comes back.)*

**He fights — politely, twice, and both fights are framed as protecting THEM:**
- **The word "sabotage."** *"An unproven allegation of sabotage, over your four signatures, invites imperial counter-inquiry — of you. 'Explosion of undetermined origin' states what you know and exposes you to nothing. I'm trying to keep your names safe in a file I can't control once it leaves this desk."* *(Every clause of that is true. That's what makes him good.)*
- **Any edit that attributes the incident to a person rather than the place.** He'll rephrase around it three different ways before he'll accept it. This is the tell — the one direction the document is not allowed to point.

**🎲 The rolls (3, dice-forward):**
- **Presence, Difficulty 14** — win a *contested* edit (each fight rolled separately; concessions cost nothing). On a win with Fear: the edit stands, and Caius files something about Tenson away behind his eyes.
- **Instinct, Difficulty 13** — *fallback only, if the player doesn't say it himself:* the conclusions section never moves. Findings bend, warmth enters, language softens — the **Assessment** paragraph survives every version untouched. A document written to reach a conclusion, again — this time with the author sitting across the table being charming about it.
- **Instinct, Difficulty 12** — read Caius's genuine pleasure: he is enjoying this. Nobody in two years has been worth negotiating with. *(True, disarming, and worth a Fear later.)*

## THE PLANT — Bigsby's Demonstration *(the scene's real payload — three brightnesses)*

### Brightness 1 — SHOWN *(guaranteed; fire this no matter what)*

At some point Tenson disputes what was actually *said* at dinner — the struck quote, Remy's description, anything. Caius, without looking up, does what he always does with the furniture:

> "Attendant — the record, please."
>
> The thing in the corner pauses. Under the surface of its face, fine lines of script stream sideways, like a ledger being riffled by wind. Then, in a voice as even as ruled paper:
>
> "Retrieving. On the fourteenth instant, at the second course, the witness stated, quote: 'Remy got us out. I don't — sorry — without Remy we're dead, all four of us, it's that simple.' End quote. No statement regarding responsibility appears anywhere in the record."
>
> It even did the stammer. It reproduced Fletcher's *stammer.*
>
> And Caius nods along, pleasant, unbothered — "A synthesis, as I said. My office reached too far" — and moves to the next line item like nothing whatsoever just happened.

**What the table just watched:** the original contradicting the copy, out loud, in the author's own dining room — and the author *not caring*, because to Caius the attendant is a pen. Give it a beat of air. Let a player say "wait."

### Brightness 2 — SAID, free, no roll *(Tenson's citadel expertise — the standing rule)*

The moment the demonstration lands, hand Tenson the character's knowledge plainly, [[tenson-arc#The Paper Trail — a map of every document in play|Budding Bureaucrat rule]]:

> "Tenson — you *know* this, from years of citadel work. Living-spell attendants aren't clerks. They're commissioned by the imperial records office, and under records procedure the attendant's transcript **is the legal original.** Summaries are courtesy copies. Everything that has ever been said or filed in this building has an unsteered original — and it's standing in the corner, holding a pen it doesn't need. Caius can edit anything that crosses his desk. He cannot edit *that.*"

The deduction that follows — what the original of the *whole file* might show, and how one might lawfully ask — stays the player's.

### Brightness 3 — SAID AGAIN, another mouth *(fallback, only if the table doesn't bite within a session)*

Lucien Aubert at [[the-long-room|the Long Room]], when Tenson's next dispatch to [[silence|Silence]] goes out — dry, over his ledger:

> "You dined at that Embassy with an *attendant* in the room, and you're litigating the wording of the summary?" He doesn't look up. "The spell is the record, man. The paper is what the Liaison decided the record meant. Any auditor worth their seal would be asking what else the attendant heard." A beat. "That advice was free. It's the last thing here that is."

### The courtesy beat *(optional tonight, load-bearing eventually)*

If Tenson addresses Bigsby **directly** — a question, a thank-you, anything spoken to the attendant as a party rather than a fixture — fire the beat from [[bigsby|his file]]:

> The pause is longer than usual. The script under its surface goes completely still, for the first time all evening.
>
> "Query addressed to the attendant directly." A beat. "This has not occurred previously."

**Guardrail:** [[caius-maren|Caius]] notices *nothing* — not the demonstration's weight, not the courtesy, not the crack. Two years of treating the construct as furniture is characterological; his blindness to Bigsby is the door the whole thread walks in through, and it stays shut to *him* until far too late. Do not let him wise up because the players did.

## If they sign · if they stall · if they refuse

- **They sign the amended version:** Caius is warm, grateful, *genuinely* pleased — and document fourteen is filed, in a version four witnesses fought for, which makes it stronger. Don't punish the choice; just let Tenson notice, later, which parts survived.
- **They stall:** he never presses twice in one meeting. The screw turns administratively, one notch per session: (1) a warm follow-up note; (2) an offer of *assistance* — records access, resources, help with their "situation" — generosity as leverage; (3) the quiet one: a **credentials verification** on "a traveler bearing citadel introduction," dispatched to the capital through imperial channels. That query takes weeks, and it will cross [[silence|Silence's]] path — a clock the party can't see starts ticking, and her next letter arrives knowing something Tenson didn't tell her.
- **They refuse outright:** he accepts it with perfect grace — *"Then I'll file my own account as unwitnessed observation. Weaker, but honest about what it is."* And he stops needing them. The warmth remains; the *usefulness* protection quietly lapses ([[caius-maren|his file]]: useful things are maintained). The party should feel the temperature drop about two degrees and not be able to say where.
