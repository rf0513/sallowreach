# Session 7 — Recap (As Played): *Kept*

*The as-played record of Session 7 (2026-09-01). **Canon.** The plan was [[session-07-run-sheet]] (*"Kept"*); this is what landed. The Celebration is two days away (split-day → Long Rest 1 → Full Downtime/Town Day → Long Rest 2 → Daytime Celebration in Session 8).*

## Previously, on Sallowreach

Today was the split day!

Fletcher and Leavy sailed deep into the Mire to count dying rookeries for the Hunters' Guild. They met Old Plenty, a giant spoonbill spirit who speaks only in tallies, and fought the massive Bull Gar that had been terrorizing the nests. They dragged its carcass back to town, recovered a shed snake skin stitched down the spine with an imperial **2H-3** cargo brand, and Leavy took a calcified spirit-antler while discovering Orna's frantic notes about a lost miracle cure.

In town, Tenson went to the southwest salt tower and danced with Oswin Cray—uncovering Caius's bogus denial memos alongside Oswin's suspicious 800-coin "Field Research" line, before enjoying a surprisingly rowdy, fun vegetarian dinner with Sable and the garrison.

And Isotta walked Old Basile to the Elm House, cracked the 70-year mystery of Marguerite Morel, and guided him to a peaceful crossing. The Tallykeeper arrived to balance the ledger, leaving a cryptic chill: spirits flee before the Celebration like animals before a disaster.

## The Beats (canon)

- **The Rookery & Old Plenty** ([[set-piece-the-wrong-skin]], as played). Fletcher and Leavy arrived at the dying rookery. Old Plenty stood up, spoke in tallies, and confirmed the deep channels were emptied four years ago. The spirit gave them the 12-foot kept shed skin from the high branches.
- **The Bull Gar Fight & Harvest.** Leavy cast root-binding magic to restrain the monster; Fletcher and Leavy brought down the Bull Gar. They **towed the entire carcass back to town**. Fletcher harvested bone-hard scales to knap into arrowheads. Leavy received a **calcified spirit-antler** from Old Plenty as a catalyst for high-tier brewing.
- **Orna's Chaotic Notebook.** Leavy cracked Orna's borrowed 5th notebook and discovered her frantic, crossed-out notes trying to document her lost "all-cure" miracle recipe before her mind dissolves into swamp, referencing sap from the southwest tree.
- **Guild Debrief & The Reveal.** Fletcher showed Brin Sevarre the traps taken from Gault and unrolled the 12-foot skin. Brin confirmed Gault's poacher gear, recognized the stitched seam as laboratory-made, and identified the **2H-3** stamp as imperial cargo freight. Fletcher got paid Guild bounty; Brin is reaching out to old contacts for upcoming snake hunts.
- **The Maintenance House & The Dual-Audit** ([[set-piece-the-maintenance-house]], as played). Tenson visited the southwest tower and questioned apprentice Aldric (learning the wall is failing fast, but settles for two days after every Celebration). Tenson danced with Oswin and audited the papers: catching Caius's lack of fiscal authority and Oswin's 800-coin "Field Research" skim. Tenson left without confronting Oswin immediately, taking the crate of **Augury Vane's 200-year-old journals**.
- **Barracks Dinner with Sable.** Tenson had dinner at the Drillfield mess hall. Sable served a fresh raw spread of marsh celery and walnuts. **Tenson genuinely had fun** with the brawny, rowdy garrison.
- **The Elm House Deduction** ([[set-piece-she-lets-me-win]], as played). Isotta and Basile visited Yvette Morel, experienced true Sallowreach warmth, sorted the jumbled trunks, and deduced the name: **Marguerite**. Basile's memory flooded back when his hand touched the gemstone on his cane (*"She let me win on purpose... walk me home"*).
- **The Crossing & The Tallykeeper.** At the Still Cup with Vael, Basile squared the dominoes (*"Keep the score"*) and crossed peacefully, leaving behind **Basile's Ivory Domino**. The Tallykeeper arrived to collect the clean debt and dropped a cryptic warning: **spirits disappear on Celebration days, like animals fleeing before a natural disaster**. Vael confirmed it.
- **Isotta's Next Move:** Isotta is now 1000% convinced something dark is happening during the Celebration and plans to visit Adaline Roux at the Cultural Center / Hall of the Mire. Vael warned her to tread carefully.
