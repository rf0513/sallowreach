## 🧭 In Plain Words *(the whole session — no jargon)*

Last session ended in the crime boss's back room. The party asked to buy his pet owlbear cub so they could give it back to its mother, who's waiting outside with her other cub. He said yes, for a favor from the auditor.

Tonight opens right there. The favor: take the evidence the auditor already found against the wall wizard to the wizard's Guild, and get him fired. Once the wizard loses his Guild protection, the crime boss can collect what the wizard owes him, however he likes. The crime boss hands the cub over now, on a handshake. As a receipt he gives the auditor a short piece of old rope, a piece of the noose the Empire hanged him with. He wants it back when the job's done.

The party takes the cub upstairs, the mother leaves town with both cubs, and the party walks home. On the way, hired thugs try to grab the ranger alive, to ship him somewhere as cargo. It's a short, fun fight.

They sleep. Then it's the Celebration, the city's big weekly festival: a day of games and food, then sixty thousand people singing an old chant nobody can translate. Partway through, the imperial official who never leaves his building appears at the feast. He turns out to be a paper copy of himself, sent to pester the auditor for a signature. During the chant, the party's curse pulls tight. At the lantern release, all four of them recognize the exact sensations they felt the night their ship sank. End on the quiet walk home.

## 🎲 Dice First

*The reflex: the moment a PC tries to* get *something — read a person, find a thing, push past a "no" — call the roll* **before** *you narrate the result.*

**Action roll** = 2d12 (Hope die + Fear die) + trait vs **Difficulty.** Hope die high → PC gains **Hope.** Fear die high → **you** gain **Fear.** Matching dice = **Crit** (auto-succeed, gain Hope, clear Stress).

- ⭐ **Spend a Hope → +2 from a relevant Experience.** Say which one when you call the roll.
- **Help an Ally** → helper spends 1 Hope, rolls +d6.
- **Advantage** → +1d6.
- ⭐ **Under Marcel's eye** *(Beat 0 only, the Pale Room's Composure passive)*: any roll that lies to Marcel, pushes him, or tries to read him — **mark 1 Stress first, or roll with disadvantage.** Player's choice.

**Four answers, not two:** success-with-Hope · success-with-Fear · failure-with-Hope · failure-with-Fear.

**Diff ladder:** 11 baseline · 13–14 hard · 15 a real reach.
