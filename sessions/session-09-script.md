# Session 9 — The Script *(read straight down — nothing else to open)*

**Tonight:** the casino, then a mother owlbear, then a fight on the walk home. **Don't start the Celebration.**
**On the table:** 6 chips · the freight tag · a napkin + pen · Isotta's card *(copy her read from Beat 3)*.
**Fear:** spend it on the two fights, not the casino. Keep 2–3 back.

---

## 0 · Oswin's laugh

> So the girl's still smiling at you. She's already got Tenson's coat over her arm — and honestly, nobody saw her take it.
>
> Past her the room opens up — white tablecloths, green felt, and the lamps are turned way down, so everything's kind of amber.
>
> And it's quiet. Like, nice quiet. Just cards slapping, and glasses, and somebody laughing at the bar.
>
> And then, from the far corner, there's a different laugh. A little too loud. A little too high.
>
> Tenson — you know that laugh. You sat across a desk from it two days ago.
>
> It's Oswin. He hasn't seen you yet.

**Put down the 6 chips. Say:**

> "See these six chips? That's Oswin's stack. Every time anybody rolls in here, I take one away. When they're gone, so is he — the house walks him out. If you want something from him, you're on the clock."

🎲 **Instinct 13** *(Eye For Danger)* or **Knowledge 13** *(Budding Bureaucrat)*. **Take a chip.**

**On a success:**

> He's wearing glasses. Little round ones, smoked dark, like for sun.
>
> In here. Where you can barely read your own cards.
>
> And when he tips his head toward the man next to him — just for a second, the lamp catches the lenses, and you'd swear you can see that man's cards in them. Backwards. Like a reflection in a shop window.
>
> He hasn't noticed you noticing.

- **With Fear, add:** *"Across the room, the girl who took your coats is looking right at you. She looks away. She doesn't hurry."*
- **Failure:** he's just winning, loud and charming. He buys the table a round.
- ⭐ Wait for Tenson's player to say ***"He doesn't wear glasses."*** If they don't, the bartender's regular says it later.

## 1 · The floor *(~20 min — every roll takes a chip)*

**Ask:** *"What do you do?"* Offer these:

- **The Count** — three shells, one black pearl. 🎲 **Instinct 13.** Crit: the dealer nods at you — the room's highest compliment.
- **Pins** — a tower of bone pins. Pull one, the pot doubles. 🎲 **Agility 11 → 13 → 15.** Stop any time. Topple it and the three nearest tables go quiet.
- **Wake** *(the long card game)* — win **4 hands** before you fold. 🎲 **Finesse or Presence 12** each hand. ⚠ Eats 4 chips.

**The bar — Octave** *(old man, third stool from the end, a row of free drinks he never touches)*:

> "Two to one you're here about the man in the back. No — don't tell me. I like the suspense."

- **To Tenson, warmly, cutting them off mid-sentence:** *"Look at the shoulders on you! Five to one you've never lost an arm-wrestle."*
- **If nobody spotted the glasses:** *"Wizard got new spectacles. Started winning the same week. I'd lay four to one they're not for reading."*
- **If anyone mentions tomorrow:** *"Marcel feeds the whole city tomorrow. Every pot on every table. And not one person'll say his name over it."*

**Camille** *(the hostess)* **is the tied-up girl Whimsy remembered from their boat.** Say nothing until the players do. If they name the boat or describe the bow:

> Her smile doesn't move. But for one second — just one — her hand goes to something in her apron pocket. Then she's fine.

Later: *"Did you ever repaint the bow?"* — or their drinks just stop being charged.
If asked about the cage: *"The cold men opened the cage. One of them gave me his coat because I was shaking. Nobody said anything the whole way home."*

### Tenson sits with Oswin

> He looks up. And for a second his whole face just — drops.
>
> Then the smile's back on. "The *auditor.* Of course. Of course it is. Sit, sit — you'll want to watch me win, it's very educational."
>
> He doesn't take the glasses off.

**Slide the napkin and pen to Tenson's player.**

- **"I saw your hands"** → he goes white. *"Everyone in here cheats. They just do it with money."* [beat] *"What do you want."*
- **"Marcel knows"** → he laughs. *"He'd have said. That's the thing about Marcel — he always says."* [beat] *"…How long?"*
- **A payback schedule** → he reads it twice. *"Quarterly. Into the ward."* [laugh] *"You've made me into an account."*

🎲 **Presence 13** *(Budding Bureaucrat; advantage if they saw the glasses)* → **success:** he signs · **with Fear:** he signs, and Camille's watching · **failure:** *"Write it to the Guild."*

### When the last chip goes

> Two men in grey cross the room. They're not in a hurry. Nobody in here is ever in a hurry.
>
> One of them puts a hand on Oswin's shoulder. Really gently. Like a friend would.
>
> Oswin looks up. He's still smiling. And then he isn't.
>
> They walk him to the stair. He doesn't fight it. He leaves his chips right there on the felt.
>
> And the dealer just sweeps them in.

## 3 · The table *(Marcel: calm, polite, never tricks anyone, never raises his voice)*

> There's a door at the back you didn't see before. She walks you through it.
>
> It's a smaller room — just one card table and one lamp.
>
> There's a man at the table, and he stands up when you come in. He's tall, in a grey suit — a really good one. And he's not sweating. Everybody in this city sweats, all the time, and he's just… dry.
>
> "Fletcher. Leavy. Isotta. Tenson." He gets every name right, and he doesn't say how.
>
> He shakes your hands. His is cold — not cool, *cold* — and it creeps up your wrist before he lets go.
>
> "Please. Sit. Can I get anyone a drink?"
>
> And when you're all sitting, he asks what you need. Not what you want. What you *need.*

**Say once:** *"Any roll that lies to him or pushes him — mark a Stress first, or roll with disadvantage. Your pick."*

### 👁️ Hand Isotta her card *(to her alone)*

> Isotta — every ghost you've ever met is an *unfinished* thing. Open. Aching to be tied off. That's what your sight is for.
>
> He reads **finished.** Tied. Knotted — the knot every spirit at that wall would drown the world to get. A closed account.
>
> And the closed account is standing behind the card table, dry in a room full of sweating people, asking if anyone would like another drink.

### Caspar

> "Before the spear." *[beat]* "The curator."
>
> "You took Caspar Renne out on your boat, and you came back without him. I'd like to hear it from you."

**Stop talking. Let them answer.**

- **Truth → no roll.** *"Thank you."* *"A man can run a long way from a closet of vases. That's his business now."*
- **Lie → 🎲 Presence 14.** Success: *"I'm sorry for your trouble."* · **Failure:** *"That's not all of it."* [beat] *"That's allowed. It isn't my business until it touches the terms."* *(Their word is worth nothing tonight.)*

**Then, either way — like it's small talk:**

> "Caspar thought I wanted him dead. I wanted him *nervous.* He tried to cut me out once. Nervous was the lesson."
>
> *[beat]*
>
> "And he paid. Every month. Men who pay don't have accidents."

🔒 **Only if someone says the Embassy has the report:** *"Not yet, it doesn't."*

### The Deal

> "The spear. You've come for it, and it's yours, for one thing."
>
> "The ward wizard has been stealing from his own wall to pay what he owes me. Deliver the evidence to his Guild in a way that results in his removal, and the spear is yours."

**Only if they ask:**

- **Why him?** → *"He owes me a great deal, and he's lied to me about paying it. And three weeks ago he started cheating at my tables."* [beat] *"In my house. Where the games are fair."*
- **Why not do it yourself?** → *"He wears a Guild coat on an imperial wall. Something happens to a man like that, the Empire sends someone to ask why. I'd rather nobody asked anything in this city."* [beat] *"Take the coat off him, and he's just a man who owes me money."*
- **What happens to him?** → *"Whatever collecting takes."* [beat] *"I don't decide that in advance."*
- **"We saw the glasses"** → *"Three weeks. I wondered if anyone else would see it."*

### The cub

> There's a sound behind a curtain at the back — claws on a wood floor.
>
> And an owlbear cub just pads out. She's about the size of a big dog, feathers on her head sticking up every which way.
>
> She walks right up to Marcel and puts her head on his knee.
>
> And he feeds her. From his hand. He doesn't stop talking. He doesn't even look down.

*It's Whimsy's sister. If they tell him the mother's coming:* he goes still. *"How close?"*

### The counter-offer

- **The napkin** → *"His signature is worth what his word is worth."* [beat] *"Whose name goes under it?"*
- 🎲 **Presence 14** *(Budding Bureaucrat)* — the terms decide; the dice just color it.
- **Before anyone agrees, he always says:** *"If the schedule breaks, the original price comes back. And the name under his is the name I'll come to."*
- **And:** *"The spear moves when the first term is met. Not before. That isn't distrust. It's the order things happen in."*

⚠ **Don't let them finish. Interrupt the closing with Beat 4.**

## 4 · ⚔️ The mother

> The cub picks her head up off his knee.
>
> She makes a sound — high and thin, like a kettle right before it starts to whistle.
>
> Every glass on the table starts shivering — like, all of them, at once.
>
> A little line of dust comes down from the ceiling, right onto the felt.
>
> And out on the floor, somebody comes running down the stair. First time all night anybody's hurried.
>
> Marcel's gone completely still.
>
> He looks at the cub. Then he looks at you.

**Wait for someone to say "that's her mother."** *(Marcel: "…Ah.")* **Wait for someone to ask for the cub.** Then:

> He doesn't answer right away.
>
> He picks the cub up. She's heavy, and he doesn't make a show of it. He just holds her for a second.
>
> He says something to her — really quiet. You don't catch it.
>
> Then he holds her out to you.
>
> "Go on. Before she finds the stair."

*As they go:* *"Keep her off my stair and we'll discuss what that's worth."*

### Top of the stair

> You come up the stair and it's *loud* up here.
>
> The whole boardwalk's done up for tomorrow. There are lanterns strung between every post — not lit yet — and folding tables leaned up against every door.
>
> People are standing in their doorways in their nightclothes.
>
> And down at the end of the boardwalk, something big is coming. The planks are popping up in front of her like loose floorboards.
>
> Marcel's two men are at the top of the stair with crossbows, already shooting. A bolt sticks in her shoulder and she doesn't even slow down.
>
> She's coming straight for the stair. Straight for what you're holding.

### Her stats

**The Mother Owlbear — Tier 2 Solo** · **Diff 14** · Thresholds **10/20** · **HP 9** · Stress 4 · ATK **+3**
- **Raking Talons** — Melee, 2d8+3
- **Storm of Talons** — mark a Stress: hit everyone in Melee, 2d8+3
- **Relentless** — spend Fear to act again, up to 3 times a turn
- **The Keening** — the first time each PC gets Close, they mark a Stress
- **Wing-Burst** *(1 Fear)* — everyone Close gets thrown to Far and marks a Stress. Then she walks *past* them

**Two rules:** she only attacks what's between her and the cub. **Never let a cub get hurt by accident** — warn twice.

**Toys:** the stair *(narrow — Tenson holds it)* · drop a lantern string on her, 🎲 **Finesse 13** = one free round · shove a table, **Strength 13** · stop the crossbows, 🎲 **Presence 13** — or shout down the stair and Marcel says *"Enough."* · a kid in a doorway · a man in a nightshirt at the far end, writing in a notebook *(the Empire's man — Marcel's nightmare)*

**Round one or two:** she steps *over* a downed PC, toward the cub. Wait for Fletcher's player: *"She's walking a scent. We're just standing on it."*
**Isotta, free:** *"There's no ghost here. All this grief, and every heart on this field is still beating."*

### ⭐ The Calm-Down

**Say it the first time someone lowers their weapon:**

> "If you want to calm her instead of fight her — weapons down, cub forward, voice low. Instinct or Presence, 14. Three successes calms her. Three failures and she's done listening. **And any hit on her counts as a failure — so do those crossbows.**"

- Fletcher has **advantage.** Isotta can add **Animal Whisperer.**
- **1 success:** *"She stops mid-swing. Her head snaps around. First time all night she's looking at someone instead of through them."*
- **2 successes:** *"She's gone still. Chest heaving. The keening drops so low it's almost gone — she's listening."*
- **3 successes:**

> She goes down. This mountain of talons and slate — she folds down onto her forelegs in front of the cub, and the sound she makes at it is nothing like the keening. It's small. It's the smallest sound you have ever heard something that big make.
>
> And the cub answers it.
>
> You've just watched a storm turn back into a mother. Take a breath. Nobody at this table is going to forget tonight.

- **3 failures:** free Wing-Burst; she shoves past toward the cub. Keep fighting.
- **They hit her Severe threshold:** she pulls back. She'll come again in two nights.

### She doesn't go

> She doesn't go.
>
> She's just standing there in the middle of the boardwalk with the cub riding up on her shoulders, breathing hard.
>
> And then her head turns. Slow. Off over the rooftops, toward the other side of town.
>
> Fletcher — you know which way that is. That's the Ranging House.
>
> She takes one step that way. Then she stops, and she looks back at you.

**Whimsy's there.** Ask: *"Do you walk her over, or go back down to Marcel?"*
- **Walk her:** Brin opens the pen. Both cubs, one mother, gone into the Mire. *(Camille on the stair: "Monsieur Dray has time. He'll have it after the lanterns.")* → go to **Beat 5.**
- **Go back down:** she goes alone. Brin handles it. They hear about it later.

## 5 · ⚔️ The walk home

⚠ **Out of time?** Read just the arrow, hand over the tag, and end.

> So it's late. Like, really late. The boardwalk's empty — just the tables leaned up against the doors for tomorrow, and the lanterns strung up overhead, still not lit.
>
> Fletcher, there's a punt drifting along the channel next to you. No light on it.
>
> Somebody on it stands up.
>
> You don't see the bow until it's already moving.

🎲 **Agility 13 — Reaction** *(Eye For Danger)* → **success:** it thunks into the post an inch from his head · **failure:** it grazes him — **mark an HP.**

> The arrow's still shaking in the wood.
>
> *[beat]*
>
> It's yours. The one you put across Gault's bow.
>
> There's a tag tied to it with string. A freight tag. Just a number.

**Hand him the tag.**

> And this time the punt doesn't go anywhere. It bumps up against the boards.
>
> There's another one behind it.
>
> People are climbing up onto the walk. Four, five, six of them — boat hooks, knives, and somebody finally lights a lantern.
>
> The one in front's got a sling hanging loose in one hand. He nods at Fletcher. Really polite.
>
> "Evening. We're just here for the frog. The rest of you can walk home."

### The hands *(this one's the fun fight — let them win big)*

**2 Runners** · Diff **12** · Thresholds **7/13** · HP **5** · Stress 2 · ATK **+1** · Hook (Melee) 1d8+1 · Sling (Far) 1d6+1
*When a Runner takes a hit, it can mark a Stress to duck behind a table or a friend.*

**6 Lackeys** · Diff **10** · **One hit drops one** *(every 4 damage drops another nearby)* · Knife: 2 damage
*Spend 1 Fear: every Lackey near one target piles on — one attack, 2 damage each.*

- **They want Fletcher alive** — they grab and drag him toward the punt. No killing blows.
- **Toys:** tables · yank down a lantern string · **shove someone into the channel** · push a punt off with someone still on it.
- **They run** when the second Runner drops, or half the Lackeys are down: *"Not paid for this."* **Leave one behind.**

**The one who stayed** *(he just talks)*:

> "It was a job. That's all."
>
> "The frog. Alive." *[beat]* "Half up front."
>
> "Man at the night berths. Didn't give a name. Paid good." *[beat]* "He had the tag already written out."
