# The Loot Ledger — Treasure & Signature Items

> **The house loot philosophy:** no random gold, no +1 swords. Every item is a **toy with strings** — it does something fun at the table *and* pulls a thread (a relationship, an arc, a front). Signature items are **earned from people**, not found in chests; the Mire's chests are other people's graves. Currency stays in the canon **coins → handfuls → bags** scale ([[The Mooring]]). All mechanics are homebrew sketches — `[VERIFY]` every one against the SRD's loot/consumable rules before it hits the table, per house rule.

**Already in canon, not repeated here:** [[the-tidebreaker]] (the endgame key), [[the-first-maintainers-journal]] (quest item), Leavy's grimoire & the three draught-slips ([[leavy-arc]]), Seven's hoard props, the grace cord ([[the-tallykeeper]]), and **the party's boat** (S3 — see its upgrade menu below).

## Fletcher — the professional's kit, finally loved

### ⭐ The Blind-Weave Cloak *(signature — Brin's payment for Side-Quest 1)*
Guild-made: Mire reed split fine as thread, wax-stiffened, dappled like channel-light on water. Brin hands it over folded, with the Guild's coin on top: *"The coin's for the job. This is because I intend to keep sending you out."*
- **While still and downwind, beasts read Fletcher as scenery** — advantage on stealth against beasts, or beasts must beat his Difficulty to notice him `[VERIFY]`. Movement breaks it; it fools noses and eyes, **never the amphisbaena's Waking Head at close range** (nothing fools that — [[fletcher-arc]]).
- **The string:** it's the *ethical* version of what the deep water made [[gault|Gault]] — predators overlook them both, but Fletcher can take it off.

### Gravois's Tally-Broadheads *(pathos loot — the kept camp, Side-Quest 1)*
Six broadheads etched with population tally-marks, from the dead hunter's kit — kept dry and squared for two seasons by **Old Plenty**, the rookery's guardian spirit ([[set-piece-the-wrong-skin]]). Delivering the tally-book closes Brin's two-year "missing, presumed" file; the arrows are what Gravois leaves the living. Brin, handing them over: *"Six left. He'd want them spent right."*
- **A tagged wound tracks:** a creature struck by one leaves a trail Fletcher follows unerringly for a day `[VERIFY]` — built for the hunt's real problem: **tag the snake, don't fight it yet.** Six. Count them at the table.

### Corentin's Reserve Tin *(comedy consumable — the eel-smoker, S3 callback)*
Smoked eel so good it's contraband-adjacent. Corentin presses it on Fletcher: *"For the next demon. They all eat, friend."*
- 3 uses: advantage to bait, lure, or distract **any** creature — beast, Tessak, or unwary person `[VERIFY]`. It has never once failed. Do not ask what's in the brine.

## Leavy — the craft made portable

### ⭐ The Tessant Cauldron *(signature — Orna's gift, after the gloamcap run)*
Orna's third-best travel cauldron, copper gone green-gold, fits in a pack, always faintly warm. Given with zero ceremony: *"You stir like it matters. Things that are stirred like it matters remember. Take it before I dissolve enough to get sentimental."*
- **It remembers:** advantage on any recipe Leavy has successfully brewed in it before; holds a working temperature without fire `[VERIFY]`.
- **The string (GM):** it remembers *Orna's* brews too. Some nights it hums a recipe nobody taught it. This is the physical path to the **lost miracle-cure** ([[orna-tessant]]'s secret) — endgame candy for the Tessant Codex thread ([[leavy-arc]] idea bank). It is also, quietly, coven-shaped: a hearth that travels. *(Guardrail: it never touches the memory-draughts — those stay tongue-keyed.)*

### The Thimble Tap *(party-sharing toy — Consortium make, gloamcap-run or Cold Cache loot)*
A silver thimble, Consortium-stamped. Fill it from any potion: another creature can drink the thimbleful and get **one round of the effect, or the taste told true** — a safe preview `[VERIFY]`.
- **The point:** Leavy's craft becomes something the *party* handles — pass-the-thimble is a table ritual. **It does not work on the memory-draughts.** The one time someone tries, it tastes of nothing at all, which is its own clue.

### Consortium Seal-Jars ×4 *(Cold Cache loot — [[fletcher-arc]] Side-Quest 2)*
Waxed stasis-jars, Consortium-stamped, from Gault's cache: whatever goes in stays exactly as fresh as it went in `[VERIFY]`. Worth handfuls each — or worth more *unsold:* returning them to the Consortium with the right paperwork is a door-opener for the gloamcap errand. One arrives **occupied** (GM's choice — something small, glowing, and alive; what the party does with it is a quiet Gault-thesis test).

## Isotta — light for the lit doorway

### ⭐ Anselme's Stern-Lantern *(signature — the Drowned Poler's legacy, [[session-04-salt-wall]]; delivery re-cut post-S4)*
The brass stern-lantern off the boat Anselme built for Solène. *As played, he crossed before any handover* — so the lantern arrives through **Solène**: she finds it among her father's gear when the punt comes off its tarp (the **Thouvenel Lease**, [[coin-in-sallowreach]]), and gives it to the woman who carried his words. Her line does the work his would have: *"He kept a light burning at the stern his whole life. You're the only one out here still keeping one. So."* [she doesn't finish the sentence — she just hands it over]
- **It burns without oil on thin-veil nights.** Its light is warm to the dead and counts as Isotta's **anchor** — she can sleep inside its glow (managing her "the dark pulls" cost). Once per rest, grounding by its light clears a Slipping segment or a Stress `[VERIFY]`.
- **Spirits inside its light parley calm** — advantage on Presence with the dead in its radius `[VERIFY]`.
- **The cost that comes with the gift (house style):** the dead can see it from *very* far away. The Lantern Docket queue grows. She is now visibly, deliberately open for business — and one more light the [[the-tallykeeper|Tallykeeper]] can navigate by.

### Grave-Chalk, One Stick *(tactical toy — Vael's drawer, earned during a docket case)*
Unbleached chalk cut with grave-salt from the Order's old stores — the one piece of her toolkit that **doesn't bite her.** A line drawn with it wards like salt but is keyed to *let her through:* the rare warding line the half-ghost can cross `[VERIFY]`.
- One stick. It draws maybe a dozen lines. Vael, dry: *"The Order makes one of these per slayer. You get mine. Don't chalk anything you love."*

## Tenson — the audit, weaponized

### ⭐ The Ridgeback Door *(signature — commissioned from [[Théo Rouchard]], paid or gifted via [[Remy Duval]])*
A tower shield built from the salvaged hull-door of **The Ridgeback** — Remy's murdered boat, pulled off the bank where the party washed ashore. Théo's best work; Remy's idea: *"She carried you to shore once. Let her keep doing it."*
- **Stalwart synergy:** when Tenson takes damage meant for an ally behind the Door, reduce it / mark Stress instead of HP `[VERIFY vs Guardian/Stalwart features — build to complement, not duplicate]`.
- **The string:** it is physical proof of the sabotage — scorch-patterning on the timbers that an investigator (or an incident report…) could read. Caius has *opinions* about this shield existing. Tenson carries the crime scene into every room.
- **The Rung-4 bridge ([[tenson-arc]]):** when a professional eye (Fletcher's hazard-read, or Théo mid-build) reads the pattern as a **rigged charge** — shown — Remy says the rest: *"A hired job? On my water? Nothing gets* hired *on this water without the Pale Room hearing the price."* That line is what sends the party to Marcel with the right question. It fires off the salvaged timbers whether or not the shield is ever commissioned.

### The Truth-Teller's Ink *(plot key disguised as loot — [[the-long-room|the Long Room]], from Lucien Aubert; hand it over in [[set-piece-the-long-room]] Beat 3)*
A pot of sap-red archival ink, native-made, older than the imperial presence. Brushed thin over a page, it **blooms where the page has been altered** — erasures, substitutions, a signature written over a scraped name `[VERIFY — mostly a narrative key; roll only under pressure]`.
- **The point:** Tenson's redlining becomes a superpower. Aim it at the incident report, Caius's file, Oswin's funding denials, the Cold Cache waybill. Lucien's price isn't coin — it's a favor owed to the Long Room, on the books, properly witnessed. *(Tenson will insist on the paperwork. Let him.)*

### Sable's Whistle *(warm loot — the Drillfield, after "Sweat First")*
A dented brass drill-whistle, Sable's own. *"You coached Perrin better than I ever drilled him. Whistle's yours, brain-boy. Means you're staff."*
- Once per session, when Tenson coaches an ally through a moment (his buried sports-medicine skill, out loud), that ally clears a Stress or gains a Hope `[VERIFY]`.
- **Table joke with teeth:** locals go pale if he whistles it *on the water* — you don't whistle on the water ([[session-01-prep]] superstition, now a prop the GM can fire).

## The Boat — the party's biggest item, and its upgrade menu

The smuggler's boat (S3) is loot that keeps on looting. Upgrades are the **coin sink** now that the boat-fund is solved — each is a story errand, not a shop line:

| Upgrade | Source / errand | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **A name & christening** | The party — locals insist; an unnamed boat is unlucky | A naming scene, not a stat. *(Quiet arc rhyme: the boat gets a true name before Leavy does. Fletcher — "naming is for things you keep" — gets outvoted, and secretly likes it.)* |
| **Tessak prow-tooth** | Earned in the Side-Quest 2 parley, or traded for wreck-salvage | Deep-channel clans read the boat as *spoken-for* — one free pass or parley opening per trip `[VERIFY as narrative]`. Cuts both ways: it also reads as *involved.* |
| **Ray-safe running lamp** | [[Remy Duval]] builds it; lantern-ray shutter-glass | The boat's light doesn't spook Mire bioluminescence — wonder access: the rays will run beside the hull. Remy cries the first time. |
| **Poler's true-charts** | [[odile-verret|Odile]] or Remy, relationship-gated | Reduce one [[mire-complications]] roll per journey `[VERIFY]` — the Mire moves, but the charts know its habits. |
| **The scrubbed hull** | Théo's yard, a bag of coin | Strips the pipeline cache-marks — the boat stops reading as Gault's. **Choose:** disguise kept, or identity cleaned. Can't have both. |

## Consumables & curiosities *(drop-in wonder; all `[VERIFY]`)*

1. **Gloamcap spores, sealed twist** — thrown: a scene of soft, veil-dim gloom even at noon. Leavy's quest-adjacent; Orna calls wasting one "vandalism."
2. **Lantern-root preserve** — eat a spoonful: your outline glows faint gold for an hour. Useless. Beloved. The Celebration sells out of it.
3. **Mire Spirits, Celebration Reserve** — Marcel's bootleg, the good barrel. One glass: reroll one roll made with Fear tonight; the second glass always makes things worse. Illegal to export, naturally.
4. **Odile's reed-salve** — clears a Stress when applied by *someone else's hands* (it simply doesn't work self-applied — she made it that way on purpose).
5. **A Tallykeeper feather** *(found, never taken)* — a grey heron feather that is always cool to the touch. Does nothing. The dead go polite around whoever carries it. Isotta's player will not put it down.

## Villain loot — banked for the day it drops

- **Gault's weighted nets** *(masterwork)* — his restraint kit, if taken off him: advantage to restrain a target; the Season-Eater sheds out of them anyway, which is the joke `[VERIFY]`.
- **Gault's cache-map** — the true prize on his body or skiff: every way-station on the pipeline, in his own hand. It closes Brin's three-year case, hands the Guild the whole network — and marks two caches *even Fletcher's trail never found.*
- **The redacted waybill file** — Cold Cache + Cauvin's ledgers assembled: the paper that proves an imperial facility built 2H-3 and shipped it unmanifested. Not an item — *ammunition.* Who the party gives it to (Brin? Adaline? Caius's enemies?) is a campaign lever.
- **Old Patience's token** *(slot reserved)* — the encounter already banks partial tether relief; if it leaves a physical token, design it there, in its file, when the table gets close. Not pre-authored here.
