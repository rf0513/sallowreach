# The Mother Owlbear *(Front 9 — the set piece)*

*The combat the party built for themselves in Session 3: one cub wounded, saved, and kept at [[brin-sevarre|Brin's]] Ranging House; the other in [[marcel-dray|Marcel's]] parlor; the smuggler who took them, dead. She is coming for both, and no one manages her but the Mire. **Designed fight-optional:** she is a Tier 2 storm of talons with an exit built into the stat block — because she is not hunting anyone. She is searching. Lead with the want ([[sallowreach-roster]] rule), and let the table discover that the way to end this fight was never on a character sheet. Slot: tells from S4, arrival S5–6 — tick her with [[world-in-motion]] Front 9. **Table-ready run file — read-alouds, roll ladder, performed Fear menu: [[set-piece-the-mother-arrives]].** This file is the stats and the branches; that one is the performance.*

*Thematic rhyme, for the GM's ear only: a vast grieving parent reaching toward stolen young, kept from them by human greed. The party meets the [[Mangrove Spirit]]'s whole story at fur-and-talon scale, and calms it with reunion — long before they understand they've rehearsed the campaign's ending.*

---

## The Approach — the escalation ladder *(one tell per session until she arrives)*

1. **The answering call.** Already canon: the night the cub was hurt, something answered from far off. If it wasn't played, it's remembered — Fletcher would have clocked it.
2. **Trappers' tales.** Something huge moving up the southwest channels. Kills found on the margins — *not eaten.* Torn open and left. She isn't hunting. She's looking inside things.
3. **The cub knows.** The saved cub at the Ranging House goes still at the same hour each night and cries toward the dark. Brin starts posting a night watch and won't say why in front of the guild.
4. **The near sign.** A print in boardwalk mud where no upland animal should be. A smashed crab-rack. A smell Fletcher knows from the nest-boat.
5. **Arrival.** Wherever the party isn't ready: see the three arenas.

## The Three Arenas *(where it happens depends on what the party did with the tells)*

- **The channels (best case — they intercepted).** The party picks the ground, brings the cub if they've planned, fights or calms on open water. Hardest logistics, most control.
- **The Ranging House (default — she follows the strongest scent).** Cub #1 is here. So are Brin, the maps, the guild. Brin cooperates completely with a reunion plan — her arithmetic is immaculate: *"A mother with her cub leaves. A mother without one keeps digging. Give her the cub."* The building is a toybox: net-rigs, specimen cages, the map-room choke point.
- **The boardwalks, heading for the Pale Room (worst case — nobody acted).** She follows the second scent into the city: civilians, panicked guards, [[marcel-dray|Marcel's]] people shooting at her from the casino windows, and [[caius-maren|Caius]] filing every splintered plank as incident evidence. This version is a disaster the party manages rather than a fight they win — and it torches the brokerage credit (see below).

## Stat Block — The Mother Owlbear

**Tier 2 Solo** *(statted a shade soft of the SRD benchmark — Gorgon, Tier 2 Solo: Diff 15, Thresholds 13/25 — on purpose: this is a Tier 1 party and a fight built to be ended, not won)*

*A storm of talons and grief. She does not want anyone dead. She wants what everyone in this campaign wants — the thing that was taken — and she is done asking the world nicely.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Find the cubs, tear open whatever is between her and the scent, leave when they're found

**Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 9 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** +3 | **Raking Talons:** Melee | 2d8+3 phy · **Crushing Beak:** Melee | 2d10+3 phy *(only against a target that has wounded her this scene)*
**Experience:** Follows the Scent +3

**Features:**
- **Relentless (3) — Passive:** she can be spotlighted up to three times per GM turn (spend Fear as usual). *(SRD wording confirmed — matches the Hydra's Relentless.)*
- **The Keening — Passive:** the first time each PC comes within Close range each scene, her grief lands somewhere old — that PC marks a Stress. *(Custom passive, legal idiom — cf. the SRD Shark's Terrifying.)*
- **Searching, Not Hunting — Passive (the exit, shown through behavior):** she never pursues a target who retreats *away* from a cub-scent; she presses only *toward* the scent. She attacks what stands between her and it. **This is the tell the party can read** — and per the clue rule it fires twice: shown by her behavior, said by Fletcher (free, no roll — his whole skill set): *"She's not hunting us. Look at her line — she's walking a scent. We're just standing on it."*
- **Storm of Talons — Action:** mark a Stress to attack every target within Melee range; targets hit take 2d8+3 physical.
- **Wing-Burst — Fear:** spend a Fear; every target within Close range is knocked back to Far range and marks a Stress. She uses this to *clear a path*, not to kill — she moves toward the scent immediately after. *("Knocked back to [range]" is the SRD's forced-movement idiom — cf. the Bear and Minotaur Wrecker.)*
- **Nothing Left to Lose — Passive:** if a cub is harmed in her presence, she fights to the death, the Calm-Down is locked forever, and Crushing Beak works on everyone. Do not let this happen by accident — telegraph it.

### The Calm-Down *(the ending the fight is designed to reach)*

While a cub is **present and unharmed**, any PC can use their action to attempt calm instead of harm — lowering weapons, backing off the scent-line, pushing the cub forward, voice and body. **Instinct or Presence, Difficulty 14** (Fletcher rolls with advantage — this is his lane; a prepared calming draught from Leavy grants advantage to anyone — granting advantage over shifting Difficulty is the SRD's own preference).

Run it as a countdown: **three successes before three failures** ends the fight — the storm stands down and becomes, visibly, a mother. **Any damage dealt to her while the countdown runs adds one failure.** Three failures: she rejects the parley, Wing-Bursts the field, and presses on; the countdown can restart only in a new scene. *(Legal: a dynamic Progress/Consequence countdown — SRD countdown rules verified in [[STATUS]] SRD Facts.)*

### If they fight her for real

- **Driving her off:** when damage marks her Severe threshold, she withdraws — she will not die for nothing while her cubs live; survival serves the search. **She returns two nights later**, angrier, at the next arena down the list.
- **Killing her** requires pursuing and finishing her after she breaks — the table must *choose* it, on purpose, in cold blood. If they do: Brin receives the report with a long silence — *"The arithmetic held. Doesn't make the sum good."* — the saved cub is the party's responsibility forever, Marcel keeps his pet, and Front 9 closes the way most things in this Mire close: quietly, and worse.

## The Decision Tree *(clean branches — couple this to [[marcel-dray#The Table — the Marcel Decision Tree (GM reference)|Marcel's table]])*

1. **The half-reunion (most likely first outcome).** They produce cub #1 (Brin hands it over gladly), the Calm-Down lands, the storm ends. **Then the flip:** she doesn't leave. She circles the city at a distance, and the saved cub cries toward the Pale Room at night — *she can smell the second one.* Peace bought time, not resolution. The road to actually finishing this runs through Marcel — and the party is now holding the exact card his decision tree prices: **the brokerage, offered before she comes for the casino.**
2. **The full reunion.** Marcel's cub, brokered ([[marcel-dray#The Table — the Marcel Decision Tree (GM reference)|Branch 3]] — he's put out, he pays anyway, arithmetic beats fondness), both cubs delivered, and she goes home southwest with them. Front 9 closes warm — the campaign's thematic rhyme, paid in full, and the table won't know yet that they just rehearsed the ending.
3. **Driven off, unresolved.** She comes back on a clock, each arena worse than the last, and every splintered boardwalk plank is a page in Caius's file (Front 6 ticks).
4. **The kill.** Above. Legitimate, mournable, and permanent.
5. **Nobody acts.** She arrives at the worst arena on her own schedule, and the session becomes disaster management: civilians to shield (Tenson's lane — the Stalwart between the storm and the crowd, the *guardian* work he swears he's done with), Marcel's people making it worse, Caius's aide taking notes.

## Party jobs *(so the storm belongs to everyone)*

- **Fletcher** — the read (free clue above), the tracking that turns "arrival" into "interception," advantage on the Calm-Down. This crisis is entirely about his choices and not at all about his snake — let him be needed for love before he's needed for revenge ([[fletcher-arc]]).
- **Tenson** — the wall. Bodies behind him, talons in front. The arc-relevant sting: the night is won by the exact guardian work he's trying to retire from — and nobody else on the field can do it.
- **Leavy** — the calming draught (a prep-scene brew if they heeded the tells — dice-forward, [[leavy-arc]] set-piece skeleton), and the field triage when the Keening and the talons stack Stress across the party.
- **Isotta** — one pointed line, no roll: *"There's no ghost here. All this grief, and every heart on this field is still beating."* [beat] *"I didn't know it got louder than the dead."* Then let her fight or shield — the living are allowed to need her too.
