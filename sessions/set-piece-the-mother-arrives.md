# Set Piece — The Mother Arrives

*The payoff of [[world-in-motion|Front 9]] and the combat the party built for themselves in Session 3. **Stats, features, and the decision tree live in [[the-mother-owlbear]]** — this file is how you perform it: the tells, the arena reads, the roll ladder, and a Fear menu written as moments, not rules. Designed dice-forward (4+ real rolls beyond attacks) and fight-optional by design — the way to end this fight was never on a character sheet. Every key clue fires **twice: shown once, said aloud once.***

---

## Where it slots

- **S4** — tell 3 fires via the cub button (already in [[session-04-run-sheet]]; secret #10). **As played: better.** Leavy's Nature's Tongue got it from the cub directly — now named **Whimsy** — mother confirmed coming, plus a sister cub and the tied-up girl ([[camille-babineaux|Camille]]). The party *knows*. Treat the prep window as open from S5; compress the tell ladder freely — tell 2 now plays as confirmation with teeth, not news.
- **S5** — tell 2 lands at the First Celebration (trappers' tales travel fastest at feast tables), and Front 9 ticks after the session.
- **S6 strong start** — tell 4, the near sign, first thing. **She arrives that day.** Don't let her crash the Celebration itself — the morning-after beat is better and crueler: the city sang all night while something grieved its way closer through the dark.
- **If the party acts early** (interception), collapse the ladder — she can arrive any session after S4. She's on her own schedule; the tells just tell them so.
- **If [[set-piece-the-bad-season]] fires before she's resolved** *(canon 2026-07-19)*: Whimsy is gone from the Ranging House — she arrives to an empty pen, turns southwest after the crate, and this file's Calm-Down migrates into **[[set-piece-beyond-the-season]] Phase 5**. Run the tells as written; the arena never happens in town.

## The Tells — read-alouds

### Tell 2 — Trappers' Tales *(S5, the feast tables — let an NPC say it over food)*

An old margin-trapper, three cups in, to anyone at the table:

> "Something big's moving up the southwest channels. Not gar-big. *Big* big. Found two of my kills on the margin this week — and here's the thing, they weren't eaten. Torn open. Laid out. Like something went through their insides looking for a thing that wasn't there... and then just moved on."

**Said-aloud channel (hand it to Fletcher — his lane):** a hunter reads that sign instantly, no roll:

> "Predators don't do that. Nothing kills and doesn't eat — not unless it's not hunting at all. That thing's *searching*. Opening things up to see if what it's looking for is inside."

*(If his player hesitates, Brin says it drier next time they're at the Ranging House: "Kills not eaten means it's not appetite. It's grief. I've seen it before, and I've posted a night watch, and I'd rather not say why in front of the guild.")*

### Tell 4 — The Near Sign *(S6 strong start)*

> Fletcher, you're up before the others — habit — and you're the one who sees it. There's a print in the boardwalk mud. Four toes, talon-tipped, wide as a serving plate. No upland animal has any business this deep in the Mire, and nothing that size has any business being this *quiet* — one print, a smashed crab-rack, and nothing else.
>
> And then the smell hits you. Wet feathers and musk. You know that smell. Last time it was on your hands, you were standing in a nest-boat holding a bleeding cub.
>
> She's here. She was *right here*, in the night, close enough to touch your boat. And she moved on — because what she's looking for isn't with you anymore.

*(The scent that led to the party went cold when the cub moved to the Ranging House. From here the party has hours, not days. What they do with those hours picks the arena.)*

## The Prep Window *(hours, not days — if they use them, reward it)*

- **Fletcher — find her line.** Instinct (tracking), **Difficulty 13**, advantage if he starts from the print. Success: he reads her approach — she'll come up the drainage channels past the net-sheds, and there's a stretch of open water where the party could *meet her* instead of waiting. **This roll is what unlocks Arena 1.** Failure forward: he finds the line but late — she's closer than the plan needs, cut one prep scene.
- **Leavy — the calming draught.** A fast brew if they heed the tells ([[leavy-arc]] skeleton): two rolls, Knowledge then Finesse, **Difficulty 12/13**. Full success = draughts for two PCs (advantage on Calm-Down attempts — granting advantage is the SRD's own preferred lever, [[STATUS]] SRD Facts); mixed = one draught; miss = one draught and a [[tables/leavy-brew-side-effects|side effect]]. Brewing burns prep time — say so out loud and make her choose.
- **Brin — the plan.** She cooperates completely, instantly, with zero sentiment: *"A mother with her cub leaves. A mother without one keeps digging. Give her the cub."* She will hand cub #1 over without being asked twice — it's the arithmetic. What she can't provide is the *second* cub, and she says that plainly too (clue, said aloud): **"There were two cubs on that boat. She can count to two."**
- **The Marcel option — the branch he doesn't see coming.** If anyone thinks of it *now*, before she arrives: brokering cub #2's return is worth a real credit at his table ([[marcel-dray#The Table — the Marcel Decision Tree (GM reference)|Branch 3]]). After an attack it's cleanup, worth a fraction. Don't prompt this — but if a player reaches for it, light up.

## The Three Arenas — openings

### Arena 1 — The Channels *(they intercepted; best case)*

> You picked this spot — open water, the drainage channel widening out, dawn mist coming off it in sheets. You hear her before you see her. Not a roar. A low sound, way down under hearing, the kind you feel in the boat's hull.
>
> Then the mist ahead of you *moves* — and she comes through it like a ship. She's the size of a fishing boat stood on its stern. Feathers like wet slate, talons dragging furrows in the shallows. And here's the thing you can't stop looking at: she isn't looking at any of you. Her beak's in the air, turning, side to side.
>
> She's not hunting. She's *listening* for something.

*Toys: open water and room to maneuver; the party's boat (and everything on it); the mist — visibility is short, which cuts both ways; nothing behind them but Mire. Most control, fewest bodies.*

### Arena 2 — The Ranging House *(default — she follows the strongest scent)*

> It starts with the animals. Every specimen cage in the Ranging House goes off at once — every bird, every crawling thing, all of them screaming in the same second, like somebody threw a switch. Brin comes out of the map room already moving. She doesn't ask what's wrong. She looks at you and says one word:
>
> "*South wall.*"
>
> And then the south wall comes to you. Timber folds in like wet paper, and through it — talons first, then the beak, then the rest of her, too big for the room, dragging half the wall with her. The keening starts. It hums in the floor. In your teeth. Somewhere under it, upstairs, the cub starts crying back.

*Toys: net-rigs overhead (a Finesse 13 rig-drop buys one clear round — she stops to tear free); specimen cages (they open if things go wrong — see the Fear menu); the map-room choke point (one door, defensible, and everything Brin can't replace inside it); Brin herself — competent, armed, and completely committed to the reunion plan. Cub #1 is HERE. So is the fastest ending.*

### Arena 3 — The Boardwalks *(nobody acted; worst case — manage it, don't win it)*

> The Mooring bell starts ringing and doesn't stop. By the time you're out the door there are people running past you the wrong way, and over the rooflines you can see planks going up into the air like a giant walking through a woodpile — because that's what's happening.
>
> She's cutting a line straight through the boardwalks. Not chasing anyone. Not eating anyone. Walking *through* the city like it's reeds, head up, beak turning — following a scent to the one lit building at the end of her line. The Pale Room. And Marcel's people have started shooting at her from the casino windows, which is making everything worse.

*Toys: crowds to shield (Tenson's lane — see party jobs); collapsing plank sections; Marcel's enforcers making it worse every round; [[caius-maren|Caius]]'s aide at the edge of the scene, taking notes — every splintered board is a page in his file (Front 6 ticks). The party's job here is triage: pull civilians, stop the shooting, get a cub in front of her. This version wrecks the brokerage credit — say nothing; let Marcel price it later.*

## Running Her — the fight itself

Stats: [[the-mother-owlbear]] (Tier 2 Solo, Difficulty 14, thresholds 10/20, HP 9). Two rules govern every round:

1. **Lead with the want.** She attacks what stands between her and the scent — never the nearest target, never the biggest threat. Retreat *away* from a cub-scent and she ignores you completely.
2. **Never let a cub be harmed by accident.** Telegraph everything near a cub twice. If a cub is harmed in her presence, the Calm-Down locks forever and she fights to the death — that outcome must be *chosen*, never stumbled into.

### The clue that ends the fight *(fires twice, early)*

- **Shown:** round one or two, on her spotlight, she *doesn't* press a downed or retreating PC — she steps over them, toward the scent.
- **Said aloud (Fletcher, free, no roll — his whole skill set):** *"She's not hunting us. Look at her line — she's walking a scent. We're just standing on it."*
- *(NPC fallback if his player doesn't bite: Brin, from the map-room door: "Stop fighting the animal and look at where it's GOING.")*

### The Keening *(her passive, performed)*

First time each PC comes within Close range, her grief lands somewhere old — that PC marks a Stress. Don't just say "mark a Stress." Say where it lands:

> The sound gets inside you, and for half a second it isn't her grief, it's yours — *(pick the PC's own loss: the person Fletcher was sailing toward; the name in Isotta's docket; whoever's waiting at the end of Tenson's assignment; what Leavy's body used to be)* — mark a Stress.

### The Fear Menu — hers *(spend in bursts; aim to spend 4–6 across the scene)*

Every player Fear-roll banks you one — in a Tier 2 solo fight they'll come fast. Spend them as *moments*:

- **1 Fear — Wing-Burst** *(her Fear feature — she clears a path, never kills)*: "She rears up and her wings go out — for one second the whole field is in shadow — and then the air hits like a door. Everyone within Close: you're airborne, you land at Far range, mark a Stress. And she's already moving. Not at you. *Past* you."
- **1 Fear — Relentless spotlight** *(extra action, up to 3/GM turn)*: "Before the dust is even down she moves again — the map table goes through the wall / the boat spins like a leaf / a plank section just *stops existing*."
- **1 Fear — the grief brushes the tether** *(Sallowreach flavor, [[gm-table-pack/panic-button|Fear menu]])*: her keening peaks and one PC — pick whoever's loss is closest to the surface tonight — gets one clear image of the person *they* can't get back to. Mark a Stress. No explanation. *(This is the fight teaching the campaign's theme: they are all grieving mothers here.)*
- **2 Fear — arena hard move**, once per fight:
  - *Channels:* her wake swamps the boat — everyone aboard, Agility **Reaction 13** or into the water with the gar-smell of blood spreading.
  - *Ranging House:* the specimen cages tear open — one panicked creature from the [[sallowreach-roster|roster]] joins the field, hostile to everyone.
  - *Boardwalks:* a plank section drops a screaming civilian into the channel — someone's action, right now, or the scene gets a body.

**What you never spend Fear on:** killing. Every spend moves her *toward the scent* or raises the cost of standing on it. The moment the fight feels like a monster grinding down HP bars, re-read her want.

### The Calm-Down — the ending the fight is built to reach

While a cub is **present and unharmed**, any PC can spend their action on calm instead of harm — weapons down, body off the scent-line, the cub pushed forward, voice low. **Instinct or Presence, Difficulty 14.** Fletcher rolls with advantage (his lane); a calming draught grants advantage to anyone.

Run it as a **linked countdown, Progress 3 / Consequence 3** *(standard countdowns tick per action roll — SRD-verified 2026-07-06)*: successes tick Progress, failures tick Consequence, and **any damage dealt to her while it runs ticks Consequence**. Say that last rule out loud the moment the first PC tries calm — the whole party has to *choose* to lower their weapons, together, while a Tier 2 solo stands over them. That's the scene.

Perform the ticks:

- **Progress 1:** "She stops mid-swing. Head snaps around. For the first time all night, she's looking *at* someone instead of through them."
- **Progress 2:** "She's gone still. Chest heaving. The keening drops so low it's almost gone — she's listening."
- **Progress 3 — the reunion:**

> She goes down. This mountain of talons and slate — she folds down onto her forelegs in front of the cub, and the sound she makes at it is nothing like the keening. It's small. It's the smallest sound you have ever heard something that big make.
>
> And the cub answers it.
>
> You've just watched a storm turn back into a mother. Take a breath. Nobody at this table is going to forget tonight.

- **Consequence 3:** she rejects the parley — Wing-Burst for free, and she presses on toward the scent. The countdown can restart only in a new scene, at the next arena down the list.

### If they fight her for real

Per [[the-mother-owlbear#If they fight her for real|the stat file]]: at Severe she withdraws — she won't die for nothing while her cubs live — and **returns two nights later, angrier, one arena worse.** Killing her must be pursued and chosen in cold blood, and it closes Front 9 the way most things in this Mire close: quietly, and worse.

## The Flip — she doesn't leave *(the half-reunion; most likely outcome)*

They gave her cub #1. The storm stood down. Give them the win, the whole scene, Brin's dry exhale, all of it. **Then, that evening or the next:**

> From the boardwalks at dusk you can see her out there. A big slow shape moving against the reeds — around the city. And around again. She's got the cub with her; you can see it riding between her shoulders. She's not leaving.
>
> Fletcher — you know exactly what that is. That's an animal walking a scent-line it can't finish. She found one. **She can smell the other one.**

**Said-aloud channel — Brin, plainly:** "There were two cubs on that boat. You gave her one. She can count to two, loves. Everything between her and the Pale Room is just... waiting to be walked through."

Peace bought time, not resolution — and the party is now holding the exact card [[marcel-dray#The Table — the Marcel Decision Tree (GM reference)|Marcel's table]] prices: **the brokerage, offered before she comes for the casino.** The fight hands them a negotiation; the negotiation is Branch 3. Let them discover they'd rather talk to the crime boss than fight the mother again.

## Clue Ledger

| Secret | Shown | Said aloud |
|---|---|---|
| She's searching, not hunting | kills not eaten (tell 2); steps over retreating PCs | Fletcher: "she's walking a scent" / Brin fallback |
| The way to end it is a cub, not a weapon | her whole behavior; the Calm-Down existing | Brin: "Give her the cub" |
| There's a second scent — the Pale Room | dusk circling; the saved cub crying toward the city | Brin: "She can count to two" |
| Damage poisons the parley | first Consequence tick on a hit | GM states the rule when calm is first attempted |

**Held back on purpose:** the thematic rhyme — a vast grieving parent reaching toward stolen young, kept away by human greed; the [[Mangrove Spirit]]'s whole story at fur-and-talon scale. The table calms it with reunion and never hears the word "rehearsal." GM's ear only.

## GM quick-reference

- **Rolls:** tracking (Instinct 13, Fletcher adv.) → brew ×2 (Knowledge 12 / Finesse 13) → rig-drop or arena stunts (Finesse/Agility 13) → Calm-Down (Instinct/Presence 14, countdown 3/3) → arena reactions (13). Attacks vs. her Difficulty 14. House benchmarks legal per SRD (5–30 ladder; prefer granting advantage over shifting Difficulty).
- **Fear economy:** bank on every player Fear-roll; spend 4–6 in bursts — Wing-Burst, Relentless spotlights, the tether-grief beat, ONE arena hard move. Never spend to kill.
- **Stress economy:** Keening 1/PC at first Close approach; Wing-Burst 1; tether beat 1. Watch Leavy's triage load — that's her second job tonight.
- **Party jobs:** Fletcher = the read (free), the track, Calm-Down advantage — needed for love before he's needed for revenge ([[fletcher-arc]]). Tenson = the wall between talons and bodies — the exact guardian work he swears he's done with, and no one else can do it. Leavy = draughts before, triage during. Isotta = one pointed line, no roll: *"There's no ghost here. All this grief, and every heart on this field is still beating."* — then let her fight or shield; the living are allowed to need her too.
- **Guardrails:** lead with the want, every round · telegraph twice near cubs — Nothing Left to Lose is chosen, never accidental · she never presses a retreat away from the scent · Marcel learns everything afterward, always.
- **Threads opened:** the Marcel brokerage (Branch 3 — his credit priced by *when* they offered) · cub custody long-term · Front 6 ticks if the boardwalks burned · Front 9 to "half-resolved: circling" or closed warm.
