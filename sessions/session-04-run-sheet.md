## 🧭 The Theme *(say nothing, feel everything)*

Every scene tonight is about **what's true on paper.**

- **Caspar** wants a lie written so well it becomes true — *"Imperial paper doesn't get argued with. It gets repeated."*
- **Caius** wants four signatures on a truth steered until it points where he needs it.
- **Oswin** (if reached) begs *"say what's true"* over books that don't.
- **The salt wall** is the one thing in this city that reads no paper and takes no bribes — and it makes an exception for Isotta anyway.

Don't announce this. Just know it. When in doubt tonight, aim any improvised beat at the same question: *what does the record say, and who does it serve?*

### 0a · The Note *(cold open — before breakfast)*

Whoever steps out of Remy's first finds it. Hand over the prop; let them argue over Remy's cooking. Full GM notes in [[session-04-caspar-note]] — the load-bearing bits:

- **"Ink and one small, respectable lie"** should make *Tenson* more nervous than a threat would. Watch his face; say nothing.
- **If they show Remy:** he IDs the hand instantly — *"That's Caspar's lettering."* Fine. The note's job is the meeting, not the mystery.
- **Closing bell = tonight.** That's the session's soft clock — the day is theirs; the evening is spoken for. (If they ignore it: he waits two nights, then Plan A, alone, badly — [[session-03-caspar-encounter#If it goes sideways]].)
- **"Do not tell the healer"** — if they tell Delphine anyway, she's quiet a beat: *"Caspar doesn't frighten easy. He frightens* accurately.*"* One line, plants that his fear is real, move on.
- **🧦 The Chausson button (60 seconds, mid-argument):** while they argue over the ominous note, Tenson (or whoever wears socks) finds one gone — a **smooth blue river-pebble** in the boot where it lived. Remy gasps, genuinely moved: *"He took to you already! Oh, that's* rare. *You're properly in."* **No roll — hand a Hope + a grin** ([[chausson]], S3 mechanic). If anyone moves to burn the note per its instructions, Remy, scandalized: *"Not in my house — Chausson hates smoke."* The joke is the reverence; keep him light, never deep.

### 4 · Closing Bell *(evening centerpiece — [[session-03-caspar-encounter]], invited path)*

All four, one door, one polite knock. Run **The Closing-Bell Meeting → Beat 5 → Beat 6** as written. Slotting notes:

- **The knock is the password and the ceremony** — he waits for it on an open door. Let the table laugh; it's the last laugh before the floor drops.
- **Leavy's job (new tonight):** the canal-door hedge-ward is guttering, half-cast. Her read, free, no roll: *self-taught work — coven-less, exhausted, and cast by someone protecting himself* alone. *One practitioner clocking another.* It buys Caspar a drop of her sympathy before he says a word.
- **Beat 5 is a gusher — pace the three payloads:** the Marcel rumor (*executed twelve years ago — I checked*) · the Tidebreaker papers **into their hands** · the point at Marcel's house. Breathe between each. The Marcel rumor primes Isotta's stage-2 sight-gag for whenever she first sees him — do not spend it tonight.
- **Beat 6 — the Ask.** Say the teeth out loud before anyone agrees: perjury over Tenson's seal · the report *feeds Caius's file* · Marcel finding out is a front, not a footnote. **Bargain, never yes/no** (house rule): the report's wording, the timing, the archive, the counter-offer they may find themselves — *"stay, help us take the closet."*
- **⚠ The collision to relish:** Tenson currently holds Caius's report he's redlining *for accuracy* — and is now being asked to author a *false* one. Same seal, same office, same week. **Do not resolve this for him.** If the player names it, that's the whole session working; go quiet and let the table have it.
- **Whatever they choose:** *the archive stays in Sallowreach* (guardrail — the old-words transcription never leaves on a barge) · **hold the redline meeting for S5** — walking into Caius's parlor an hour after this scene flattens both; if they insist on going tonight, run [[session-04-redline-meeting]] straight but know Caius receives *only* the redlines — Caspar's report isn't written yet, and shouldn't be rushed.

## 😨 Fear Tonight *(spend where it breathes)*

- **Showcase the economy (new-to-DH fix):** move a visible token EVERY time a PC rolls with Fear, and say *"I'm spending a Fear"* out loud before each spend below. Players can't dread a currency they can't see. Legal spends (SRD-verified, [[STATUS]]): interrupt/extra GM move · adversary or environment Fear feature · add an adversary Experience.
- **Spend one early and small** (first hour, any Fear roll: the heron turns its head to *watch the party* leave Remy's — nothing else happens). Teaches "Fear = the world moves out of turn" cheaply, so the wall and the bell land on a primed table.
- **The wall:** the pull (Beat 5) — her outline flickers, the Slipping clock debuts.
- **Closing bell:** someone crowds Caspar → **the Warden takes one step** (Presence 14 to talk him back). One step is scarier than a fight.
- **Anywhere:** the [[panic-button]] menu is live; Gault's parting line already bought you table-dread for free.
- **Banked, not spent:** Marcel's verification visit · Caius's *"odd, isn't it"* — those are S5+ dials, listed in the encounter doc.
