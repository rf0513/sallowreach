# Set Piece — You Give Way *(Leavy at the Kept Root, the day before)*

*Leavy's stop on the S8 town day ([[session-08-run-sheet]] Slot 2d), **on her way to Orna's** — before Fletcher arrives with the journal. She needs marsh-lichen for her Glow-Eye Elixir, and it grows on the oldest root in the city. Built 2026-09-29. **2 rolls. ~8 min.** Room canon: [[the-kept-root]]. Voice: [[voice-cards]] (Prosper Doucet).*

---

## 🧭 In Plain Words

Leavy, the party's brewer, needs a crusty plant called lichen for a night-vision potion. It grows on a huge old tree root that pokes up through the boardwalk in a little plaza — the city's shrine, where people leave small gifts. The old man who sweeps the plaza is on his knees fighting a stuck board. If she helps him, she ends up face-down on the deck and sees that the root has been slowly moving for two hundred years — always in the same direction, toward the middle of town — and he tells her so, like it's a boring carpentry fact. Then she has to get the lichen the polite way. You can't ask for things at this shrine, and you can't take them. You leave the first of something you made, and then the lichen is *given* to you.

> So there's this little plaza where two boardwalks meet, maybe thirty feet across. Open air, black water underneath.
>
> Right in the middle there's a hole cut in the deck, and a huge black root comes up through it. Big as an ox. It's polished shiny about waist-high, where people touch it.
>
> Around the hole there's a ring of pale, scrubbed boards with little gifts on them — a heel of bread, a cup of rum, a button. One crawfish, which some kid has arranged very carefully.

>
> Down by the root, an old man's on his knees with a pry bar. One of the pale boards is jammed tight against the root, like it's been shoving on it. He heaves. It doesn't budge.
>
> He sits back and wipes his face. "Getting her a fresh ring for tomorrow." *[beat]* "She's being difficult."

*(Locals call the root "her" and can't tell you why. That's **Prosper Doucet** — sixty years a carpenter, talks in loads and weights.)*

## 🪵 Beat 1 — The Stuck Board *(the clue — only if she helps)*

**Your rule: if she doesn't offer to help, the clue doesn't fire.** Don't nudge. The lichen still works without it.

🎲 **Strength 11** — levering the board while he trims it. Either way it comes free. **Then she's down on her knees, and it's right under her:**

> Down here, you can see the old cuts in the deck.
>
> There's the ring you just pulled up. Under it, an older ring, scrubbed pale. And under *that*, an even older one.
>
> Each one's shifted over. Same direction. About a hand's width.

**Prosper, sitting back on his heels** *(his canon speech — to him it's a carpentry fact)*:

> "Quarter-inch a year, toward the middle of town. Forty years of my own marks say so." *[beat]* "You don't lay a stringer in front of something that's walking."
>
> *[beat]*
>
> "You give way."

And he's back to work.

🚫 **What NOT to say:** what's in the middle of town · why it moves · that it's alive. If she asks why, he shrugs: *"Wood does what wood does."*

## 🌿 Beat 2 — The Lichen *(the etiquette puzzle)*

**The shrine's rules** *(canon — Prosper will teach any of them if asked)*:
1. **Never ask for anything.** Guests don't get handed chores.
2. **Never name, never count.**
3. **Leave firsts** — the first coin of a new job, the first cup of a new batch.
4. **Don't watch it go.** Set it down and walk away.
5. **Anyone hungry may take.**

**So she can't just scrape it off.** Here's how each move plays:

- **She scrapes it, or asks for it** → Prosper, not unkind:
  > "Took?" *[beat]* "Nothing gets took here. Things get received." *[beat]* "Mind your words on this deck."
- **She asks the root, or asks Prosper what to do** → *"You don't ask her for things. She's a guest, not a winch."*
- ⭐ **She leaves a first** — the first of anything she made herself, set down on the pale boards, and she walks away without watching → Prosper, sweeping, scrapes a patch off with his thumbnail and holds it out:
  > "Root had some to spare."

  🎲 **Then Instinct 11** *(Plant Encyclopedia)* — picking the good patch. See Rolls at a glance.

**Payoff from the docks:** if Leavy gave Prosper the gar's saliva glands this morning, he's warm, and he nudges her toward the answer: *"You gave me my glue."* [beat] *"Root notices that kind of thing."*

## 💡 If They Get Clever

- **She leaves something weird** (a button, a spoon, a drop of oil) → if she *made* it or it's the *first* of something, it counts. Prosper doesn't judge the gift, only the manners.
- **She watches the offering after setting it down** → Prosper clears his throat. Once. She'll get it.
- **She's hungry and takes the bread** → allowed. Rule 5. Nobody says a word.
- **She asks Prosper how long he's worked here** → *"Sixty years in the trade. One job I ever walked off."* [beat] *"Boards needed sweeping. Still do."* (His crack, canon — he won't say more.)
- **She asks where the middle of town is** → he points with the pry bar, vaguely. *"That way. Everything's that way, one way or another."*
