## ▶ 3 · The table — *Tenson leads · 30 min*
- **Back room. One table. A dry man.** Every name, right. Cold handshake. *"What do you need?"*
- 👁️ **Isotta's card — now, to her alone.** *(a closed account)*
- **3a · Caspar:** *"Before the spear. The curator… I'd like to hear it from you."* **Shut up. Let them answer.**
  - **Truth → no roll.** *"Thank you."* Their word is good.
  - **Lie → 🎲 Presence 14** *(mark a Stress or disadvantage)*. Hope: *"I'm sorry for your trouble."* · Fear: filed · **Fail:** *"That's not all of it. That's allowed."* → their word is worth nothing tonight.
  - **Then always:** *"Caspar thought I wanted him dead. I wanted him nervous… And he paid. Every month. Men who pay don't have accidents."*
  - 🔒 Only if they claim the Embassy has it: *"Not yet, it doesn't."*
- **3b · The Deal:** *"Deliver the evidence to his Guild in a way that results in his removal, and the spear is yours."* *(Tenson already has it.)*
  - *Why?* → *"He owes me, he's lied about paying… three weeks ago he started cheating at my tables."*
  - *Why not yourself?* → *"A Guild coat on an imperial wall… Take the coat off him, and he's just a man who owes me money."*
  - *What happens to him?* → *"Whatever collecting takes. I don't decide that in advance."*
- **3c · The cub:** pads out, head on his knee, he feeds her without looking down. **Whimsy's sister.** Fletcher's card: *her mother is coming.* → *"How close?"*
- **3d · The counter:** napkin → *"Whose name goes under it?"* · 🎲 **Presence 14** · snap-back said plainly · **the spear moves when the first term is met.**
- ⚠ **Before the terms close → Beat 4.**
