# Set Piece — The Double Knot

*[[fletcher-arc]] Stage-2 climax. The purest fight of the three big set pieces — [[set-piece-the-mother-arrives|the owlbear]] was grief you calm, [[set-piece-the-tessak-camp|the Tessak]] were arithmetic you argue, **this one you kill.** Hunt as heist: illegible approach, Rung 4 found in the ground, then a fight against a fully mundane horror.*

*GM's mantra, every beat: **flesh, venom, appetite.** No spirit, no curse, nothing to parley with. Pity is permitted; the arithmetic doesn't change.*

## Cold-Open Box

**What happens:** the party sneaks (masked, silent) to a hidden clutch-mound guarded by a two-headed snake that has never lost a fight because it can't be surprised and never repeats a mistake. They find the mound, then fight it — Fletcher's phobia forces a scripted freeze mid-fight, the snake learns their tactics as they use them, and venom is the real clock. Afterward: three quiet scenes, including what to do with the eggs.

| Roll | What | Diff |
|---|---|:--:|
| Read the dead hunters' ground | Instinct/Knowledge | 13 |
| Pole the Knot itself (group) | Agility | 13 |
| **The fight** | — | **Diff 15 / HP 10** |
| Escape Drag Under | Strength/Agility | 15 |

**Countdowns:** drowning (3, ticks per action roll while grappled) · venom (4, ticks per roll once bitten with Fear — Leavy's antivenin ends it instantly).
**Fear economy:** spend 5–7 — Learning Head reads (the star), the shift change, one staged Drag Under, Fletcher's freeze (once), Gault's crew if that front is live.
**Party jobs:** Fletcher = the read, the steer, the freeze, the shot (no solo snake scenes, ever) · Leavy = the draught + the antivenin (the fight's brake) · Isotta = the drag-under hostage exchange + "ledger with no entries" read · Tenson = boat-lane watch; ID's Gault's gear if his crew shows.
**Guardrails:** no sympathy flip, no lore pipe, no onscreen lab · the mound decision is a scene, not a roll · endings stay a menu ([[fletcher-arc#Endgame — a menu, not a script|the five]]).

## Prerequisites — Don't Run It Early

This fight is unwinnable *legibly*. Before the boat points southwest, the party should hold:

- **The antivenin + masking draught** (Leavy's brews). No antivenin = the venom countdown has no brake — say so through Brin, once, before they leave.
- **The read** (Brin's approach maps, the matched castings, what the two dead hunters did wrong).
- **The anchor** (a named PC who holds Fletcher's freeze — practiced, not improvised).

If they sail without pieces, don't block them — let Brin do the math: *"Two hunters better prepared than you are right now went past the Double Knot. I filed their gear."* Then let them choose.

**Gault collision (optional dial):** if the Race is live, his capture crew works the same water tonight — a second front, and the Third Knife choice inside it.

## The Approach — Being Illegible *(~30 min, let it breathe)*

> Remy won't take you past the last marker, and he isn't embarrassed about it. "This is where I turn around. Not because of the snake — because everything that knows this water better than me turns around here. You watch what the Mire does, and you do what it does. And the Mire does not go in there."
>
> The channels past the Double Knot are quiet in the way the Tessak camp was quiet — settled, owned — except nothing surfaces to price you. Nothing surfaces at all. Fletcher: no herons. No gar wakes. No frog-song. The water's not empty. It's *vacated.*

1. **The masking draught goes on** — no roll. The party smears on Leavy's brew and vanishes to scent. *(If someone refused it, note it silently — the Learning Head will not be silent about it.)*
2. **The dead hunters' ground — Instinct/Knowledge 13.** Success: what the lost hunters did wrong is visible — downwind creep, an easy bait line, textbook — and their gear is there.

   > "Their gear is stacked at the foot of a root-arch. Neat. Dry. Coiled rope, oiled bow, tin cup on a flat stone — like they'd just stepped away. Except the moss has grown around the stack. It's been arranged like this for two years. It didn't stack itself, and nothing that stacks gear lives out here — except the thing that watched them long enough to learn what their belongings meant to them."

3. **Fletcher's cold skin** — the phobia's gift, always a true positive: *"Fletcher — your skin goes cold."* He marks a Stress; the party learns which channel *not* to pole down.
4. **The Double Knot itself — group Agility 13**, poling through the root-maze. Failure forward: noise — skip to the mound with the snake already aware.

## The Mound — Rung 4, Discovered in the Ground

> The root-tangle opens into a pocket of still water, and in the middle of it there's a mound. Fresh-built — mud and reed packed up above the waterline, steam coming off it in the dawn cold. Fletcher, you've robbed enough nests to survey them by eye, and your eye has already done the math you don't want done: it's a **clutch mound.** Size and shape for eggs — big ones.
>
> It's warm. It's *tended.* And it means the thing you've hunted for three years did something not even the people who built it knew it could do.
>
> It bred true.

- Fires the secret shown; said by Leavy or Brin-by-echo: *"It's not a specimen anymore. It's a species — unless it isn't, after tonight."*
- **The clutch decision belongs to the table, not this file.** Don't rescue anyone from it, don't rush it, don't decide it *during* the fight — the mound is why the fight happens (it defends the nest ground now). What to do with it is the quiet scene after.

## The Fight

**Arena:** the nest pocket — black water waist- to swimming-deep, root-arches for cover that cuts both ways, the mound at center, one clear lane back to the boat.

**Opening — it's already awake (Two Minds: it can't be surprised):**

> You did everything right. The draught held, the wind held, nobody splashed. And the water beside the mound opens anyway — no lunge, no strike. Two heads come up slow, side by side, like periscopes. One fixes on the boat. One fixes on *you*.
>
> It's been awake the whole time. One of them is always awake. That was the point of it.

**Fletcher's freeze (the arc's load-bearing beat):** first moment within Close range, GM may spend a Fear — he can't act toward it until his anchor holds him with the named voice, his name said plainly. Stage it, don't rush it: someone holds, someone covers.

### Fear Menu *(this fight runs hot — spend 5–7)*

- **1 Fear — The Learning Head reads someone:** "The left head watched you do that the first time. You do it again and it's already *moving* — not dodging. Countering. It learned you." *(Disadvantage on the repeated tactic; say "learned" every time.)*
- **1 Fear — the shift change:** "The heads *trade* — the one that was fighting goes flat and glassy, the one that was watching comes forward. It doesn't get tired. It gets *briefed*."
- **1 Fear — the freeze** (Fletcher, above — once, at the worst moment).
- **2 Fear — Drag Under, staged (the kill move):** "The right head has stopped attacking you. It's been *herding* you — and you realize it half a second before the water explodes: the bite clamps, the body rolls, and the channel swallows them." Target Restrained + Submerged, drowning countdown running. **If it takes Isotta:** she's face to face with a thing built to kill her in a way she can't die. Give her the moment.
- **2 Fear — the Gault variable** (only if that front is live): lure-rig fires early, a hired hand screams from the far channel, the cage-boat's lantern shows through the roots. The snake now has more legible prey to choose from.

### Running It Honest

- **It fights like a product demo:** never surprised, never twice-fooled, kills at its chosen range. The horror is competence, not malice.
- **It defends the mound** — always between the party and the clutch. Three years of pure appetite, and now it has something to lose. *(Don't say this. Let Fletcher read it and choke on it.)*
- **The venom is the clock.** First bite with Fear starts it; antivenin is the brake. No antivenin = fail forward into retreat and a second approach next session.
- **The shot is Fletcher's.** Engineer nothing — when the opening comes, give him all the time he wants. Say nothing after the roll. Let the water go quiet on its own.

## After — Three Quiet Scenes *(pick what the table reaches for)*

- **The carcass.** Understood first, killed without audience, used whole — nothing wasted, per Brin's creed. If towed back: the Tessak surface at the old markers, watch it pass in total silence, then one of them dips its head. Respect from the man-eaters. *(One head-dip. No more.)*
- **The clutch.** The decision above, now unavoidable. Chosen out loud, on purpose.
- **The letter.** Fletcher finally writes home — not "job complete." Slide him paper, or just ask: *"What's the first line?"* End the session on his answer.

**Threads opened:** what 2H-**3** implies about 1 and 2 · Gault's reaction to losing his cargo (Front 10 closes or recuts) · if eggs survived, Front 10 becomes Front 10b, and Brin knows before the party tells her.

## Stat Block — The Season-Eater (2H-3)

**Tier 2 Solo** *(at Tier 3: Difficulty 17, Thresholds 15/28, ATK +4, damage up one die step `[VERIFY]`)*

*One body, two minds. Never surprised, never caught, never read four people who love each other before.*

**Motives & Tactics:** Guard the mound, read the prey, herd targets to deep water, kill at its chosen range, never repeat a mistake.

**Difficulty:** 15 | **Thresholds:** 10/20 | **HP:** 10 | **Stress:** 5
**ATK:** +3 | **Twin Strike:** Melee | 2d8+4 phy *(two heads — on a success may deal 1d8+2 to a second target within Melee)* `[VERIFY]` · **Lashing Coil:** Close | 2d6+3 phy + prone `[VERIFY]`
**Experience:** Reads the Hunter +3

**Features:**
- **Two Minds — Passive:** can't be surprised or ambushed; Hidden gives no advantage against it; spotlight up to 3× per GM turn. `[VERIFY]`
- **The Learning Head — Fear:** 2nd time a PC repeats a tactic, spend a Fear — it's read, countered, that roll made at disadvantage.
- **Drag Under — Action:** mark a Stress, attack within Melee. Success: 2d8+4 phy, target Restrained + Submerged — drowning countdown (3), ticks per action roll until escape (Str/Agi 15, or an ally deals 8+ to the holding head). `[VERIFY]`
- **Two-Headed Venom — Passive:** a bite that succeeds *with Fear* starts an escalating countdown (4), ticking on every roll the poisoned PC makes; zero = dying. `[VERIFY]` **Leavy's antivenin ends it instantly.**
- **Shed — Reaction:** once/scene, when Restrained/netted/grappled, sheds its skin and slips free. *(Why Gault never caged it — and why he needed Fletcher.)*
- **Nothing It Owes — Passive:** immune to parley, fear tactics, spirit-work. Isotta's free first-round read: *"It's empty. Not like the dead are empty — like a ledger with no entries. Nothing ever wrote to it."*
