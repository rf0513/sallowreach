# Player Handouts — The Maintenance House Papers

*Two documents [[oswin-cray|Oswin]] hands over in [[set-piece-the-maintenance-house]] — the correspondence he volunteers and the expense summary Tenson pries loose (both in Beat 4). Give them to Tenson's player physically and let him work. **The anomalies are real and findable — do not flag them.** GM answer keys at the bottom. The puzzle is allowed to take days of table time; it keeps.*

*(Currency note: figures are bare numbers — Daggerheart's handfuls-and-bags don't ledger well, and the ratios are the content, not the denomination.)*

## 🔍 GM Answer Key — Handout 1 *(what an auditor's eye catches)*

The player gets time first; 🎲 **Knowledge, Difficulty 13 `[VERIFY]`** confirms any tell he's circling but shouldn't replace the read.

- **The provision is misapplied.** Provision Nine governs *subventions for capital works* — new construction. The wall is a standing work under a maintenance covenant. Oswin even says so in Petition Two; the "review" that affirmed the classification is one sentence long and cites nothing. Nobody upstream ever checked, because nobody upstream ever sees these — the channel dead-ends at the Liaison's desk.
- **The stamps are impossible.** Petitions crawl through four civic desks for eleven-plus days; every denial returns by direct courier within a day — twice the *same* day. The decision exists before the reading. These were never deliberated; they were *awaited.*
- **The routing is the anomaly.** A cultural liaison holds no fiscal authority anywhere in the Empire. Yet every coin bound for the wall crosses this one desk — someone built this channel, and building it was a choice. *(⚠ **Deliver this one free, no roll** — it's in-world knowledge only the* character *has. The [[tenson-handout|player handout]] calls Caius "the ambassador," so "Cultural Liaison" on a fiscal stamp won't register by itself. Say it plainly as Tenson's own citadel expertise, per the [[tenson-arc]] grand rule. The discovery isn't the signature; it's that the signature shouldn't exist.)*
- **Not one fact is ever disputed.** Five denials, zero contested figures. An office that believed the wall was sound would say so — cheaper and stronger than procedure. An office that denies *only* on procedure is an office that knows the facts are against it and does not care. Oswin himself spots this in Petition Five; he just draws the wrong conclusion (indifference) instead of the right one (intent).
- **Petition Three's chilling detail:** the drowned households' petitions were "separately noted **and filed**." They were — in Caius's incident file. The displacement is being *collected.* (Connects to Solène's notice at the wall, [[session-04-salt-wall]] Beat 1, and hardens into pattern with the Kit Room memo — [[sable-crue|Sable's]] payoff.)

## 🔍 GM Answer Key — Handout 2

- **Labor at zero** — matches the man they watched doing two-man work alone. The "lapse of the standing crew arrangement" is doing a lot of quiet work in that appended note.
- **The research line is the skim.** Appears eighteen months ago; grows; round figures against every other line's ragged ones; "receipts retained on site" (they're in the iron box, fabricated — [[oswin-cray#The Evidence (In His Office)|his file]]). And the man said it out loud on the tour: *he stopped researching in his second year.* If the player connects the line to the laugh, Rung 2 of [[tenson-arc]] cracks — his deduction, never an NPC's.
- **The books balance too well.** Honest years return unspent change (27, 36). The skim years land on 2,400 to the exact copper — twice. Real books never do that; fabricated ones always do. *(This is Secret #5 — deliberately the subtlest tell, held for the player to find with no roll and no fallback. If he finds it, he's earned the whole arc.)*
- **The salt line hides a second theft:** the invoiced grade stayed constant while the delivered grade dropped — Leavy's tongue-and-eye channel on the tour (coarse cheap salt in the new barrels), pocketed difference. Cross-checkable against the half-empty shed.
- **The horizon finding (late, once rungs 1–2 are both cracked):** even Year 1's honest 2,373 was losing ground — the disbursement hasn't changed in fifty years while the Mire's growth has. Restore every stolen coin and grant every petition, and the arithmetic *still* fails on a long enough line. That remainder is the audit's edge and the campaign's door — [[tenson-arc#The Remainder (guardrail — the audit's edge)|the Remainder rule]]. Tenson proves the anomaly's size; he never gets to explain it.

## What is deliberately NOT in these papers

The debt. The Pale Room. Marcel's name. Anything confession-grade — that's the iron box, later. These handouts prove *pretext* (Doc 1) and *diversion* (Doc 2) to a careful reader, and nothing to a careless one. If the player misses everything, the papers keep — and **each rung has its own designated re-asker**, per the clue rules:

- **The pretext** (Doc 1) is re-asked by Solène's denial notice at the wall ([[session-04-salt-wall]]) and hardened by the Kit Room stack ([[sable-crue|Sable's]] payoff) — same hand, different victim, pattern.
- **The skim** (Doc 2) is re-asked by the Pale Room rumor — *"someone connected to the failing ward gambles here and owes badly"* — the whole conclusion said aloud by the city (Sinope's casino thread, canon since S2), with Marcel's copy of the evidence as the confession-grade backstop.

Nothing in the arc is single-channel. If a clue in these papers dies at the table, it is scheduled to come back through a mouth.
