# Session 10 — Recap *(as played)*

**Where it ended:** mid-chant, at the Celebration. The whole city is singing, and Isotta has just seen **the Eleven**: ghosts worn down to smoke, up front in the best spots, mouths perfectly clear, singing *before* the town starts. Cut there. **S11 opens mid-Celebration.**

> **Previously on…** Tenson shook Marcel's cold hand and walked out with a piece of the rope the Empire hanged him with. The cubs went home to their mother. Gault's people came for the frog the second the party landed, and they had Tessak in the water with them. Then the city woke up to flutes, carried its tables into the street, and sang. And Isotta saw who else was singing.

## The Rope (Marcel)

- Marcel's favor for the second cub: **the Oswin job.** Take the evidence of Oswin's skimming to his Guild, get the coat taken off him, and he's just a man who owes Marcel money.
- **Tenson negotiated one term:** no lying and no cheating, in any form. **Marcel was fine with it.** All he wants is for Tenson to do his job.
- They shook. **The rope** is Tenson's receipt: *"Don't lose it. I've only got as much as the Empire tied around my neck."* **The line landed big.**
- **The spear never came up.** Marcel still doesn't know they want it.
- Leavy cashed out the casino chips at the house window.

## The Cubs

- Both cubs went back to the mother. She left with them.
- Fletcher worried about the owlbears' natural habitat.
- ⭐ **Improvised canon:** **the owlbears live near the secret lab that made the snake** (the two-headed creature, imperial cargo 2H-3).

## The Ambush at the Landing

- The party was ambushed as soon as they landed in town: Gault's arrow came back with a **freight tag** on it, then **2 hired rogues and 4 Tessak** came for **the frog, alive.**
- They nearly ran, then fought and won. Leavy rooted enemies with vines.
- **The ringleader knows Gault** and said so plainly: Gault sells live creatures (alchemists, collectors, imperial buyers), **the Tessak work for him**, and he's paying for word of the two-headed thing.
- On him: an **empty jar, labeled in advance** with the frog's number.
- ⭐ **Improvised canon:** Sallowreach has no jail ("crime doesn't happen here"). They handed one rogue to **Sable**, who was uncomfortable and will **hold him for 48 hours.** They need a permanent fix.

## The Celebration *(rushed)*

- Remy at the door, in the shirt they fixed. *"Get your coats."* The city builds itself, tables end to end.
- **One in a Krillion** was a hit.
- **Leavy gave the kids potions:** one calming, four caffeine. Nothing has come of it yet.
- ⭐ **Leavy at the talent show** *(improvised canon: there's a talent show)*: shapeshifted into **Tenson**, and turned **Chip Chip into an owlbear** with a temporary potion from Orna's recipe book (a big roll). The crowd loved it.
- **Isotta's Find fired:** the ghosts are all gone tonight.
- **The chant began.** The party **knows for a fact that it's an incantation.**
- **Isotta tried to write the words down** and rolled badly. No copy yet.
- **The Eleven, first read-aloud.** End of session.
