# Session 8 — Recap (As Played): *Packing*

*The as-played record of Session 8 (2026-09-29). **Canon.** The plan was [[session-08-run-sheet]] (*"Packing"*); this is what landed. The party went off-plan early — **together instead of split** — and ended the night at the door of the Pale Room. The Celebration is still tomorrow.*

---

## Previously, on Sallowreach

The day before the Celebration!

The whole Mooring turned out for your giant gar — and Remy, drunk on glory, had promised pieces of it to half the dock. Fletcher sorted out every promise, and settled a war between three aunties by pulling names out of a hat.

Isotta watched the town's dead walk out of the city ahead of the festival, and stopped one to ask why. Then Isotta, Leavy, and Fletcher went to the Hall of the Mire, pestered the clerk with questions, and pitched in setting up for tomorrow. Tenson took a call from Magistra Silence, then joined them.

In the archives, you found the founders' book: every City Leader for two hundred years signing for *the keys, the seal, and the box* — and the very first one received them from nobody. Adaline told you the old words are a blessing. The box stayed shut.

At a little shrine, an old root runs warm and goes down deeper than anyone's looked. Prosper told you a story about it: she was hurt once, and lost a piece of herself — and her sweetheart made a weapon out of it.

And then you laughed at yourselves for ignoring the casino this whole time — and walked down a dry stair into the Pale Room.

## The Beats (canon)

- **The Gar at the Mooring — *Everybody Wants a Piece*** ([[session-08-run-sheet]] Slot 1). Remy's List handed over. **Fletcher delivered every piece:** the jaw to the apron lady, teeth to the kids, the saliva glands to Prosper, **the eyes to Sinope**, the tail *(recipient — see Carried)*. **The three aunties' cheeks were settled by names in a hat.**
- **The dead leaving.** Isotta saw the town's dead walking out of the city ahead of the Celebration — thickest at the Hall — and **questioned one of them.** It went well.
- **Plans changed.** The party went **together**: Isotta, Leavy, and Fletcher to the Hall of the Mire (the Cultural Center); Tenson stayed to work his reports, then joined them.
- **Tenson & Magistra Silence.** A magic video call. Empire lore offered *(see Improvised Canon — confirm which landed)*.
- **The Hall of the Mire.** Mostly questions to **Florent Guidry**, the clerk; then the party **helped set up the festival.**
- **The founders' book** ([[set-piece-they-give-us-the-night]] Beat 2). Every City Leader signs *"Received of —: the keys, the seal, and the box."* **Page one: received of nobody — a dash.** The first leader: **Reine Sauvage**, first name water-stained. *(The Guest Book was not found.)*
- **Adaline.** Asked about the keys, the seal, and the box. She explained, warmly, that **the words are a blessing.** **She did not open the box; they didn't pry.**
- **The Kept Root** ([[set-piece-you-give-way]], partial). The root is **warm** and **goes down** — Isotta's Knowledge roll: it's the tip of something under the Hall. **Prosper's folk tale:** *the tree was hurt once and lost a piece of itself; its partner made a weapon from it* — the Tidebreaker's origin, told as a legend. Prosper: **it glows during the Celebration**; **the roots go way down.** Leavy left an offering and received **marsh-lichen**.
- **Glow-Eye recipe.** Leavy read Orna's notebook: lichen (got), three drops of something that glows, and **water drawn on lantern night** — so it can't be finished before tomorrow.
- **The Pale Room.** After the second Tidebreaker lore drop, the party cackled about how long they'd been ignoring the casino — and **went.** **Session ended at the rope:** Camille's welcome — *"everything you say here is worth something — so say what you mean to spend."*
