# The Reveals — Read-Aloud Bank

*The campaign's emotional peaks, pre-written in the spoken register — so the biggest moments of the next year arrive in a performing voice, not improvised prose. Each entry: the trigger, the staging, the script, and what NOT to say after. All of it obeys the master rules from [[the-celebration-clue-map]]:*

- ***Recognition, never exposition.** The players do the connecting. These scripts deliver sensation and evidence; they never deliver conclusions.*
- ***No NPC mouth for the truth.** Nobody in town knows it. The only voices allowed to say true things are the water, the spear, and the spirit — and even they don't editorialize.*
- ***The Turn's fingerprint never varies:** glass-still water → the slow underglow → iron and crushed green → the pressure of a vast hand closing. Same four beats, same order, every time. Consistency is the clue.*
- ***Endings stay menus.** Nothing below commits the table to a lever.*

***Deliberately NOT in this file** (already scripted where they live): the cut-that-shows and Caspar's Step-4 gift-reframe → [[the-tidebreaker]] · Vane's recognition entry ("read verbatim, stop talking") → [[the-first-maintainers-journal]] · the Tallykeeper's introduction and grace parley → [[the-tallykeeper]] · the Adaline conversation ("Show me" — the box, the word, her click) → [[set-piece-show-me]] · **the Tree Spirit's own scripts (the descent, first contact, the borrowed-words voice, the ask) → [[set-piece-the-honored-guest]]** · Isotta's and Leavy's personal curse-cut payoffs → stage from their arc files at the table's own moment; they're character scenes, not campaign scenes.*

## 1 · THE CLICK — the second Celebration *(Layer 1's gate; hint #7, now staged)*

**Trigger:** the second *attended* Celebration, and only if a tethered PC is **positioned near open water** at the height of the chant (the [[session-05-first-celebration]] "After" notes track your candidate — the PC who half-noticed the first Turn). If nobody's positioned, the Turn passes buried again; don't force it. The click keeps.

**Staging:** deliver to the positioned PC alone if you can — lean over, drop your voice, make the table watch a private thing happen. Speak slowly. This is the campaign's hinge; spend a full minute on it.

> The chant peaks — and the water in front of you goes still. Not calm. *Still.* Glass. A thousand lanterns sitting on a black mirror.
>
> And your body knows this stillness before you do. You've met it once before.
>
> Then the glow comes up from underneath — slow, deep, like something enormous turning over in its sleep — and the cold spot where your curse sits... *answers it.* It doesn't hurt. That's the terrible part. It answers the way your name answers when somebody calls it.
>
> Iron on your tongue. Crushed green in your nose. And the pressure — soft, patient, everywhere at once — of a vast hand, slowly closing.
>
> The last time you felt this — all four of those things, in exactly this order — you were drowning. Open water, black night, forty miles from this dock, the night the ship went down.
>
> And the entire city is singing.

**Then stop.** Don't repeat it, don't summarize it, don't glance meaningfully at anyone. If the player says some version of *"the thing that took us is... this"* — that's Layer 1 landing; the campaign just changed owners. Let them carry it to the table in their own words.

**What NOT to say:** anything containing "ritual," "binding," "the Celebration did this." Suspicion is the whole payload — the prison stays three layers down. If they corner an NPC about it, every mouth in Sallowreach gives them warmth and a shrug: nobody knows, because nobody knows.

## 2 · Tether Twinges — recurring priming, one-liners *(chain #3 ammo; use between reveals, one per session max)*

*The fingerprint, rationed in fragments. Never near [[fen|Fen's]] busking — her music is mundane and must stay clean.*

- *Crossing charged water at night:* "Half a second — the cold spot in your chest *leans*, the way a compass needle leans. Then it's gone."
- *Near the working's drums (the real ones, at the Celebration):* "The curse doesn't hurt tonight. It's worse than that. It's *listening.*"
- *Celebration eve, anywhere in the city:* "You slept wrong and you can't say why — like a night spent in a house where someone's expected."
- *Deep in the Mire, southwest:* "The pull you always feel toward the place you were going? For one breath, something out here pulls back the *other* way. Then the Mire is just the Mire again."
- *After the click has landed (upgrade the register):* "The water goes still, and this time you know exactly what's about to happen — that's new, and somehow that's worse."

## 3 · The Communing at the Heart *(Layer 4's gate — the love story; mechanics in [[the-mangrove-spirits-heart]])*

**Trigger:** the party reaches the Heart with the spear and communes *(the journey and the night are staged in [[set-piece-the-delivery]])*. The environment file rolls for *how much gets through* — this is the narration for a strong result; on a weak one, cut everything after the base and let them come back.

**The base — the grief** *(everyone present feels this)*:

> The spear hums in whoever's holding it — and the Heart answers. It doesn't speak. There aren't words down here. It *remembers at you.*
>
> Two hundred years, all at once. Reaching. Roots driven down every channel. Growth, year over year over year, always in the same direction. Every flood, every new arm of water, every inch this swamp has ever taken — it was all *one gesture.* An arm, sweeping the dark for something it dropped.
>
> You are standing inside a grief so old it has forgotten that it's grief. It only knows that it is reaching. It has never stopped to ask whether the thing it reaches for is still there — because it cannot afford the answer.
>
> And underneath everything, older than the salt wall, steady as a heartbeat, one certainty:
>
> *Taken. Not lost. Not wandered. **Taken.***

**Add-on — if they hold Vane's sentence or the click** *(the assembly paying out — Risk C)*:

> And here's the thing you can't un-feel: the reaching has a *heading.* Every root, every flood, two hundred years of growth — one compass bearing, held longer than any nation holds anything.
>
> You know that bearing. You've sailed it. It points at Sallowreach.

**Add-on — the tether, recognized** *(if the cut-that-shows fired — [[the-tidebreaker]])*:

> And your tether — the four small threads — hums in sympathy with all of it. Same weave. Same maker's knot. Whatever holds you is a thumbnail sketch of whatever this is.

**What NOT to say:** "prisoner," "Celebration," "the city did this." The spirit knows its companion was taken; it does *not* know by whom, or how, or that the taking is still being sung. That's Layer 5, and it needs the chants.

## 4 · The Reading — the chants translated *(Layer 5's gate; Caspar's "ask it to read my fairytales back to me")*

**Trigger:** the party brings the chant transcription (the physical prop: [[old-words-handout]] — the translated lines below match its GM lexicon word for word) to the Heart and offers it through the spear. This is the moment the campaign's prettiest thing becomes its worst thing. Read the translated lines slowly, flat, one at a time — the horror is in the flatness.

> Someone holds up Caspar's transcription — the old words, the ones every family sings and no family can read — and the spear goes cold in your hands. Cold like a key in winter.
>
> And the old words come back through it. Not sung. *Translated.* One line at a time, in something that is almost a voice:
>
> *"Stay, honored guest."* — That's the welcome verse. The one the children learn first, the one they sang at your first feast.
>
> *"Stay. Remain. Hold."* — That's the second movement. The one nobody is allowed to vary. Eight bars, two hundred years, not one note moved.
>
> *"We keep what keeps us. We hold the great presence in the heart of our home — and it shall not pass out from among us."*
>
> [stop. count three.]
>
> *It shall not pass out from among us.* The prettiest line in the festival. Sixty thousand people, every week, hands over their hearts.
>
> It was never a welcome. It's a lock — sung in a language nobody kept, by the kindest people any of you have ever met.

**Then let the silence run.** The spirit adds nothing — no plea, no demand, no target. What the party does with the worst sentence in the campaign is the campaign.

**What NOT to say:** anything that assigns the city blame or innocence, and *any* mention of a lever. When they ask "so what do we do" — and they will — the answer lives in [[resolution-options]] and in them, not in the spirit's mouth.

## 5 · The Compass Turns Inward *(the coda — plays whenever they next enter the city; canon: [[the-tidebreaker]] spear-compass-home)*

> All the way back, the spear pulled southwest — toward the Heart, the way it's pulled since the day you won it. You got used to it. It was almost company.
>
> Then you step inside the walls, and it changes its mind.
>
> It pulls *in.* Past the market. Past the feast tables. Toward the one building at the center of everything, the one every street in Sallowreach eventually faces — the Hall of the Mire.
>
> It pulls the way a key pulls toward a door it was cut for.
>
> And somewhere behind you, out on the boards, somebody's tuning a drum for next week's Celebration.

*(Now they know everything: what's trapped, where, by what — and that freeing it frees them too. The levers are open. When they follow the pull — down, past the pilings, into the deepest water in the walls — the meeting itself is [[set-piece-the-honored-guest]]. From here, the file serves the table.)*
