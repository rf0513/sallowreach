# Session 13 — Run Sheet: *On Credit*

*Prepped 2026-11-15. Online table. Everything you say, in order. You don't need to open any other file.*

## 🧭 In Plain Words

The session opens at sundown: the kids who've been hyper all day crash, and one falls asleep against Isotta and slides right through her. Then the party goes back to the underground casino to get the old fishing spear from the man who runs it. He thinks the spear is junk, and he hands it over tonight on credit. His price: he wants to lend money to the poor families on the flooded edge of town, families who are losing their homes and won't take money from him. They trust Isotta, because she once helped one of those families talk to their dead father. So he asks her to carry his offer to them and tell them it's honest. And it *is* honest. That's the trap: saying yes helps people, and it also puts the poorest part of the city in a crime boss's ledger. That night the heron gives Isotta a missing-ghost case, and the next day she follows his trail to the glowing root in the middle of town, fading a little as she goes. A young ranger asks Fletcher whether she should take a poacher's triple pay. On the second night, the salt wall breaks.

**Tonight's one goal:** the spear is in their hands, and Isotta walks out owing her word.

## 1 · Sundown: The Kids Crash *(the opener — Leavy and Isotta, still on kid duty)*

*Session 12 ended on Day 1, just before sundown, with Leavy and Isotta still babysitting. The caffeine kids have been bouncing off walls since dawn. **Open the session here:** at sundown, they drop. All at once, wherever they're standing.*

> One second they're arguing about whose turn it is to carry the bag.
>
> The next, the smallest one just… sits down. On the boards. Out cold.
>
> Then the next. Then the next. Like candles going out.

- *"Should I get a wagon?"* (somebody's mom, or a passing poler, or the calm accountant kid)

**The slip:** one of them stumbles into Isotta and leans on her to sleep.
> He leans on you. You feel the weight.
>
> And then you don't.
>
> He slides right through you, and lands soft on the boards on your other side. Still asleep.

- **The calm accountant kid,** very quietly: *"I'm writing that down."*
- No explanation. Nobody else in the street saw.
- **Then:** carry them home. The mothers are *grateful* now. Use one or two:
  - *"Look at that. Out like a lamp."* *[takes the kid]* *"…You can wind him up again sometime, cher."*
  - *"He re-tarred the roof AND he's asleep by dark? Witch, you're invited to dinner."*
- Cut to the party heading for the Pale Room.

## 2 · Evening: The Pale Room *(Day 1, after the kids are home)*

*GM only:* Marcel thinks the spear is a pretty bit of junk Caspar paid him with. He doesn't know what it is. He'll hand it over tonight **on credit**, the same way he gave them the cub.

### The door

> The stair down. The cold coming up it.
>
> White tablecloths. Low light. Somebody laughs at the Wake table, and somebody else doesn't.
>
> And Camille's at the bottom of the stairs, hands already out for your coats.
>
> "Welcome back."

**If they ask Camille about the cage** (they've been wondering whether she's here against her will; she isn't):
> "The cold men opened the cage." *[beat]* "One of them gave me his coat." *[beat]* "Nobody said anything the whole way home."

- She never says Marcel's name. Then, lighter: *"I count things now. He pays me to."*
- **If they push** ("are you free to leave?"): *"I came back. Nobody asked me to."*

Then a drink arrives that nobody ordered.
> "Monsieur Dray has time."

### The table

He uses all their names. He offers drinks. He looks at the rope on Tenson.
> "Still on my books." *[beat]* "Good. Take your time." *[beat]* "Don't take too long."

That's all he says about it. **If Tenson explains his progress,** Marcel listens to all of it and nods once: *"Do your job."*

### The spear

When they raise it:
> "That old thing?" *[beat]* "Caspar paid me with it, once. Pretty. Useless. Can't even sell it, nobody here can tell what it's made of." *[beat]* "It's yours. What's it worth to you?"

**Let them try.** His taste, so you can judge offers on the fly:
- **Turns down, warmly:** coin (*"I have coin."*) · promises (*"Everyone promises."*) · moral appeals (*"That's not a price, that's a sermon."*)
- **Says yes to:** something specific, honest, and *interesting*. If a player pitches something good, take it. The loan deal below is the fallback, not a script.

🎲 **Presence 14** to pitch an offer. **Terms decide the deal and the dice only color it.** **Success:** he leans in, interested. **Failure:** he's polite, and the offer just sits there. **With Fear:** someone marks a Stress under his eye.

### His counter *(to Isotta)*

If nothing lands, or after it does, he turns to Isotta.
> "You're the one Solène Thouvenel believed." *[beat]* "About her father. When nobody else would." *[beat]* "That's worth more than anything on this table."

> "The edge is going under. Thornside. Relocation orders, ink running in the rain. Those families need money to move, and the city's not giving them any." *[beat]* "I have money. They won't take it from me." *[beat]* "They'd take it from you."

**The deal, said plainly:**
- **Isotta carries his loan offer to the Thouvenels,** and through them to the row.
- **She tells them it's honest.** It is. Fair interest. Monthly. *"And repayment isn't a matter of opinion."*
- **Whether they sign is theirs.** She owes him the asking, not the signatures.
- **The spear goes home with them tonight, on credit.**

**He corrects misreadings before anyone agrees** (that's who he is):
- *"Do I owe you if they say no?"* → *"No. You owe me the asking. Not the yes."*
- *"So this is charity?"* → *"Charity's for people who can't pay it back. These people can. I'm offering them the dignity of owing me."*

**The right question.** If anyone asks *"What happens if they can't pay?"*:
> "Whatever collecting takes." *[beat]* "I don't decide that in advance."

That's true, plain, and cold. Never sharpen it into a threat or soften it into a promise.

**If they refuse the deal outright:** *"Then it's still a pretty piece of junk, and it's still here."* The door stays open. They can come back.

### The handover *(on a handshake)*

> He gets up. First time you've seen him stand. Goes to the wall.
>
> It's just hanging there, between two paintings. Like decoration.
>
> He lifts it down and hands it over, and the wood's — honestly, it's weird. Amber, then green, then almost black, depending how you hold it.
>
> And his hand's cold where it touched yours.
>
> "Bring it back if you don't do it." *[beat]* "That's all."

- **The spear does NOT cut the tether.** If someone tries tonight, nothing happens. Don't explain it.

### 🚫 If they go in loud

- **Violence is not a fight.** The room's enforcers (**Pale Room Enforcer**, [[sallowreach-roster]]) step in quietly and walk people to the stair. Nobody draws on Marcel twice.
- **If they steal the spear:** Marcel knows within hours. No hit squad. **The door just closes:** no loan deal, Isotta never gets asked, and any future business costs double. Quiet, total, and priced.
- **Rude but not violent:** he stays polite and the spear stays on the table. Rudeness doesn't cost them anything with him. Only cheating does.

## 3 · Night 1: The Heron *(Isotta)*

> It's late. The heron's not on the roofline tonight. It's on your windowsill. Inside.
>
> One foot is standing on your tally sheet.
>
> "Report."

- *"What did you see at the singing."* · *"How many."* (it takes the number like a clerk) · *"What holds them."* (she doesn't know, and neither does it)

**Then it gives her the case:**

## 4 · Day 2: Ferrand's Trail *(Isotta; she sees one thing, someone says one thing, and that points to the next stop)*

| Stop | She sees | Someone says | Next |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · The Mooring bench** | Ghost pipe smoke. Wet bootprints that go **inland**, not out to the water | **Sabine:** *"Ferrand? Stubborn old goat. Said he'd sit the festival out at the Last Board like he always did."* | Last Board |
| **2 · The Last Board stool** | His stool, frost-cold, and a ghost ring where his mug sat. The prints are fainter | **Maud:** *"His stool's been cold since the festival. Nobody'll sit on it."* | The square |
| **3 · A doorway on the square** | His outline, faint as breath on glass. **She walked right past him** at the festival. The prints turn away from the singing | **Isotta, or Remy as a fallback:** *"He wasn't going to the singing. He was going somewhere else."* | Side boardwalk |
| **4 · The Kept Root** | The last print, right by the root. His ghost-pipe on the offerings ring, set down neatly, **still warm.** Then nothing | **Prosper:** *"Morning after the festival, the offerings were all pushed aside. Like somebody made room to sit."* | — |

**What she sees, out loud (one per stop):**
1. **Bench:** *"There's smoke over the bench. Pipe smoke. Nobody's there. And there are wet boot prints, but they don't go to the water. They go into town."*
2. **Stool:** *"The stool by the window has frost on it. In this heat. There's a ring on the bar where a mug used to sit. The prints keep going. They're harder to see now."*
3. **Doorway:** *"There's a shape on this door, like breath on a window. A man, standing. You walked right past this spot during the singing."*
4. **The root:** *"One print. Then a few boards later, another. Then right by the root, the last one. And on the offerings, a pipe. Set down neat. You touch it. It's still warm."*

🎲 Use whatever rolls the players reach for (Instinct to follow faint prints, Presence to get Maud talking), at **13**.

**⚠ Stops 3–4: on a Fear roll, show a slip.** Pick one, never the same twice:
- **Her own print** on the boards next to his. And hers is fainter.
- **Remy** (or whoever's along) turns to say something to her, and talks to the empty air a foot to her left.
- **Her hand on the warm pipe:** for a second she can see the wood of the offerings ring *through her fingers.*

Show it, then move on. Never say why. *(GM only: the singing wears down ghost-touched things, the same thing that happened to Ferrand.)*

- **The pipe** says he went *to* the tree like he was visiting company. It didn't take him. Never explain it.
- **Leavy saw this exact root** in the Mangrove vision: every root in the Mire growing toward it. Let the party notice.

**The report back (that night, or whenever she finds the heron):**
> "Then his line does not close."
>
> It steps off your tally sheet.
>
> There's a wet footprint on it now, right on the number.
>
> And it's gone.

## 5 · Day 2: Coralie *(Fletcher, ~5 min, wherever he is)*

*GM only:* Brin told Fletcher to go talk to her rangers. He never did. So one of them comes to him. **He doesn't know Gault is hiring until this scene.**

**Coralie Pelletier**, one of Brin's rangers. Young, broke, and honest about being tempted. Chews the end of her braid when she's thinking.

> A ranger you half-recognize from Brin's. Young. Mud to the knees.
>
> She falls into step next to you like she's been working up to it.
>
> "You're the one with the frog." *[beat]* "Can I ask you something? Ranger to ranger."

> "There's a man hiring. Triple pay. Southwest, into the deep channels. Said bring nets, not spears." *[beat]* "Said he's short on hands. Somebody broke his last crew." *[beat]* "You'd know. Is that a job I come back from?"

**What Fletcher just learned:** Gault is hiring, he's heading into the Double Knot's water, he wants it *alive*, and he's short-handed because of the party.

**Fletcher's choice is his.** Talk her out of it, tell Brin, ask to go along, or let her take the money and be his eyes on the inside.
- 🎲 **Presence 13** to talk her out of it. **Success:** she doesn't go, and she tells two other rangers not to. **Failure:** she goes anyway. *"Triple, though."*
- **If he asks her to spy:** she says yes too fast. *"What do I look for?"* Now she's in danger, and it's his doing.
