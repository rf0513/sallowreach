## Previously, on Sallowreach *(table-ready "previously on" — read it aloud)*

> Last time: a grey heron stood at the foot of Isotta's bed — and it wasn't there for the fish.
> It led her southwest, to the salt wall. And there Isotta learned something the hard way: in her human shape, the wall let her stand at the line. In her ghost shape — it *threw* her. Two hundred feet. Whatever that wall was built to refuse, it read her, and it refused her.
> Old Anselme was done waiting at that wall. Isotta carried his words to his daughter Solène and proved they were really his — the hardest conversation she's had in this city, and she won it. The family made its peace. The boat sale came off the market. And while Isotta was away, the old man finally crossed — with the heron's help.
> Meanwhile, Leavy sat down with the owlbear cub and gave her a name: **Whimsy.** And Whimsy told her two things. There was a girl on the smugglers' boat — tied up, alive. And her mother is coming.
> At the Ranging House, you walked in on a quiet war: a scarred man named **Gault**, trying to buy Brin's maps and shed skins — and Brin throwing him out of her own guild hall. She has a job for you instead: a rookery count, middle Mire. Your boat's first real commission.
> Leavy came home from the Consortium with the gloamcap distillate in hand — and a debt on the books she hasn't mentioned to Orna yet.
> Sable ambushed Tenson into a street scuffle, grinned through the whole thing, and collected a promise: a real spar, tomorrow at dawn.
> And after dark, you knocked on the museum door. Caspar Renne let you in, heard you say Delphine sent you — and laughed like a man reprieved. Then he said the sentence we ended on:
> ***"I need you to help me fake my death."***
> That's where we open.
