# Session 12 — Run Sheet: *Unfinished Business*

*Prepped 2026-10-25 → 2026-11-02. Online table. Everything you say, in order. Full notes live in the arc files linked at each section. You don't need to open them.*

## 🧭 In Plain Words

The festival is over. Tonight sets up each player's personal quest so the next sessions can finish them. It opens at dawn the next morning, when four angry mothers dump their hyper kids on Leavy and walk away. Over the next two days, each PC gets their own thread. Fletcher goes to Brin: she reads the snake's hunting map with him, and he finishes his bow. Leavy, with the kids tagging along, gathers three weird ingredients for a sweat-lodge trip into their familiar's memories. Isotta gets a visit from the heron, which demands a report on the festival and then hands her a missing-ghost case. Tenson gets squeezed: Magistra Silence calls, angry, with a twelve-day deadline. On the second night, the salt wall breaks.

**Tonight's one goal:** every player leaves with their finish line in sight. That means Fletcher's map and bow, Leavy hearing the name "Seven," Isotta at the end of Ferrand's trail, and Tenson racing a twelve-day clock.

## 2 · Fletcher → Brin's War Table *(Day 1)*

> Brin's back room at the Ranging House is basically one big table.
>
> Her channel map's pinned flat across it. Four old snakeskins down one side, and the big new one rolled up at the end like a carpet.
>
> Gault's traps are in the corner, stacked and tagged.
>
> Brin's got her hat off, which you've never seen. She points at the chair across from her.
>
> "Sit. I've got three questions before I've got any answers."

1. **"How many are you bringing?"** If the answer is anything but four:
   > "It's read every hunter that ever came." *[beat]* "It has never read four people who love each other."
2. **"What do you know about the venom?"** She taps the broken fang from the gar:
   > "Two hunters went after it. Everybody says they drowned. They didn't. The venom took them first." *[beat]* "You need antivenom. And the best reagent in the Mire is in your pack."

   *(She means the pressed skins in his journal.)*
3. **"Now you know somebody made it. Still want it dead?"** Whatever he says:
   > "Pity's allowed." *[beat]* "The arithmetic doesn't change."

**The map.** Share the map image.

> Brin slides her channel map across the table. It's got red pins in it.
>
> "Every skin anybody's ever brought me. Yours, mine, the rookery's." *[beat]* "It came in from upriver. That's the arrow."
>
> *[she sits back]*
>
> "Read it for me."

Then **stop talking.** *(GM only: the pins make a spiral, and its empty eye is the nest.)*

- **If he stalls,** Brin runs her finger along the pins, around and in: *"Animals wander."* [beat] *"This one doesn't."*
- **If someone says "it's a spiral":** *"Nothing in the Mire moves like that."* Then she waits.
- **When he points at the middle:**
  > "Write it down." *[hands him a pencil]* "It's called the Double Knot." *[beat]* "Two hunters went in there. Neither came back."

🎲 **Instinct 13** + *Swamp Specialist* to read the approach. **Success:** *"Both came in from the east. Same way twice. It learned the way."* That gives advantage on the approach later.

**Her checklist,** written in the margin: antivenom · something so it can't smell you · all four of you. Then:
> "After the lanterns. Nobody hunts on Celebration day." *[beat]* "So. Now."

**The bow, at her bench.** 🎲 **Finesse 13** + *Swamp Specialist*. Two boxes left.

| Roll | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Success with Hope / Crit** | It's done. **Water-Strung.** Brin turns it over in her hands: *"Nothing wasted. Good."* |
| **Success with Fear** | One box. He finishes it at the long rest. |
| **Failure with Hope** | No box, but Brin shows him the knot gar sinew wants. Advantage on the next try. |
| **Failure with Fear** | The sinew slips and takes a Gar-Tooth Broadhead with it. He's down to two. |

**🏹 Water-Strung** *(homebrew, `[VERIFY]`; full text in [[fletcher-arc]] §The Bow)*
- **Passive:** shoots wet, swimming, or from a swamped boat, with no penalty.
- **Breach (spend a Hope):** the arrow runs under the water and **comes up anywhere within Far range**. It hits targets that are underwater, behind a hull, or behind cover, **with advantage**, and makes no sound until it surfaces.

**If he shows her Gault's freight tag** (from the S10 arrow): *"That's a shipping tag. He tags what he means to move."* [beat] *"He doesn't do angry. He does accounts. You're on one now."*

## 4 · Leavy → Three Ingredients *(Day 1, kids in tow)*

Orna said to "mind meld" with Seven in a sweat lodge. Leavy wants to find the ingredients in the wild. Full notes: [[leavy-arc]] §The Mind Meld.

**The lead:** Leavy asks the swamp and gets nothing. Then a sticky-faced kid says:

> "You can't eat the blue stuff under the Mooring. Tomas's brother did and he talked to a fish for two days."

1. **Old Plenty's antler.** Leavy already has it. This is the part that lets the trip reach a spirit.
2. **The blue moss** on the Mooring's pilings, just under the waterline. It glows at night, and every parent says "don't." Keep it short and silly, with a roll for the kids.

   > The Mooring at low tide. The pilings are slick and black.
   >
   > And just under the waterline, there it is. Blue. Glowing a little, even in daylight.
   >
   > All four kids lean over the edge at once.
   >
   > "Don't touch it," says one of them, already touching it.

   **The accountant kid,** quietly: *"I'm writing down that we told you not to."*
3. **A salt-root from the wall.** On the swamp side, the mangrove roots grow right up to the salt line and stop. Where a tip touches salt, it goes white and hard. Leavy has to stand at the line and cut one.

> You cut. And the water on the swamp side… leans toward you. Like a dog lifting its head.
>
> The roots shift. Just a little.
>
> You step back. It settles.
>
> You step in. It leans again.

🚫 **No voice and no feelings.** It's weather and tide, nothing more. Let the party connect it on their own: *this* is what the wall keeps out, and it just noticed one of us.

## 6 · Leavy → The Sweat Lodge *(whenever all three ingredients are in)*

Everything is seen through Seven's eyes. The kids wait outside, and the tiny accountant kid takes notes.

> It's hot. Hot like the inside of a mouth.
>
> The antler's in the coals. The blue stuff's smoking. The salt-root's going white.
>
> Seven's on your knee. Not moving. Just watching you.
>
> And the walls start to breathe.

🎲 **About every two scenes, a roll to go deeper** (your pick of trait, 13). **Fear:** a feeling from the old life sticks after waking. **Failure:** Leavy wakes early but keeps the scene they were in.

**1 · The Doors**
> You're small. Black and white. You've got feathers, and you're up on a windowsill.
> Down below there's a gate. A kid walks out of it with a bag over their shoulder. Doesn't look back.
> Then it's later. The leaves are a different color. Another kid. Another bag. Same gate.
> Somebody's waving them off. An old witch. Grey braid, ink all over the fingers. You know that face. You've never seen it.
> They shut the gate, look up at you, and go, "Well. Just us, then."

**2 · The Table**
> A round room. Nine chairs, and a plant carved into the back of every one.
> Eight of them have witches in them, and they're all smiling. They're smiling at the ninth chair.
> The old witch is sitting in it. Carved on the back, deep: a little sprig of rue.
> Somebody pours tea and says, real sweet, "Any luck with a student yet, Rue?"
> And all eight of them wait.

**3 · The One Who Stayed**
> A kitchen, full of smoke. Something in a pot just died horribly.
> There's a kid at the stove. Soot on their face, eyebrows mostly gone.
> The old witch is laughing so hard they have to sit down.
> Next morning, the kid's back. Same apron. They knock twice.
> The old witch opens the door, doesn't say a word, and puts the kettle on.

**4 · The Recipe**
> Same kitchen. Evening, candles.
> One of the witches from the table is here, the one who poured the tea. She's holding out a little folded paper.
> "For your teacher. Tonight. It's tradition."
> The kid takes it like it's made of glass.
> You don't like that paper. You hop down and grab it in your beak, and she flicks you right off the table. Laughs. "Your bird's a thief, dear."

**5 · The Cup**
> Night. The good cups are out.
> The kid holds one out with both hands. They're shaking a little. They're so proud.
> The old witch takes a sip.
> And stops. Just for a second. You see it. Nobody else does.
> Then the cup goes down on the table, gentle as anything. A smile.
> "It's perfect. You're ready."
> And the hand still touching the cup is going grey. Going hard. Going metal.

**6 · The Cart**
> A road, in the rain. You're on a fence post, soaked.
> A cart goes by. There's something under the tarp in the back, shaped like a person but wrong. A metal hand hangs off the edge.
> You follow it, post to post. Days. Weeks.
> And somewhere else, in a big kitchen, the kid is on their knees with a scrub brush. Somebody walks past and says, "Missed a spot."
> Then a voice, old and warm, right in your ear: "Seven."
> Leavy, you open your eyes. The bird's sitting on your chest, looking at you.

**Let the player say "Seven" out loud.** When they do, the bird answers. Somewhere in the last scene, something huge leans in, the same lean as the water at the wall. It's felt, never explained.
