# Session 4 — Caspar's Note *(handout + morning beat)*

*Session 4 opener material — the fourth S4 file, alongside [[session-04-ranging-house]], [[session-04-salt-wall]], and [[session-04-gloamcap-run]]. Print or read the note as a physical prop. **Delivery:** pinned to Remy's front door before dawn — whoever steps outside first finds it, before the heron beat. Whole-party scene before breakfast; let them argue about it over Remy's cooking. It triggers the retriggered Caspar encounter: [[session-03-caspar-encounter#How It Fires Now — the Note]].*

*The handwriting is small, precise, and beautiful — the exact hand you'd letter a museum placard in. No signature.*

---

To the one who knocked:

You knocked once, politely, and left when no one answered. Forgive me, I do not open the door anymore. That is not a figure of speech, I've literalled nailed my door shut.

A little rat told me you dined at the Embassy. I am hoping (_and you should understand I am staking rather a lot on this hope_) that this means you answer to something bigger than this city. Everyone else here answers to something smaller.

I know not why you knocked on my door, but I am hoping we can come to an agreement that will cost you nothing but ink.

Sunset. The canal-side door, not the front. Knock the way you did before: once, politely. That's how I will know it's you.
  
P.S. This note will self-destruct in 45 seconds, please do try and avoid getting burnt up by it.

---

## GM notes

- **The password is the polite knock.** Nobody in Sallowreach knocks politely — that knock is the entire reason Caspar chose Tenson. If the players ask "why us?", that's the answer, and it should feel earned: the whiffed S3 scene was the audition.
- **"Ink and one small, respectable lie"** tips the shape of the ask without the content — it should make Tenson *more* nervous than a threat would.
- **The contradiction is characterization:** he pinned a secret note to the door of the biggest gossip's inn in the city, and then wrote *do not bring the innkeeper*. Caspar is a terrible liar with bad plans — that's canon. If a player points at it, they're paying attention; reward it (it's also a quiet clue that he's an amateur, not a player in Marcel's league).
- **"Do not tell the healer"** — he's guessing at their web (Delphine sent people to him before). Wrong guesses included on purpose.
- **If they show the note to Remy anyway:** Remy recognizes the hand instantly — *"That's Caspar's lettering. Whole museum's written in it."* — which un-mysteries the sender and lets a cautious table prep. That's fine; the note's job is the meeting, not the mystery.
