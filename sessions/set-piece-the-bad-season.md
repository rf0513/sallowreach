# Set Piece — The Bad Season *(the night the détente breaks)*

*The campaign's big heroic battle: the Tessak raid on the town's edge, and the robbery it was cover for. **Stats live here and in the rosters; Gault's plan is canon in [[gault]] §The Bad Season.** Timing: ~S7–8, after [[fletcher-arc]]'s Offer is refused. The party wins the battle; Gault wins the night. The finale this arms: **[[set-piece-beyond-the-season]]**.*

---

## 🧭 In Plain Words *(the whole night — no jargon)*

The swamp is home to a clan of lizardfolk who eat people. For two hundred years an unspoken truce has held: they take only what the water brings them — wrecks, strays, the drowned — and never come up into the city. Gault, the poacher the party keeps crossing, has spent months feeding that clan staged wrecks in exchange for labor. Tonight he calls in everything: he fakes the signs of the biggest wreck the clan has ever been offered, *inside the town's own waterways*. The clan follows the promise of easy meat up into the streets, and the two-hundred-year truce breaks in one night. While every fighter in Sallowreach — the party included — battles man-eaters on the boardwalks, Gault robs the undefended town: he empties the ray basin he's been poaching, takes the hunt-maps he was refused, and steals a living owlbear cub the party rescued and named. The party gets the fight of their lives and wins it in front of the whole city. Then dawn shows them what the fight was *for* — and whose sign the raiders were following.

**Two goals:** the table kicks ass with zero guilt for a whole night — and at dawn, hates one man more than they have ever hated anything.

## 🎯 Gault's Plan *(GM truth — one box)*

Three takes in one night, priced cold: **the basin** (every remaining lantern ray in the brightfin shallows — the score of his career), **the drawer** (Brin's four castings + channel map — the trail-read he was refused: *"There's always a number. Yours is just sentimental."* The raid is the number), and **the pen** (Whimsy, crated — a live owlbear cub is the richest freight his pipeline has ever moved). He is never on the boards. He is never seen. He spends the town's blood and Brin's courage like coin, and by dawn he is three channels southwest.

**His one mistake, and it's the arc's engine:** he priced everything in that room except what the cub is worth to the thing that loves it. **The mother can track her child where no map goes.** The party doesn't need the stolen map. They can follow her.

## ⚙️ The Machine — how four PCs fight a city-wide raid

*The raid is big; the play space isn't. Three stations, two clocks, one rule for helping. That's the whole machine.*

### The Three Stations *(+ the quiet fourth)*

- **The Drowning Edge** — the lowest boards, closest to the water. Solène and the edge-families. Where it starts.
- **The Mooring** — the night berths. Sabine, the boats — the town's escape routes and its lifeline.
- **The Last Board** — Maud's porch at the end of the boards. The refuge point; where the runners bring people.
- *(The quiet fourth: **the Ranging House.** No fighting there. That's the point. Don't mention it until dawn.)*

Put three tokens on the table — the stations are the battle map. The party can't hold all three at once. That's the game.

### The Night's Ledger *(two visible clocks — draw them where players can see)*

A dynamic Progress/Consequence pair *(SRD-verified framework — [[STATUS]] §SRD Facts)*. Every action roll made **against the raid** ticks one:

- **THE TOWN HOLDS — Progress (10).** Crit +3 · success with Hope +2 · success with Fear +1.
- **THE WATER TAKES — Consequence (6).** Failure with Hope +2 · failure with Fear +3 · **+1 whenever a station flare goes unanswered.**

**Win condition is the clan's own arithmetic:** the party doesn't kill sixty Tessak — that's not a session, that's a war. *A predator that takes losses over one catch is doing bad math* ([[sallowreach-roster]], canon). **When THE TOWN HOLDS fills, the raid stops mid-strike** — the whole clan does the math in front of the table, and withdraws. The players beat Gault's arithmetic with better arithmetic. Say it that way at the table.

**Consequence thresholds** *(scripted, not rolled — dials you turn later, not deaths you narrate now)*:
- **At 2:** a span of boards burns or breaks — one station is **Bleeding** (rolls there gain disadvantage until someone spends a beat shoring it).
- **At 4:** **someone is Taken** — a named townsperson dragged toward deep water, alive (predators carry prey; they don't waste it). A rescue window opens (Isotta's lane). If nobody goes, decide their fate *after* the session, never tonight.
- **At 6:** a station **Breaks** — the raid takes its full catch there. Dawn counts the cost.

### The Rally *(the Hope engine — this is what makes it feel city-wide)*

> **When you succeed with Hope during the raid, you may also — free, on top of the normal Hope — send one of the town's named hands to a station you can't reach.** Say who goes. Each name spends once tonight.

- **Sable & Perrin's shield-line** *(the Drillfield)* — Tenson's spar-mate and Tenson's trainee, holding a choke exactly the way Tenson taught the town to.
- **Maud's porch empties** — the Last Board's regulars come off their stools with boat-hooks and bad intentions.
- **Fen's runners** — fast kids relaying warnings and moving elders. They never fight. They *evacuate* — a flare they answer is a flare with nobody left standing on it.
- **Remy on the water** — the one punt moving *toward* the trouble, pulling swimmers out all night.
- **Brin's bow on the roofline** — she's already out there (see the doors). Spending her name just aims her.

**Effect:** the next flare at that station resolves without ticking THE WATER TAKES. Fictional truth: the party's Hope literally becomes the town saving itself. This is the "feel like heroes" mechanic — protect it, perform every rally as a small scene (one line of what the players see across the water).

### The Flares

Between spotlight exchanges, flare a station where the party isn't (a horn, a scream, fire on the water). The party splits, rallies a name, or lets it ride (+1 Consequence). Two or three flares per bell. Never flare faster than the table can breathe.

## 🔔 The Three Bells *(the night's spine)*

### First Bell — The Landing *(the Drowning Edge; teach the machine)*

Cold open, spoken register:

> *"The third bell of night, and the boards are quiet. Then — one scream, from the low edge, cut off in the middle. Then the horn. Then every dog in Sallowreach at once."*

One wave: **4–6 Tessak Raiders + 1 Scout** hitting the lowest boards. Teach the minion rule (Raiders die in satisfying sweeps), the Ledger, and one flare. Solène's family is on these boards — the party defends people they know by name.

**Clue 1 fires here** (see the ledger below): the pilings are dabbed with faint blue-green marks — a harvest-line, painted in ray oil. Fletcher's pattern-read (Instinct, **Diff 13**, his lane): *this isn't a frenzy, it's a route.* The raid is being **steered**.

### Second Bell — The Long Night *(the engine at full speed)*

Two stations flare at once — the Mooring *and* the Last Board. Now the machine plays: split the party or spend the names. Waves mix **Raiders + Divers**; the Scouts work the edges with bolas; **Sound the Channel** brings gar boils up under the boards. The **Taken** beat lands this bell if Consequence hits 4.

**Clue 2 fires here:** a downed young Raider, dying or captured, answers any question the same way — the clan *worked* for the man; they know him. *(Line below in the ledger.)*

### Third Bell — The Turning *(the war-leader; the stand)*

Spend the 3-Fear opener: the war-leader surfaces — Drevath or the splinter's boldest (doors above). The biggest wave of the night, aimed at the station the party has made theirs. This is the set-piece fight: leader + Divers + Raiders, the party planted, every name they saved fighting somewhere behind them.

**When THE TOWN HOLDS fills** — perform the withdrawal:

> *"It stops. Mid-strike — the spear just… lowers. The war-leader looks down the boards: at the shield-line, at the fires that didn't spread, at what tonight has cost against what it's brought in. You watch a predator finish long division. Then the water takes them, all of them, in one breath — and the boards are yours."*

Give the table the cheer. They earned it. First light comes up on the smoke. **Then run The Dawn.**

## ⚔️ Adversaries *(reuse the rosters; one new block)*

- **Tessak Raider — Tier 1 Minion (4)** *(new, statted on the house Lackey/Gar idiom)*
  **Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** None | **HP:** 1 | **Stress:** 1
  **ATK:** +0 | **Bola & Knife:** Melee | 3 phy
  **Features:**
  - **Minion (4) - Passive:** Defeated when they take any damage. For every 4 damage a PC deals, defeat an additional Raider within range the attack would succeed against. *(Let the party mow through the young and hungry — the danger is the numbers and the water.)*
  - **Take, Don't Trade - Passive:** Attacks with advantage against Restrained targets and anyone in the water — but a Raider never presses a set line (braced shields, planted spears, a held choke). It looks for easier meat instead. *(Tenson's wall literally aims the raid.)*
  - **Pack Take - Action:** Spend a Fear to spotlight all Raiders within Close range of one target: one shared attack roll; on a success, 2 physical each, combined, and the target is dragged Close toward open water.
- **Tessak Scout** *(Tier 1 Skulk — [[sallowreach-roster]])* — the bola-and-drag specialists working the rails.
- **Tessak Diver** *(Tier 1 Standard — [[sallowreach-roster]])* — the veterans, second and third bell.
- **The war-leader** — run as a Tessak Diver at **Difficulty 14, HP 8**, Experience *The Delivered Season +2*, plus one leader feature: **The Take Call - Fear:** spend a Fear — every Raider and Scout in the scene converges on one named target or objective.
- **Mire Gar / Old Gar / Bloodmidge Swarm** *(([[mire-bestiary]]))* — arrive via **Sound the Channel**; fire and smoke drive them off (their own reactions — Leavy's lane).

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 The Lanes *(one apiece — seed these, don't force them)*

- **Tenson — The Wall.** Guardian at the choke, Perrin at his shoulder: Sweat First pays off in held ground. Every set line he plants turns off the Raiders' advantage and *steers the raid* — the auditor finally gets to be the thing the town points at and says "there."
- **Fletcher — The Roofline.** No snake tonight. Nothing to fear but losing. Ranged lane, the pattern-read, the harvest-line clue — let the table watch the frog simply *kick ass* for a whole night, clean.
- **Leavy — The Kitchen Holds.** The cauldron as area denial — fire and smoke are the counterspell to every gar boil and swarm on the board (their statted weakness). One Nature's Tongue beat: a gar boil isn't clan — she can becalm or turn one boil (Instinct or Spellcast, **Diff 13**), un-firing **Sound the Channel** in front of the Tessak who spent it. And somewhere mid-bell, Clank wears a shape the town will be telling stories about for years.
- **Isotta — The Black Water.** She cannot drown. When the **Taken** beat fires, she is the only one who can follow. And along the rails, the town's uncrossed dead are standing in a line, watching — only she sees them — and every one of them is *pointing at where someone just went under.* Each pointed finger is a true rescue vector. *(Ghosts are town business and hers alone — [[cosmology]]. Tonight the town's dead help hold the town.)*

## 😨 Fear Menu *(performed — spend 5–8 across the night)*

- **1 — Sound the Channel:** a Scout trills through the water; a gar boil erupts under a station's boards.
- **1 — The Bola Line:** weighted cords out of the dark — the PC who's hurt them most is Restrained against the rail, water at their back.
- **2 — The Flare:** a station erupts where the party isn't. (This is the machine's main verb — see The Flares.)
- **2 — The Taken:** a Diver goes over the rail with someone in its arms — *name them* — and makes for deep water, unhurried. Alive. Predators carry their catch. The window is open.
- **3 — The War-Leader Surfaces:** third bell only, once. The water goes still first. Then the whole channel stands up.
- **Never spend Fear on:** killing the Taken on-screen, gore near the kids, or making the clan cruel. They are not cruel. They are shopping. The horror is the *calm* — and the never-said fact that tonight, for the first time in two hundred years, the shopping came indoors.

## 🌅 The Dawn — Three Cuts *(read in order; spoken register; let each sit)*

**Cut 1 — the basin.**
> *"Remy's at the brightfin basin at first light. He's not moving. The water's clean, no nets, no blood — just empty. He counts. He counts again. You've seen him do this before, at forty-three. This time he doesn't say a number at all."*

**Cut 2 — the Ranging House.**
> *"The door's not broken. It's open. He knew the latch — he stood in this room in front of all of you, once, being told no. The map wall is bare. The specimen drawer is out, and it's empty, and it's been closed again, gently. He tidied up after himself."*

**Cut 3 — the pen.**
> *"The pen's empty. There's no blood, no fur, no splinters — Whimsy went into a crate the way freight goes into a crate, professionally. There's a tag wired to the bars. A number on it. That's all. Brin, who did not cry at the fires, is looking at that tag and — Fletcher, she hands it to you without a word."*

**Then the turn — the reason they can still win** *(Brin, and this is the arc's hinge — deliver it flat, like she's reporting weather)*:
> *"He priced everything in that room except one thing. That cub's mother is somewhere southwest, and she can track her child through water, through dark, through anything he knows how to do. He stole the only cargo in the Mire that comes with something that loves it."* — a beat — *"First mistake I've ever seen him make. We follow her."*

## 🏁 After *(dials, turned post-session — [[world-in-motion]])*

- **Standing:** the whole town watched them hold the boards. This is [[set-piece-show-me]]'s evidence gate cracking open on its own — Adaline saw the shield-line too.
- **No coin beat.** The town pays the way Sallowreach pays: the Homecoming turned on its defenders — food, mended gear, gifts a host can't refuse ([[coin-in-sallowreach]] §The Floor). If someone tries to pay them, that's a person with a *reason* — one beat, max.
- **The clan:** the raid failing IS the proof of *tessavrak* — the elders reassert, and **Sresh's door opens**: the party can end the war at the camp with the night's own count. The Weighing, reprise — this time the party brings the arithmetic.
- **Caius:** man-eaters inside a "pacified administrative zone" is the most useful incident report of his career. Tick Front 6.
- **Front 9 re-aimed:** the mother turns southwest, tracking the crate. She is now the party's compass to the finale — and the fourth force arriving at the Double Knot with no interest in anyone's contract ([[set-piece-beyond-the-season]] Phase 5).
- **Casualties:** your dial. Default: the town holds with wounds and one hard loss *only if* THE WATER TAKES hit a threshold nobody answered — and decide that loss in wrap-up, never at the table at 1am.
- **The binding doesn't care.** No wall beat, no tether beat, no spirit beat tonight — like the snake, this catastrophe is fully mundane: meat, math, and one man's ledger. That's what makes it his.

## 🎯 Win Condition

The table cheered at the withdrawal like it was a touchdown; nobody felt one gram of guilt all night; at least one player said the words "I hate him" out loud at the dawn cuts — and when Brin said *"we follow her,"* somebody was already reaching for the boat.
