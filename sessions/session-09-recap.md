# Session 9 — Recap *(as played)*

**Where it ended:** back in Marcel's high rollers room. Marcel will give them the second owlbear cub — for **a favor from Tenson.** Cliffhanger; the favor is not yet named.

---

## The casino floor

- **Camille** welcomed the party, explained the house rules, and gave everyone **house coins** (black, cold, a fish on one side, blank on the other).
- **Leavy was the high roller of the night.** Won at the Count, then went on a run at **Pins**: **five wins in a row**, the pot ballooning, the whole room watching. No jackpot — she kept playing until she toppled it and lost.
- Leavy kept calling **Tenson her good luck charm.** Tenson hated it.
- **Tenson avoided Oswin** the entire time. Nobody discovered the cheating (the glasses). As far as the party knows, **Oswin just left.**
- The party spent a long time with **Octave** at the bar — a good time, lots of general town questions, but **none of the pointed questions** (nothing about Marcel).
- **Isotta traded Octave a ghost story for one of his — the coven story:** 4–5 years ago, three witches in pond-green cloaks, each with a **three-strand green braided thread on the left wrist**, came to the casino. The youngest pulled pins and never missed while the two older ones held hands behind her. Mid-pull, the eldest grabbed her wrist and **all three ran** up the stair, leaving the pot — the only time Octave has ever seen anyone hurry out of the room. He watched Leavy at Pins and said, *"…Funny. She pulls pins just like that."* The eldest called the youngest by a pet name, "like you'd call a little sister" — Octave would know it if he heard it.

## The high rollers table

- Because of her streak, **Leavy was invited to the high rollers table.** Leavy and Tenson went.
- They played **Liar's Dice** with Marcel. It came down to **Marcel vs. Tenson.** **Marcel won — legitimately** (GM didn't fudge).
- **Marcel never made the Oswin offer.** He had no reason to yet. Instead he **hinted that Oswin had promised him that Tenson, "the auditor," was here to bring him funds** — that it was "all but guaranteed." **Tenson found this fishy.**
- The spear was never discussed.

## The owlbear

- The mother owlbear arrived on the boardwalk. The casino went into lockdown.
- The party went up and **calmed her down** — Isotta used **Animal Whisperer** and offered her **coat, which smelled like Whimsy.**
- They **fetched Whimsy from town** (the Ranging House).
- They came **back down to the casino** and asked Marcel to **sell them the other cub.** The **mother and Whimsy are waiting outside** on the boardwalk — but only for a few minutes.
- **Marcel said yes — in exchange for a favor from Tenson.** Session ended there.
