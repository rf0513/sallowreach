# Set Piece — The Honored Guest *(the Tree Spirit, met at last)*

> 🪧 **Disambiguation:** "The Honored Guest" names three things in this campaign — the *tradition* (how the city treats its spirits), **[[remy|Remy]]'s inn** (named for the tradition, generations ago), and *this file:* the being the tradition is unknowingly about. A link to `set-piece-the-honored-guest` always means the spirit; the inn has no file of its own — it lives in [[remy]]. The echo is intentional and worth savoring at the table: the party has been *sleeping in the answer's name since Session 1.*

> ⚙️ **The other end of the compass** *(built 2026-07-18)* — [[set-piece-the-delivery]] stages the journey *out* to the vast one; this file is the journey *down* to the kept one. It works at any point in the campaign: what the party finds depends on what they carry, not on where the plot is. Endings stay menus ([[resolution-options]]).

## In plain words

Deep under the middle of the city, straight down below the oldest pilings, there is a drowned tree. It is enormous, it is still alive, and it is the spirit the founders trapped two hundred years ago — the "honored guest" every feast in Sallowreach is secretly sung at. Anyone who dives deep enough can find it, at any point in the campaign. Without the magic spear, they find a presence: warm water, faint light, the feeling of being welcomed. With the spear, they can talk to it. After they've met the vast spirit out in the deep swamp, the tree knows the moment they arrive — and asks them, for the first time in two hundred years, for something. It is not a monster and not a tragedy machine. It's a friendly, person-scaled spirit that said yes to staying one night, long ago, and the song never let it take the yes back. It doesn't hate the city. It won't ask anyone to break anything. This file is where it is, how it talks, what it can give, and what it will never say.

## Where It Is *(canon 2026-07-18)*

- **Directly beneath [[the-hall-of-the-mire|the Hall of the Mire]].** The Hall stands on the city's first pilings — and under them the water goes down farther than anywhere else inside the walls. The founders built the first boardwalk over the deepest pool in the Mire. Nobody asks why.
- **Down past the last light: the drowned tree.** Standing upright on the bottom, big as the Hall above it, alive. Leaves moving in a current that isn't there. The grain — amber, deep green, near-black — is **the spear's grain.** Anyone who has held the Tidebreaker knows this wood on sight.
- **Its roots run under the whole city center.** Every offering-root in Sallowreach is this tree, surfacing. The [[the-kept-root|Kept Root]]'s great knee is its farthest fingertip. The offerings that vanish? Received. Some rest on the roots down here; older ones are half-grown into the bark, taken slowly to heart.
- **How the tree came to be underwater:** the spirit's memory is images only — open air, firelight, a feast, a song asking it to stay, its own yes. Then water. Whether the founders sank it or the binding pulled it under is not written anywhere. **Leave it loose.**

|---|---|---|

## Getting Down *(the dive)*

**Difficulty 13.** The pool under the Hall is a root maze in black water. Ways down, per PC:

- **Breath is a loop countdown (4).** Tick it when a diver fails a roll or lingers past a beat. At 0: mark 1 HP and surface, now.
- **[[isotta|Isotta]] doesn't breathe.** She can *walk* down. The deep water — the most charged in the Mire — doesn't reject her the way the salt wall did. The wall refuses; the binding *welcomes.* This is the one place in the campaign where her condition is purely an advantage. Give her the descent alone if she wants it.
- **Ribbit** scouts it free. **Clank** can wear something that swims. **Fletcher and Tenson** hold their breath like mortals — Tenson's armor is a decision, not a roll.
- **Light:** none needed past halfway. The deep *glows* — faint, green-gold, breathing. (GM eyes: this is the underglow of the Turn. The pulse the whole Mire sees on Celebration nights is brightest here. Don't say so. Let someone clock it.)
- **⚠ Timing:** diving on a **Celebration night** means being in the water when the binding performs at full power. See the Fear menu. The spirit knows the schedule better than anyone alive — at Stage 2+, it will warn them in the only words it has.

## Stage 1 — The Presence *(read-aloud, spoken register)*

> You go down past the pilings — and the pilings keep going. Older and older wood. Past the barnacle line. Past the light.
>
> And then the water gets warm.
>
> You're deep enough that it should be cold and black, and it's warm, and there's light coming up from under you. Faint. Green-gold. It's not steady — it swells and fades, slow, like breathing.
>
> And then you see the tree.
>
> Standing. Whole. Big as the Hall you dove under. And it has leaves — down here, underwater, leaves — and they're moving, in a wind that isn't there.
>
> And laid out on its roots, neat as a table set for company: a heel of bread. A cup. A button. One crawfish shell, arranged very carefully. Some of the older things are half *in* the bark — like the tree is slowly taking them to heart.

Then, before the breath runs out, one per diver — their [[tether-manifestations|manifestation]], gently, at the source *(match each PC's register to their entry in that file)*:

> The cold spot where your curse sits — it doesn't twinge down here. It settles. Like a key going home.

**Stage 1 guardrails:** no words, no conversation, no reveal. They surface with a certainty, not a fact: *something lives down there, and it was glad we came.* If they ask around town: warmth and shrugs. Nobody knows. Nobody has ever dived past the barnacle line — why would you?

## Stage 2 — The Conversation

### First contact *(the spear offered into the water)*

> The spear hums in your hand — and then, for the first time since you won it, it goes still. Really still. The stillness of an arrow home in its quiver.
>
> Look at the grain. Look at the tree. Same wood. You've been carrying a piece of it the whole time.
>
> And something turns toward you. Not the whole tree — something *in* it. Person-sized attention. Careful. Curious. Glad.
>
> And in your heads, in a voice built out of every Celebration you've ever stood through:
>
> *"Stay, honored guest."*
>
> It's not a threat when it says it. It's the only hello it knows.

### How it speaks — the borrowed-words rule *(the delivery tic — this is the whole performance)*

- **Its entire vocabulary is the Celebration.** Two hundred years of hearing one song: every word it can say is a line the party has heard sung. Deliver its lines slow, a beat between words — a person reusing borrowed words.
- **Words for city-things. Images for spirit-things.** The city never sang about its companion — so it has **no words at all** for the one thing that matters most. When the conversation turns there, it goes silent and *presses images instead.* Play the silence.
- **It is person-scaled in mind.** Unlike the Heart, it notices you, remembers you, wants your names — and learns them. It may say a PC's name back: the first new word it has taken in two centuries. Spend that once.
- **Temperament: courtesy and curiosity.** It has been the perfect guest for two hundred years. It is not broken, not bitter, not performing sorrow. The sad part is structural. If the table cries, it's because the spirit won't.

### Its questions *(every guest brings a gift)*

It has had no conversation in two hundred years. It asks — in question-shaped pressure — about the *outside.* The sea. A road. Snow. **Let each PC give it one memory as a gift.** The offering etiquette pays off at the bottom of the water: leave firsts. A player narrating thirty seconds of somewhere they've been is the best scene in this file, and it costs nothing.

## Stage 3 — The Ask *(post-Communing only)*

The spear has touched the one it was carved for. The moment it enters the pool:

> The moment the spear touches the water, the tree moves. Not violently — every leaf at once, turning toward you the way faces turn.
>
> Because the spear has been *there.* It has touched the one this gift was carved for, two hundred years ago and never delivered. And the tree can feel it on the wood.
>
> And for the first time — the first time in two hundred years of never asking anyone for anything — it asks.
>
> It has no words for this. The city never sang it any. So it presses three things into you, slow and careful, like a letter being folded:
>
> Itself. Alive. Still here.
>
> The feast above. Still kind. Not their fault.
>
> And the only sentence it has that says *held*: — *"it shall not pass out from among us."*
>
> Carry that. To the one that's reaching. That's the whole ask: *answer for me.*

**What the answer arms (menu, not script — [[resolution-options]]):** carrying it back to the Heart makes the party the living channel between the spirits. That can become the spear-bridge (contact without freedom), fuel for truth-and-politics (Adaline hears what's under her floor — [[set-piece-show-me]]), the blueprint for a quiet unbinding, or something the table invents. **The spirit attaches no instructions.** It asked to be answered for, not avenged, not rescued. Everything past the ask belongs to the players.

## Fear Menu *(performed moments, not stat blocks)*

- **The Drums Start** *(free — costless dread)*: mid-conversation, faint through forty feet of water: rehearsal above, an ordinary night. The spirit's attention leaves them — wholly, instantly, turned up toward the sound. Hold three seconds of being forgotten. Then it comes back, courteous. That's what its weeks are.
- **A Root Closes** *(spend a Fear)*: in its eagerness — a gesture too big — a root shifts and cages a diver's path. Agility Reaction or mark 1 HP and tick Breath. It is horrified at itself, and the only apology it owns is the wrong word: *"…stay—"*
- **The Breath Arithmetic** *(spend a Fear)*: silt blooms, blackout; the root maze on the way up isn't where it was. One roll each to ascend; failures tick Breath.
- **The Turn, From Inside** *(spend a Fear — only if they dove on a Celebration night, and they were warned)*: the water goes glass around them. The light swells *from the tree.* The vast hand closes — on everyone present. Every tether pulls taut and *answers;* each PC marks a Stress; Breath ticks twice. The spirit — urgent for the only time in this file — **pushes them up.** It takes the whole performance alone, the way it always has.

## Walking Out *(states, not endings)*

- **They tell no one.** Fine. The dive keeps. The spirit doesn't summon them back — it has waited two hundred years; it can wait for guests to return at their own pace.
- **They tell [[delphine|Delphine]] or [[vael-miroux|Vael]].** Vael goes very quiet: the vast *held* thing he could never resolve now has a face and an address. Neither can add facts — both can add weight.
- **They take it to [[adaline-roux|Adaline]].** This is evidence [[set-piece-show-me]] was built for. "There is a guest under your floor" is a sentence she has to go look at.
- **They carry the answer southwest.** [[set-piece-the-delivery]] runs again, shorter, with a letter instead of a question.
- **They start designing the disruption.** [[resolution-options]] — the five levers, none of them musical. The spirit gave them structure, never a plan. *(⚰️ 2026-07-25: Fen is not part of this and never was after the recut — no chant-alteration heist.)*

## GM Quick Ref

- **Where:** straight down under the Hall of the Mire. Deepest water in the walls. Diveable, any act.
- **Stages:** presence (nothing) → conversation (spear) → the ask (post-Communing). Stack in any order the table earns.
- **The tic:** it only speaks in Celebration lines, slow, borrowed. Silence + images for anything about its companion.
- **It is:** friendly, courteous, curious, person-scaled, tricked long ago by a yes it can't take back.
- **It never:** asks to be freed, refuses freedom, blames the city, names a lever, performs grief.
- **The three sayables:** *"Stay, honored guest." · "We keep what keeps us." · "It shall not pass out from among us."*
- **Never dive on a Celebration night. Or absolutely do.**
