# Seven — the Magpie

**Role:** Leavy's familiar — the old one, from the life before; the only witness at the table who remembers the person, and it can't tell anyone
**Location:** wherever [[the-witch|Leavy]] is; officially "resident" at [[The Verdant Margin]]
**Faction:** Independent (fiercely)
**Tier:** 1 *(narrative NPC; mechanics per the table's Witch familiar rules — Ranger companion framework as fallback `[VERIFY]`)*

> **The player names it.** Whatever devastatingly literal thing Leavy picks, every NPC adopts it instantly — record the table name here when it lands: ________. Its true name is **Seven**, and the day Leavy *calls* it that, the bird answers ([[leavy-arc#Seven — the magpie|the arc]]).

---

## Appearance

A magpie — black and white, with that oil-slick green-blue shine across the wings when the light catches. But look closer and it's *worn*: flight feathers gone ragged at the tips the way only distance does, a healed-crooked toe on the left foot, and a small patch over one shoulder where the feathers grew back white. It has crossed most of a continent. It looks like it.

It is never more than three rooms from Leavy. It took exactly one day to learn which room is Leavy's.

## The One Rule (GM)

**Seven knows everything and can say nothing.** It shows; it never says. Every clue it delivers is a fragment — an object, a gesture, a sound — and per the house clue rules, the plain-words version always fires later through a second channel (an NPC or PC says it aloud). If the players guess a charade *wrong*, Seven accepts the wrong guess with visible resignation. It has been misunderstood for three years. It's used to it.

**The sound exception:** magpies mimic. Seven does sounds, never speech — a kettle coming to boil, a door that creaks in a specific place, a two-note hum somebody used to hum. Each one is a memory clue on your schedule. *(One banked exception, GM-controlled, use late: its croak — the harsh "rru. rru." it makes when content — has been the name **Rue** the entire time. Everyone hears birdsong. Nobody clocks it until the plant-name pattern lands. It has been saying the name since the day it arrived.)*

## How to Voice a Bird That Can't Talk

The delivery tic: **the beat before the answer.** Whatever's asked, Seven tilts its head, fixes one eye on the speaker, lets the silence sit a second too long — *then* responds. That pause is the whole performance. It makes every answer feel considered, because it is.

The charade structure, when Seven really needs to say something: **object → action → stare.** It fetches or points at a thing, mimes what the thing *does*, then stares at whoever needs to get it. Three beats, then it waits. It will repeat the sequence exactly once, slower, like you're being very slow.

## The Phrasebook

*Grab-and-go read-alouds. Say them; don't polish them. Pick the first one your eye lands on.*

### Answering questions

- **Yes:** "One sharp tap on the table. Like a judge."
- **No:** "It turns its whole body away from you. Not the head. The whole bird."
- **Sort of / it's complicated:** "It rocks foot to foot. Side to side. Apparently that means 'ehhh.'"
- **That's a stupid question:** "It looks at you. Then it looks at the spot where your sentence landed, like it's physically on the floor. Then back at you."
- **It can't answer that one:** "It opens its beak. Nothing comes out. It closes it again and fixes one eye on you — and for a second it looks so frustrated you're sorry you asked."
- **It desperately wants to answer that one:** "It goes completely still. Birds aren't supposed to be able to look tired. This one's figured out how."
- **Asked about the old life, point blank:** "It starts the charade. Object, gesture, stare. Halfway through, it just… stops. Puts the thing down. Some sentences don't fit in a beak."

### Supervising the brewing *(quality control is not optional)*

- "It's watching you measure. It taps the jar. You weren't done pouring. …It taps the jar again."
- "You reach for the salt. A black-and-white blur lands on your wrist. Wrong jar, apparently."
- "It walks across the open recipe, stops on one word, and pecks it. Twice. Hard."
- "It leans over the rim of the cauldron. One eye. Then the other eye. Then it looks at you and does the little side-to-side rock. Passable. Not your best."
- "You finish the pour, and Seven does one full lap of the bench, stops, and — bobs. Head all the way down. You get the horrible feeling you were just bowed to."
- "You're about to taste it. Seven gets between you and the spoon. It tastes it first. You have a food taster now. You didn't ask for one."
- *(when the hands do an old-school move):* "The moment your hands do the thing — the fold, the turn, whatever it was — Seven stops moving. Completely. It watches your hands like it's seeing a ghost. Because it is."

### Theft & the hoard *(comedy channel — mostly shinies, occasionally load-bearing)*

- "Tenson's pen is gone. Seven is on the rafter. The pen is also on the rafter."
- "It's not even hiding it. It is holding your coin and looking directly at you."
- "Whatever you just set down — that's the hoard's now. Appeals can be filed with a shinier object."
- "You go looking for the thing. The hoard has: three buttons, a bent spoon, something of Orna's that's faintly glowing, and — huh. There it is. Filed."
- *(the gift ritual — when YOU choose to release a keepsake, per [[leavy-arc#Seven — the magpie|the arc list]]):* "It works something loose from under its wing feathers — it's carried this a long way. Sets it down in front of Leavy. Steps back. Waits."

### The old habits *(the ache — one at a time, no comment, move on)*

- "It's sunset. Seven nudges a cup toward you. There's nothing in the cup. It waits anyway. …It does this every day."
- "It lands on your left shoulder. Always the left. It settles like it's done it ten thousand times — because it has. You just weren't there for it."
- "It's grooming the air just above your head. Careful. Methodical. Like there's hair up there. There isn't."
- "You're upset. You haven't said anything — you barely know it yet. From across the room there's this low sound. Not a magpie sound. A kettle, just starting to sing."
- "It clocks every face you wear. One glance at the new disguise, then back to the hoard. Filing it. *Not that one either.*" *(quiet setup — the joy beat when Leavy accidentally wears the right one is banked in [[leavy-arc#Seven — the magpie|the arc]]; don't spend it early)*

### Reactions to the party & regulars

- **[[the-witch|Leavy]] says "I don't remember":** "It hops closer. Leans its head against your hand. And it just — stays. It doesn't ask you to be anyone."
- **[[orna-tessant|Orna]]:** "It watches Orna drink the experiment with an expression you can only call *professional horror.* Then it steals her stirring rod. She says it's the most sensible colleague she's had in years."
- **[[Delphine]]:** "Best behavior. Feet together, feathers smooth. Delphine looked at it exactly once, the first day, and it looked back, and whatever passed between them is none of your business."
- **[[Isotta]]:** "When Isotta talks, it watches the window. Not her. The window." *(her reflection — say nothing, it's her clue)*
- **Fletcher:** "It's decided the Ranger is staff. Acceptable staff. It leaves him a dead beetle on the rail. That's payment. Don't ask what for."
- **Tenson:** "Tenson has proposed a communication protocol. One tap yes, two taps no. Seven complies — flawlessly — right up until the answer's complicated, and then it just stares at him until he amends the form." *(runner: the automaton and the bird, iterating the protocol, version 4.0 has an appendix)*
- **The owlbear cub:** "It sits just out of paw range and supervises the cub like a disappointed governess."
- **A stranger asks about Leavy:** "The bird is suddenly between you and them. It's tiny. It does not appear to know that."
- **Anyone but Leavy touches the formulary:** "One warning: the ratchet sound. There is no second warning."

### When the old life surfaces *(rare — these are the knife; one per session, max)*

- **The coven comes up:** "Every feather flattens at once. It's a smaller bird suddenly. It does not like this conversation."
- **The curse / what was done:** "You have never heard a magpie growl. You have now — a low ratchet, way down in the chest, and it doesn't stop until the subject changes."
- **Feverfew** *(once the name is known)*: "It bows its head. All the way down — beak to the wood, wings a little open. And it holds it. Birds don't do this. This one learned it from someone."
- **The sprig, the pin, any keepsake being examined:** "It comes and stands next to the object while you look. Not guarding it. Attending it."
- **Leavy asks it, quietly, 'did you know me?':** "One tap. Then — after a beat — its head comes to rest against your knuckles, and the tap becomes the whole answer to every question you haven't asked yet."

## Blanked? Roll d8 *(bird is in the scene, you need business)*

1. It's stolen something. You'll find out what when its owner does.
2. Mimics a sound from the old life — kettle, creaking door, two hummed notes. *(clue channel — spend deliberately or keep it wallpaper)*
3. Rearranges whatever's on the table into Leavy's mise-en-place. Even if it's dinner.
4. The tea-hour cup nudge (if it's near sunset), or the left-shoulder landing (if Leavy's standing).
5. Judges whatever an NPC just said with the slow head-tilt. The NPC notices.
6. Preens the air above Leavy's head.
7. Brings someone a "gift": a dead bug, a pebble, a button. Recipient is being *ranked.* Nobody knows the criteria.
8. The contented croak: "rru. rru." *(It's saying the name. Nobody knows. Keep your face neutral.)*

## What It Wants

**To finish the delivery it started three years ago.** Three recipe-slips brewed, the book read page by page, and its witch's name put back where it belongs. It is not waiting to be understood. It is *working* — on the bird's schedule, one keepsake at a time.

## What It's Hiding

Everything. Involuntarily. It was there that night; it knows the coven, the teacher, the enemy, the name. The cruelty of its shape is that none of it fits through a beak — see the One Rule. It also hides one thing *deliberately:* how far it flew. If the party ever works that out (the ragged feathers, the healed toe, the white patch), let it land — three years, most of a continent, on a rumor of a clank with familiar hands.

## Key Relationships

- [[the-witch|Leavy]] — its witch. Was, is. The bond survived death, the curse, and the distance; the bird never voted on "was."
- [[leavy-arc|Feverfew]] — it was *her* gift to Leavy, hatchling-tamed for the heir. The bow is for her.
- [[orna-tessant|Orna]] — grudging colleague; her Calling didn't summon it, but her garden is the best bench in the city and it knows it.
- [[Delphine]] — the one who understood on sight. Mutual professional silence.

## Dialogue Hook

It doesn't have one. That's the point. It has the pause, the head-tilt, the one fixed eye — and a cup it nudges toward you at sunset, every day, that it will keep nudging until the person who's supposed to want the tea remembers that they do.
