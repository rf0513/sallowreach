# The Celebration — Master Clue Map & Stuck-Proofing Audit

*GM Eyes Only. The complete catalogue of every hint pointing at the campaign's central truth, the chain they're supposed to assemble into, and the audit of where a table can go hard-stuck. Built 2026-07-06 against the specific nightmare: **the party wins the Tidebreaker, points it at the tether, nothing happens, and nobody at the table knows what to do next.***

*Design constraint honored throughout: **nobody in town knows the truth** — so no hint may be an answer in an NPC's mouth. Every hint is a fragment that looks unrelated until the party holds two of them at once. The mystery must be solvable and must never be brain-dead. The difference between those is not fewer hints — it's hints from unrelated domains that only converge when a player does the converging.*

## The Plot, In Plain Words *(read this when you've lost the thread — it's the whole campaign in one page)*

**What's actually true:** Two hundred years ago, the founders of Sallowreach trapped a great spirit — the Tree Spirit — inside the city, and disguised the prison as a festival. The weekly Celebration IS the spell: the chants, the drums, the torches are the bars of the cage, and the city has sung its prisoner's walls solid every week for two centuries without knowing it. Meanwhile the Tree Spirit's companion — the Mangrove Spirit, the swamp itself — has spent those two hundred years *reaching* for it. That's why the Mire is swallowing the city: not attack, **grief.** A love story nobody knows they're standing in the middle of.

**How the players find out — five moves:**

1. **Their own curse is the thread.** They drowned during the ritual's weekly surge (the Turn) and got caught in the same net that holds the Tree Spirit — a small binding, same weave. Chasing their *cure* IS chasing the campaign's secret; they never need to care about spirits, only about going home.
2. **The cure-hunt finds the spear.** Delphine can't fix them → points at Caspar → Caspar's papers name the Tidebreaker (breaks curses!) and Marcel has it. Along the way they attend Celebrations — and at the **second** one, they *feel* their curse answer the ritual: "the thing that took us is... this." (Suspicion only. Not the prison. Not yet.)
3. **Vane's journal plants the key sentence.** The first ward-wizard's 200-year-old journal (the Preis hoard dig — **must fire before the spear**): *"Something inside the city holds the spirit's attention... I wonder if the answer is somewhere in those chants"* — struck through by his own hand. Spirit + city + chants, welded together for the first time. And the gut-punch that makes them read every page: **Vane wore their curse** — went under during a Turn in his first year, never left, died in post thirty-one years later. He had the question. They have the key.
4. **The spear works — and doesn't.** It cuts Isotta's and Leavy's curses (real, earned payoffs) — but the tether won't cut, and the cut *shows them why*: their curses were small knots; the tether is four threads in a weave of sixty thousand. Caspar re-reads his own fairytales: the spear was carved as a **gift, never delivered.** A gift needs a receiver. He hands them the chant transcription ("ask it to read my fairytales back to me") and they sail southwest.
5. **The Heart delivers the truth.** At the Mangrove Spirit's Heart, the spear opens the one possible conversation: they *feel* the grief directly — two hundred years of searching for a companion that was **taken.** The spirit reads Caspar's transcription through the spear: the beautiful old words are a binding. The welcome is a cage. And back in the city, the spear now pulls **inward** — to the Hall of the Mire, the heart of the city, the cell. Now they know everything: what's trapped, where, by what, and that freeing it frees *them* too (same weave).

**How they free it — a menu, never a script ([[resolution-options]]):**
- ⚰️ **~~The heist (surgical)~~ and ~~the quiet strike (absence)~~ — BOTH CUT 2026-07-25 (GM ruling).** No chant-alteration ending (deriving load-bearing bars and having Fen play an altered second movement) and no attendance ending (organizing empty benches). **Nothing musical and nothing to do with who shows up is a lever in this campaign.** *(Reason: both turned the endgame into ritual-attendance mechanics, and every draft kept growing them back. Do not rebuild.)*
- **The interruption (blunt):** the ritual is a working and a working can simply be stopped — refused, drowned out, or prevented from happening. No research project, no transcription, no performer. It is loud, public, and it happens *to* sixty thousand people who love this night. ([[resolution-options]] — "surgical sabotage," now stripped of its apparatus.)
- **The hard road (politics):** bring Adaline evidence she can't wave off — *"Show me"* — and let the city *choose* to stop singing. Costs the most, means the most.
- **The knife (ward-break):** collapse Oswin's salt wall and let the grief pour in. Available, catastrophic, better as a threat than a plan.
- **The compromise (spear-bridge):** leave the spear with the Mangrove Spirit as a permanent line to its companion. The reaching stops; the city stands; the prisoner stays a prisoner. It works. That's what's wrong with it.
- **And whatever the table invents.** The fronts bend; the file serves the table.

**The stakes of freeing it:** the reunion ends the Mire's growth forever — and ends the binding that makes the lumber extraordinary, the economy work, and the city's identity mean what it means. And the tether lifts, so the party can finally go home. Every ending asks the same question: *what do you owe the people you love, who are doing harm they never chose?*

1. **The wreck** — the Cold Hand; the tether takes during a Turn. *(Fired, S1.)*
2. **Remy's timing line** — "You went under on a Celebration night. The water was singing." *(S1 chain, said.)*
3. **Delphine → Caspar → Marcel** — the spear chain. *(Triple-built, live; Caspar encounter fired S3–S4.)*

   - **The chant reaches them** — Beat 5.5, all four PCs, only during the eight bars of the second movement ([[session-05-first-celebration]]).
   - **It's an incantation** — Leavy's free read: not a language anybody speaks. And nobody in the city can say what it means or finds that odd.
   - 🔴 **They write the words down** — see Risk B. **Non-negotiable; the rest of the campaign hangs on it.**
   - **THE CLICK** — at the lantern release, at the water, the tether answers and one PC recognizes the four sensations from the night they drowned ([[reveal-read-alouds]] §1). *(No longer optional and no longer "it keeps" — there is nothing for it to keep to.)*
   - **The too-good sleep** — the night button, the second half of Beat 5.5's pairing.
   
   *(Old design, retired: the first Celebration buried the Turn and the **second** one clicked. The two-night ladder is dead. Note the click's payload has also shifted — the party already knows the Celebration and their curse are connected (step 6 fired first, out of order), so the click now lands as* "the thing that drowned us was **this**, and I can feel it," *not as the connection itself.)*
6. **Vane's journal** — ✅ **FIRED S5**, and it overdelivered on a *different* leg than designed. The rewritten prop ([[the-first-maintainers-journal]]) carries **the tether precedent**, not the attractor: a Guild wizard who went under on a festival night, could never leave, and was commended for his "devotion." The party walked out **certain the Celebration caged them.** ⚠ The chant-pointer it used to carry is **gone** — [[old-words-handout]] and [[fen]] now hold that leg.
7. **The spear won** — Marcel's price paid. Isotta's and Leavy's curses cut (give the scenes). **The tether doesn't move.**
8. **⚠ THE PIVOT (Risk A)** — someone must reframe the spear from *weapon* to *gift undelivered.* Designed: Caspar Step 4 — **with a full Caspar-gone fork** (he may be dead-on-paper and smuggled out per the S4 Ask): the Legacy Bundle (archive + two-stage letter, fires the night he leaves), Vael as the replacement mouth for the gift-reframe, and the journal + spear-compass carrying the heading. See [[the-tidebreaker]] § Step 4 fork.
9. **The delivery** — the Wending, the spear as compass, the Heart, the Communing: grief, searching, a companion *taken.* *(Staged: [[set-piece-the-delivery]] — the full pilgrimage run file.)*
10. **⚠ THE CONVERGENCE (Risks B+C)** — *(the Reading is staged: [[set-piece-the-delivery]] Leg 3 + [[reveal-read-alouds]] §4 — fires only if the folio is offered)* — the chants translated (the spirit reads them through the spear) and/or the assembly: *taken companion + attractor inside the city + tether answers the ritual + "hold the great presence"* → **the Celebration is the cage; the heart of the city is the cell.**
11. **The door answered** *(built 2026-07-18)* — the compass turns inward ([[reveal-read-alouds]] §5) and someone finally goes *down*: the dive under the Hall, the drowned tree, the meeting — [[set-piece-the-honored-guest]]. The prisoner has a face, a voice (borrowed from the chants), and an ask: *"answer for me."* **Note the free channel:** the dive works in *any* act — Stage 1 (presence only, no spear, no words) is a legal early eerie plant that spoils nothing, and the spirit is the whiff-proof second mouth for the Reading if step 10's folio-offer never fires.
12. **The levers open** — [[resolution-options]] + the Hidden Layer. The campaign's endgame is now the party's choice, as designed.

## The Audit — is it enough?

**Verdict: the trail is ~80% built, and it is genuinely good** — layered, two-channeled in most places, and honest to the constraint that no NPC knows. The click chain (L1) and the spear chain (L3 path) are over-built; the endgame levers (L5) are well designed. **The remaining 20% is concentrated in exactly the scenario you named: the week after they win the spear.** Three risks, ranked:

### 🔴 Risk A — the post-spear void *(the "hard stuck" you're afraid of — highest priority)*
The spear cuts Isotta's and Leavy's curses, then goes silent on the tether. Canon says "the natural next move is back to Caspar" — natural to *us*, invisible to a table riding the high of two cures into the crash of a dead end. **Currently one channel (visit Caspar), zero forcing function.** If they don't go museum-ward, there is no second mouth anywhere in the repo.

### 🟡 Risk B — the transcription never makes the boat *(rewritten 2026-07-24 — the shape of this risk changed twice)*
The L5 translation unlock requires the party to carry **the chants** (Caspar's transcription) to the Heart.

- **The old fear is obsolete.** "The journal is an optional side quest that might never fire" — it fired, S5. ✅
- **But the journal no longer plants the chant idea at all** (hints #8/#9 cut). Chant-pointers are now [[old-words-handout|the Old Words prop]] itself and [[fen|Fen's]] five contradictory translations.
- **⚠ NEW HOLE — P2's delivery man is gone.** P2 (below) kills this risk by having **Caspar** press the folio into their hands at the send-off. **Caspar left on a balloon in S5.** His written backup ("leaves the folio at Remy's with a note") still works, and the Legacy Bundle may already have delivered it.

## The Patches *(✅ ADOPTED 2026-07-06 — P1–P4 swept into canon; P5 is procedure and lives here)*
