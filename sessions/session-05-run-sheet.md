## 🧭 In Plain Words *(the whole session — no jargon)*

Tonight is one night and one day. **The night:** resume inside the museum, mid-sentence, where Session 4 cut — Caspar tells the party the whole truth (the crime boss Marcel Dray was officially executed by the Empire twelve years ago and is somehow alive; the curse-cutting spear exists, its papers go into the party's hands, and Marcel owns it), then formally asks them to fake his death: a boat drop-off in the deep swamp plus a false imperial incident report filed over Tenson's seal. The party negotiates or refuses. Then a two-minute dream scene for Isotta, held over from last session. **The day:** Tenson's promised friendly spar with Sable at dawn, Brin's dock send-off, and then the boat trip — the abandoned camp of two dead hunters, kept eerily tidy; the extraction of a fresh twelve-foot shed snake skin; the rookery, where a starving guardian spirit asks the party to kill the giant fish eating its colony (the trip's one real fight); and the deck reveal at dusk: the skin's seam proves the snake was *manufactured*, and Fletcher finds the maker's brand in his own journal, where it's been for three years. **Floating, any time:** if Isotta directly questions the heron that's been following her, the spirit that is owed her death introduces itself — politely — and offers her a grace period. End the session on Fletcher's face.

**Two goals tonight:** the party learns who Marcel is and what the cure costs, and Fletcher's hunt finds out what it's actually hunting.

## 🎭 The Theme *(say nothing, feel everything)*

Everyone tonight is **negotiating with death.**

- **Caspar** wants a counterfeit one — imperial paper as a burial shroud.
- **Marcel** already beat a real one — executed, certified, filed, and running a casino anyway.
- **Old Plenty** *(the rookery's guardian spirit — a giant spoonbill, proud and starving)* is counting its colony down toward zero — a slow death it can't bargain with, so it bargains with you.
- **The Tallykeeper** *(the heron following Isotta — the spirit that collects the dead, which is owed her)* offers her the politest death notice ever written: *"convince me to wait."*
- **The snake** turns out to have been *manufactured* — even its appetite has an author, and it kills the Mire's next season.

Don't announce this. Just know it. When in doubt, aim any improvised beat at the same question: *what does a death cost, and who pays it?*

### 0 · The Museum, Resumed *(pick up mid-sentence — don't recap, re-enter)*

Open exactly where you cut: Caspar's sentence hanging in the candlelight. Re-entry line, then run [[session-03-caspar-encounter]] **§2 → §3** as written:

> "…help me fake my death." The words are still sitting in the middle of the room. Nobody's touched them yet. Caspar looks at each of you in turn — and then he sags, like saying it out loud cost him the last of something. "You'll want to know why. Sit down. Please. I owe you the truth, and you are not going to like where it's kept."

- **§2 is a gusher — pace the three payloads, breathe between each:** the fencing operation and Marcel's leash on him · **the Marcel rumor** (*executed twelve years ago — I checked*) · **the Tidebreaker papers into their hands** + the point at Marcel's house. This is the "who is Marcel" scene the whole session leans on. The rumor is stage 1 of a two-stage reveal — stage 2 is what Isotta's ghost-sight shows her the first time she *sees* Marcel in person; **do not spend it tonight.**
- **§3 — say the costs out loud before anyone agrees:** it's perjury over Tenson's seal · the report *feeds Caius's annexation file* (Tenson sees this first — give him the beat) · Marcel learning the truth is a standing threat, not a footnote.
- **Bargain, never yes/no:** wording (let Tenson draft it) · timing (*after* they mine the archive) · the counter-offer they may find themselves — *"Stay. Help us take the closet"* (the closet = Marcel's strongroom, where the spear lives).
- **⚠ The collision, still armed:** Tenson is redlining a true report for accuracy while being asked to author a false one. Same seal, same week. **Do not resolve it for him.** If the player names it, go quiet and let the table have it.
- **Leavy's job:** she read his guttering hedge-ward at the door (S4) — if the table stalls, her practitioner's sympathy is the bridge: *he cast that alone, exhausted, protecting himself from a man he can't fight.*
- **However it ends: the archive stays in Sallowreach.** If the deal closes, stage the Legacy Bundle — his send-off package: the chant transcription, the spear's papers, his farewell letter ([[the-tidebreaker]] §Step 4, [[caspar-letter-handout]]). If they refuse, he goes quiet and thanks them for the knock — his Plan A resumes in two nights: the do-it-yourself fake death (beet-paint corpse pallor and all), alone, badly.

### 2 · Dawn — the Spar, then the Send-Off *(15–20 min, keep it sunny)*

**The spar (Tenson promised; Sable collects at first light).** No stakes, all character: one or two friendly rolls (Agility or Strength, **11** — this is play, not peril; a good showing earns a Hope). Sable is delighted by *everything* Tenson does, including losing. The knife, mid-bout, thrown away like it's nothing ([[twist-the-knife]] Tenson #2):

> Sable catches Tenson mid-grin — because he IS grinning, that's the thing — and says: "See? You could just be happy, you know. It's allowed."

It's not a taunt. That's the problem. Move straight to the dock before anyone processes it.

**The dock (Beat 1 of [[set-piece-the-wrong-skin]]):** Brin, the map, the two tasks (the skin and the count), the maker's-stamps line finally said aloud, and the Whimsy button (the cub — and the warning that her mother heard the conversation). Full text in the set piece.

**Remy's news — the Shipment Berth *(say it at the dock, settle it on the night water)*:** as the boat loads, Remy mentions — carefully casual — that he's signed on as a channel pilot for the **quarterly lumber shipment**: real wages, about a week away at the coast handoff, starting soon. He asks for nothing; that's the problem. Let it sit all day while he captains flawlessly, then run the negotiation on the trip home ([[remy#The Shipment Berth *(S5 — the negotiation; canon 2026-07-18)*|his file]]): match the berth as a real hire · split the season · let him go. **This is tonight's coin beat** — one per session, and this is the person it's for.

### 3 · The Wrong Skin *(the day — run [[set-piece-the-wrong-skin]] as written)*

The boat's maiden commission: the kept camp → the shedding hollow → **Old Plenty's parley**, the count, and the gar fight → the deck at dusk. Slotting notes only:

- **Give the rookery air.** **Old Plenty** is tonight's lore showcase — a starving guardian spirit that speaks only in counts, met with the offering etiquette in its own house. The lesson the whole table gets in one scene: spirits keep things, spend themselves doing it, and honor each other's ledgers. The camp (Beat 3) is a quiet 10-minute setup; the spirit answers it at the rookery. *(Ghost note, ruled 2026-07-15: ghosts are town business and only Isotta sees them — the Mire shows spirits, to everyone.)*
- **Party jobs:** Fletcher → the read, the hollow, the trail · Isotta → the dead men's kit + whatever she does about *"another keeper's ledger"* · Tenson → the tally-book audit + the southwest ward-wall visible from the water, visibly thinning (a free tell for the wall-maintenance thread, Front 2) · Leavy → the reagent slack-water + processing the casting (her mouth fires the reveal).
- **The gar fight is the trip's real fight** — minions plus the old bull. Consequence, not villain: the snake emptied the nurseries, the scavengers moved up. The party draws steel against the *cascade* two sessions before they meet the cause.
- **Narration ammo:** the set piece opens with **The Water Between** — seven ~30-second voiceovers for the trip's legs, Remy at the tiller throughout. Grab one per leg; the night-water one hands you the heron Fear beat.
- **Cut line if the clock bleeds:** the rookery count compresses to one roll + the bull; the deck reveal is never cut. Voiceovers cut free — they're seasoning, not structure.

### ★ · FLOATING — the Tallykeeper's Introduction *(any moment Isotta directly, insistently addresses the heron)*

The heron is the Tallykeeper — the spirit that collects the dead, which is owed *her*. It will not refuse a direct question twice. Likeliest moments: right after Old Plenty says *"the pale one is on another keeper's ledger,"* or on the night water home with the heron on the bow-post. When it fires:

- **Open [[the-tallykeeper#The Grace Period]] and run it as written** — the collection notice, *"convince me to wait,"* the grace cord with six loose loops tied around her wrist at the table. Closed docket cases are her collateral (Anselme already counts).
- **Delivery tic:** no contractions, short ruled lines, a beat between sentences. Courteous, patient, absolutely unmoved. No spooky voice — the dread is the arithmetic.
- **If she doesn't force it:** the heron watches the whole trip from the bow-post and says nothing. The introduction keeps. Its patience *is* the pressure.

### 4 · Cut to Black — the Brand *(last two minutes)*

From [[set-piece-the-wrong-skin]] Beat 6: the casting unrolled down the deck at dusk, the seam, Leavy's line — then Fletcher, alone with his lantern and his journal, finding **the brand 2H-3 at every jaw-hinge, three years in his own pack.** End on his face. That's the session.

## 🗝️ Secrets & Clues *(master — reveal ~half; each fires twice: shown + said)*

1. The Empire **executed Marcel Dray twelve years ago** — tried, certified, filed. Caspar checked. *(§2 — scripted, say it)*
2. The museum is a front, and **"Object on Loan" means fenced to Marcel** — the leash is tribute, and the goods were never the point.
3. The Tidebreaker's own papers: *a gift carved by one great spirit for another — cuts what spirit-water binds.* **The cure has an address, and Marcel owns it.**
4. A false incident report doesn't just save Caspar — **it feeds Caius's file.** *(Tenson's expertise; let him say it to the table.)*
5. The cub's cage and the party's boat-crates carry the **same maker's stamps.** *(Brin says it at the dock — carried from S4, finally fires.)*
6. **"Someone's been keeping this thing fed and moving. Its trail has barge-stops."** *(the freight road, in sayable words at last)*
7. Two scale-lineages meet in **a straight line down the spine. Nature doesn't draw straight lines.** *(shown on the deck; Leavy says "someone MADE this")*
8. The brand: **2H-3, every jaw-hinge — and it's been in Fletcher's journal for three years.** *(no roll; the gut-punch is free)*
9. Old Plenty: **"Your hunters were never hidden. It watched their blind before they chose the blind — and it would not stay counted."** *(the snake can't be tracked, even by a spirit — said plainly by a starving one)*
10. The heron is not hunting Isotta. **It is owed her — and the only thing on its table is time.** *(fires ONLY if she forces the introduction)*

**Carried, deliberately NOT tonight:** Vael's wall-verdict line (*"it turns away the uncrossed"* — his next Still Cup scene) · "nothing that punctures the skin" (keeps for any Gault sighting) · the lab and who built the snake (the next trip — [[set-piece-the-cold-cache]]) · Isotta's ghost-sight read of Marcel (spend it the day she SEES him) · the binding, the Tree Spirit, the Celebration-as-cage (always).
