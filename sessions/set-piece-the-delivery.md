# Set Piece — The Delivery

*Critical-path steps 9–10 ([[the-celebration-clue-map]]), staged as one journey. **The scripts live in [[reveal-read-alouds]] §3–5 and the mechanics live in [[the-wending]] → [[the-deep-mire]] → [[the-mangrove-spirits-heart]]** — this file is how you run the trip: the send-off, the legs, the rolls, the threshold branch, and the night at the Heart. The campaign's wonder peak. Nothing here commits the table to an ending.*

---

## 🧭 In Plain Words *(the whole set piece — no jargon)*

The party has finally won the magic spear. It cured two of them of their personal curses — but it did nothing to the thing binding all four of them to this city, and the one expert they trust told them why: the spear isn't a weapon, it's a *gift*. It was carved two hundred years ago by one great spirit as a present for another, and it was never delivered. So the party loads their boat and sails southwest into the deep swamp to deliver it — past the last channels anyone has ever mapped, following the spear itself, which pulls toward its recipient like a compass. At the end of the water they find the recipient: a spirit so vast the swamp is basically its body, grieving a companion that was stolen from it two centuries ago. When they offer the spear, the party finally *feels* the truth — the swamp that's been slowly swallowing the city isn't attacking anyone; it's an arm, sweeping the dark for something it lost. And if they thought to bring the old chant transcription along, the spirit can translate the city's beloved festival songs — revealing that the prettiest tradition in Sallowreach is, word for word, a lock. The party sails home knowing everything. What they do about it is theirs.

**Two goals:** the table feels the love story directly — grief, not monster — and walks away holding the campaign's worst sentence. No fight at the Heart. No answer from the spirit about what to do. The ending stays theirs.

## ✅ When This Fires *(the prerequisites gate — all four, or wait)*

1. **The spear is won** — Marcel's price paid, the curse-cuts staged ([[the-tidebreaker]]).
2. **The gift-reframe has landed** — Caspar's Step 4 (*"a gift needs someone to receive it"*) or, if he's gone, Vael's version. Both scripted: [[the-tidebreaker]] § Step 4 + fork.
3. **The folio is aboard** — the old-words transcription ([[old-words-handout]]) via Caspar's send-off dare or the Legacy Bundle. **Whiff-proof:** if nobody packed it, Remy remembers the folio Caspar left at the inn — before the boat casts off, not after.
4. **The heading is known** — southwest, past the Wending; past the known water the spear is its own compass ([[the-tidebreaker]] § The Spear in the Mire).

**Party condition check:** the Heart runs on **Stress, not HP**. If they arrive Lost and frayed, the danger doubles — that's allowed, but know it going in.

### 0 · The Send-Off *(the dock, dawn)*

- **If Caspar is in town:** run his two scripted beats from [[the-tidebreaker]] — thirty seconds holding the spear, then the folio pressed on them: *"If it can speak to you — ask it to read my fairytales back to me."* He won't wave the boat off. He'll already be walking away.
- **If Caspar is gone** *(the S4 fork)*: the dare lives in his letter's postscript ([[caspar-letter-handout]]). Have someone re-read it on the dock — a player will, if the letter's in their pack; Remy asks about it if not.
- **The guides, in descending order** *(say this plainly when they plan the trip)*: **Remy** takes them to the edge of the known water and no farther — not fear, manners: *"Past the last stake, I'd just be one more lost thing you have to carry."* **[[odile-verret|Odile]]**, if they've earned her, will pole as far as the threshold — the deep water keeps her, and it lets her closer than anyone living. **Past the threshold, the spear is the only guide there is.** Each hand-off is a beat; let them feel the water getting emptier of help.
- **Isotta, one image, free** *(GM dial — skip if the grace cord isn't live)*: the heron rides the bow-post out of the harbor as always. At the last known water it lifts off and does not follow. It watches them go. Say nothing. *(The [[the-tallykeeper|Tallykeeper]]'s ledger is town business; whatever they're sailing toward is not on it.)*

### 1 · The Wending Out *(run [[the-wending]] — Diff 11 with Remy)*

- Standard crossing, Countdown (4) — but **take a route away**: *"the cut you ran last month is roots to the waterline now."* Front 1, made tactile, on the way to meet Front 1's cause.
- **The camp at the last stake** — the night scene before the deep. This is the slot for the tether twinge ([[reveal-read-alouds]] §2, deep-southwest entry): *"the pull you always feel toward the place you were going? For one breath, something out here pulls back the other way."* One per session, spend it here.
- **Remy's job at the fire:** if the four have never traded symptoms, his one line does it — *"so what's it actually like?"* — and the Comparison fires ([[tether-manifestations]]). The word nobody quite finds is *kept*. It pays off at the Reading. Say nothing.

### 2 · The Deep Mire *(run [[the-deep-mire]] — Diff 14, navigation at disadvantage)*

- **The spear takes over:** held pointed southwest, the grain drifts when aimed true, stills when it isn't ([[the-tidebreaker]]). The navigation Countdown (6) runs as written — the spear doesn't remove the disadvantage; it removes the *arguments*.
- **3–4 real rolls, the house way:** the lead's navigation (Instinct, disadv.) · a reader on the light (Instinct — the Bioluminescent Deep's early cousin: the glow stops following wind) · Undertow Roots if anyone crowds the black water (Agility Reaction) · the Welcome Channel when someone finally shows the water some manners — an offering at a root clears the disadvantage or the attention markers. **The lesson of the whole leg, said in mechanics: stop treating the Mire like terrain.**
- **Clue #29 fires here, shown** *(player-assembly special — never say it for them)*: the deeper light moves in amber, deep green, near-black. The colors of the spear's grain. If a player says *"the spear is made of this,"* light the moment up. If nobody does, it keeps — three sightings, sessions apart, is the design.
- **Camping deep:** Lost means no Long Rest — and every point of Stress they carry in is a point closer to being Lost in the Attention later. If they push recklessly, let the arithmetic show once: *"you can press on frayed, or give the swamp one more day of manners."*

### ★ · BRANCH — The Threshold *(only if [[gault]]'s third encounter is unresolved)*

Run **[[set-piece-the-last-inventory]]** (the Threshold door — his Fear menu, the mid-fight clue, and the ending menu live there), **at the edge, never inside**: Gault in spirit-adjacent dark, tracking bioluminescent concentrations he thinks are lantern rays, alone, out of his depth, too greedy to know it. The staging rules from [[the-deep-mire]] carry it — *Patient Hunter* passes him to reach the party; *Wrong animals* avoid him; the water lets him through and the party should find that infuriating. A small, ugly, human fight framed against something that doesn't register either side of it.

**After he's dead: the Mire goes still. The bioluminescence disperses slowly. No one mentions it. No one needs to.** Give the table a full quiet minute, then cross.

*(If Gault died sessions ago, the threshold is just the last narrowing — the channels close behind them, complication #1, and the only open water leads in.)*

### 3 · The Heart *(run [[the-mangrove-spirits-heart]] — the night scene; do not turn it into a fight)*

**Arrival** *(lights down, slow)*:

> The channel just… ends. Not blocked — *finished.* The boat glides out into open black water with no far side, and the light starts.
>
> Not glow. Sheets of it. Slow spirals, way down, in colors you don't have names for — and it's moving like breathing. In, and out. Slower than anything alive should breathe.
>
> Nobody says anything, because there's nothing out here to say it to. And then you realize that's wrong. There's nothing out here that's *listening* — the way a person doesn't listen to one mote of dust in a sunbeam.
>
> You're not somewhere. You're *inside* something. And it doesn't know you've arrived.

- **The Slow Attention, staged:** the first PC who truly takes in the scale marks 2 Stress — deliver it as awe, not damage. From there the environment file runs the pressure. **A PC out of Stress here isn't dying — they're staying.** Lost in the Attention is the night's real stake; another PC's reach (a touch, a name, the spear pressed into their hands, Diff 14) is the rescue. The Feature Question to have ready: *what's the memory they call them back with — and does it work because it's true?*
- **One quiet Instinct roll before the offering** *(the Bioluminescent Deep)*: whoever stops to look gets one true thing — the light is directional, always bending toward Sallowreach. Shown, not said. On Hope, give it free and let it land.
- **Who holds the spear?** Ask the table, in character, and let it cost something. Don't assign it.
- **The Communing:** Presence roll — for *how much*, never for whether. Then **open [[reveal-read-alouds]] §3 and perform it as written**: the base (the grief, *"Taken. Not lost. Taken."*) for everyone; the heading add-on if they hold Vane's sentence or the click; the tether add-on if the cut-that-shows fired. On Fear: the bearer marks Stress and the attention lingers, *examining*. Let that linger.
- **The Reading** *(only if someone offers the folio — Caspar's dare is the fuse; never prompt it)*: **[[reveal-read-alouds]] §4, as written**, one translated line at a time, flat. *"It shall not pass out from among us."* Stop. Count three. Then let the silence run as long as it wants to. **If nobody thinks of the folio, it keeps** — the trip home and Caspar's letter will send them back, and a second pilgrimage is not a failure, it's a sequel.

### 4 · The Way Home *(quiet — let the boat be a decompression chamber)*

- No new content on the return. Complications only if the table needs motion. The Mire is *easier* going home — don't explain why. *(The Welcome Channel logic: they came as guests and delivered a gift. The swamp follows its own manners.)*
- **Per-PC after-images, one line each, unprompted:** Fletcher's compass-sense runs *clean* for the first day — no wrong-way home-smell. Leavy tastes the almost-name once, gently, like something saying thank you. The water does not look at Isotta — it *inclines*, the way the wall's worn paths do. Tenson's bell is silent, and the silence feels like a watch relieved, not abandoned. *(All four registers from [[tether-manifestations]] — softened, not resolved. The binding is unchanged; something just learned their names.)*
- **The coda fires later:** next time they enter the city, **[[reveal-read-alouds]] §5** — the spear stops pulling southwest and pulls *in*, toward the Hall of the Mire. Canon: [[the-tidebreaker]] § After the Heart. Do not rush this; give it its own doorway moment.

## 🗝️ Clue Ledger *(each key clue: shown once, said once)*

| Secret | Shown | Said aloud |
|---|---|---|
| The growth is *searching*, not attacking — two hundred years of one gesture | the Communing base (felt, everyone) | the script says it plainly — *"an arm, sweeping the dark"*; a player will restate it, let them |
| The companion was **taken** — not lost, not wandered | the Communing base | *"Taken. Not lost. Taken."* — the spirit's own certainty |
| The reaching has a **heading**, and it's Sallowreach | the directional light (Instinct, shown) | the §3 heading add-on — *"you've sailed it"* (fires only with Vane's sentence or the click aboard) |
| The chants are a **lock** — *"it shall not pass out from among us"* | the folio offered, the spear gone cold | the Reading, §4, line by line (only if offered — else 🗄 keeps) |
| Clue #29 — spear grain = the Heart's living light = the city's impossible wood | shown, third sighting | **player-assembly only — no NPC mouth exists for this.** If said at the table, tick it in [[the-celebration-clue-map]] |
| The tether is a thumbnail of the great binding | §3 tether add-on (if the cut-that-shows fired) | Delphine/Vael already said the half-version post-cure ([[the-tidebreaker]] whiff-proofing) |
| The Mire ignores Gault | predators pass him; the water lets him through | Remy's contempt is already canon; Odile at the threshold, if present: *"the water doesn't bother hating him. That's your job."* |

**Deliberately NOT on this trip:** who took the companion, how, or that the taking is still being sung *by name* — the Reading shows the lock, never the locksmith's face · any lever, any "what you must do" · the Tree Spirit named as such (the spear pulls; nobody labels) · blame or absolution for the city. The spirit doesn't know. Nothing at the Heart editorializes.

## 😨 Fear Tonight *(spend where it breathes — the environments carry the menus)*

- **The Wending out:** the Reaching Dark, once, at the camp — the light arranges into a pattern that tracks nothing; 1 Stress each; **do not name it.** They'll know what it was on the way home. That retroactive click is worth more than any spend.
- **The Deep Mire:** *Lost in the Deep* — the way back moves; the only open water leads inward. Or *Patient Hunter*, strictly before the threshold — nothing hunts past it.
- **The Heart — the Reaching** *(the tonal centerpiece; spend it once, perfectly)*: a wall of new growth surges past them toward the city; Agility/Instinct Reaction 14 or 1d8+2 and swept Close, *with* the current of grief. **Never an attack. A tide.** Say afterward, in the silence: *"it wasn't aiming at you. It doesn't know it hit you."*
- **After the Communing, 1 Fear:** the attention comes back — briefly, deliberately — and rests on the spear-bearer alone. Nothing happens. It just *found* them, once. Then it's gone, back to reaching.
- **What you never spend Fear on here:** anything that reads as the spirit *fighting them*. The horror is scale; the wonder is scale; keep both.

## 🚫 Hard Guardrails

- **No fight at the Heart.** Gault resolves at the threshold or not at all. If a GM needs a stat block past the threshold, the scene has been misread ([[the-mangrove-spirits-heart]]).
- **The Communing does not lift the tether, free anyone, or assign a task.** It makes the conversation possible. Full stop.
- **The spirit never editorializes.** No plea, no target, no lever. The "What NOT to say" lists in [[reveal-read-alouds]] §3–4 are law.
- **The folio must be physically aboard for the Reading.** No folio, no translation — send them home and let Caspar's letter do its work.
- **Endings stay menus.** After the Reading, the answer to *"so what do we do?"* lives in [[resolution-options]] and in them — never in this file, never in the spirit's mouth.
- **Old Patience's Hollow is not a stop on this route by default** — it's Tier 1 water off the *known* southwest channel, and its scene ([[old-patiences-hollow]], the partial tether relief) plays best *before* the spear is won. If it hasn't fired yet, it may be the journey's first station — promote it per its own header before running it cold.

## 🎯 Win Condition

The table felt the love story once, directly, and nobody at it said "monster" afterward; somebody held the spear and it cost the others something to allow it; the silence after *"it shall not pass out from among us"* ran at least ten full seconds before anyone spoke; and the party sailed home with the campaign's whole truth and **no instructions** — arguing, out of character, about what they owe a city that sings.
