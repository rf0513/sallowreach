# Set Piece — Show Me *(the Adaline conversation)*

*The campaign's social climax: the party brings the truth to the one person who can make sixty thousand people listen. This file is the **scene**; the room's mechanics live in [[the-hall-of-the-mire]] and the woman lives in [[adaline-roux]]. Hint #38 in [[the-celebration-clue-map]] — banked since the map was drawn, staged at last. Three movements: **the door** (5 min), **the weighing** (the centerpiece, 30–45 min), **the box** (10 min, give it air).*

## Cold-Open Box *(the whole scene in 30 seconds)*

**What happens:** The party lays the truth in front of Adaline Roux — the translated chants, the Interlock, the tether. She cross-examines it with four questions. If the evidence holds and they answer her straight, she fetches the founders' box **herself** and reads the word "hold" out loud with new eyes. Her click — the wall, the edge, the reaching — is **hers to say**. What she does next is a menu, not a script.

| Roll | What | Difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The door — a private table, not a hearing (skip if Standing is strong) — Presence | 13 |
| 2 | Laying the case — Presence or Knowledge, feeds the Weighing countdown | 13 |
| 3 | The grandmother question — Presence, under her stare | 14 |
| 4 | Reading the fragment with her (optional) — Knowledge or Instinct ([[the-hall-of-the-mire#Features|Founders' Box]]) | 12 |

**The Weighing:** dynamic countdown, **Progress 4 / Consequence 4**. Hard evidence laid clean and straight answers tick Progress; overclaiming, demands, and speeches tick Consequence. Progress 4 → she goes for the box. Consequence 4 → [[the-hall-of-the-mire#Features|Adaline Goes Still]] — the door closes *for now*; the evidence keeps.
**Party jobs:** Tenson = the paper (she respects documents read clause by clause) · Isotta = the demonstrable (a thing Adaline can watch happen) · Fletcher = the wall-and-edge pattern · Leavy = what the water's been doing.
**Guardrails:** Never say "the water is reaching" for her — that sentence is her payoff · the box adds one word, not a confession · no single roll decides belief; **evidence decides, dice color the road** · she is catching up to the party, never informing them · endings stay a menu.

---

## When This Fires *(the gate)*

Two things must be true, or run **The Early Knock** below instead.

1. **They hold the case.** Minimum: the translated chants ([[old-words-handout]] + the Reading, [[reveal-read-alouds]] §4) *or* the Interlock demonstrated (attendance ⇄ [[oswin-cray|Oswin's]] morning-after wall readings) *plus* one more hard piece. Testimony alone — the click, the Communing, dreams — she files under "feelings." *"That's a feeling. I've had feelings for twenty-two years. Bring me the thing I can hold."*
2. **They've earned the door.** Standing is fiction, not a stat — count what's true: Celebrations attended · the Berth Contract settled · edge-families helped by name · Maud's ask brokered (she *sat at the Singing Table when asked* — she remembers who asked) · Cassia's Eddy table · the redline. Two or more and Roll 1 is unnecessary; she makes time. Zero, and this scene isn't available yet — that's correct, not a failure.

**The Early Knock** *(they come before they're ready):* she hears them out — completely, without apology — then asks exactly one question they can't answer yet (*"Who else has heard this?"* or *"What does it cost us if you're right?"*), and ends it: *"Come back when you can answer that. I mean it. Come back."* **Not wasted.** Turn the dial: she starts looking. When they return with the real case, the box is **already on the table** when they walk in. She's been reading it for weeks. Cut Movement 3's fetch and let them see that she got there first — and couldn't take the last step alone.

## Movement 1 — The Door

It's late. No hearing, no benches, no clerks. Read:

> It's dark when she'll finally see you. The Hall's empty — the long benches bare, one lamp lit at the far end of the table. Adaline Roux, sleeves rolled, the city's paperwork stacked around her in neat squared piles. She doesn't get up.
>
> She looks at you the way she looked at you the day you washed in. Completely. Deciding something.
>
> "Sit. You've got the look of people carrying something heavy." She moves one stack aside with two fingers, clearing a space on the table between you. "Put it down where I can see it."

- The empty Hall is deliberate — [[the-hall-of-the-mire|the Weight of the Room]] is **off**. No families, no witnesses. She chose that. Let a player notice it means she suspected what this was.
- If Standing is thin, Roll 1 (Presence, Diff 13) is the scene *getting* this table instead of a public hearing. On a failure, they get the hearing — and the room's disadvantage on proposing change. Let them withdraw and come back right.

## Movement 2 — The Weighing

She listens without interrupting for as long as they talk. Then the questions come — in this order, one at a time, a beat between. She is not stalling. She's doing what she does to every petition: finding out what it's made of.

**Run the countdown:** Progress 4 / Consequence 4. Each hard piece of evidence laid clean = tick Progress, no roll — evidence is evidence. Each straight answer to a question below = tick Progress. Overclaiming ("the city is evil"), demands ("you have to—"), or answering a question with a speech = tick Consequence. Roll 2 (Presence or Knowledge, Diff 13) is the case's *delivery* — tick by outcome (crit 3 · Hope 2 · Fear 1 · failure ticks Consequence instead).

### The Evidence Ledger *(what weighs, what doesn't)*

| Piece | Where it lives | On her scale |
|---|---|---|
| The translated chants — the lexicon, line by line | [[old-words-handout]] · [[reveal-read-alouds]] §4 | **Heavy.** She knows the old words by heart. Watching them turn into *"it shall not pass out from among us"* is watching her own mouth become evidence. |
| The Interlock — attendance ⇄ the wall, morning after | [[world-in-motion]] · Oswin's readings | **Heavy — and hers to verify.** She can check this herself, this week, without trusting anyone. The piece built for a watchful woman. |
| The tether — four people who cannot leave | [[tether-manifestations]] · demonstrable at the boards | **Real.** She's suspected the Mire kept them since the day they arrived. This tells her *what for*. |
| The First Maintainer's journal — Vane's precedent | [[items/the-first-maintainers-journal]] | **Weighs.** A founder-era hand saying founder-era things. Paper, which she respects. |
| The Communing, the click, the dreams | testimony | **Noted, not weighed.** *"That's a feeling. Bring me the thing I can hold."* |
| [[delphine|Delphine's]] corroboration | a name she trusts | **A thumb on the scale.** Not proof — but Delphine doesn't imagine things, and Adaline knows it. |
| The founders' fragment — the word "hold" | **already in her house** | **The keystone.** They can't bring it. She has to go get it. That's Movement 3. |

### Her Four Questions *(scripted — a beat between each)*

1. **"Who else has heard this? Names."** — She's counting how far it's spread and who's carrying it. A straight list ticks Progress. Evasion ticks Consequence — she can smell a managed answer at forty paces.
2. **"Tell me what it costs us if you're right."** *(her canon line — [[voice-cards]])* — The honest answer is enormous: the lumber, the economy, the identity, maybe the wall. A party that says so plainly ticks Progress. A party that minimizes — *"nothing you can't handle"* — ticks Consequence hard. She asked *before* looking because the cost is the part she'll carry.
3. **"Two hundred years of City Leaders. My grandmother sang this at my naming. You're telling me every one of them was — what? Fooled? Lying? Pick a word. Say it to my face."** — **Roll 3 (Presence, Diff 14).** The trap is the two words she offered; the way through is refusing both. *"They were faithful — to a thing that was never told the truth either"* lands. So does *"the founders kept it from them too."* On a hit, this is the moment her hands go flat on the table. On a miss, she stands and looks out at the water for a while — the scene survives, but she finishes it from over there.
4. **"And what do you want me to do with it?"** — The last trap. A demand ticks Consequence. The honest answer — *"we don't know. We thought you should have it"* — is worth two Progress, and it's the answer that decides which door in the menu she walks through. **Mark what they say. It comes back.**

### Fear Menu *(performed — the city intrudes; one, maybe two, never three)*

- **The knock.** A clerk, apologetic, urgent — a berth dispute, a flooded walk, somebody's grandmother. She handles it in forty seconds through the cracked door, kind and absolute, and comes back. The point lands without a word: *this is what she is to sixty thousand people. That's what the truth is asking her to gamble.*
- **The rota.** A drum-keeper arrives about next week's Celebration — torch order, feast tables, the eternal form. She answers from memory, every detail, while the transcription of what the form *means* sits on the table between you. Let the table feel it. Say nothing.
- **The paper.** Caius's newest filing in her stack — she touches it, doesn't open it. *"The Empire asks after our 'infrastructure' again. Everyone wants to know how the city works lately."* The other clock, ticking once, audibly.

## Movement 3 — The Box *(the beat the scene exists for — give it air)*

At Progress 4 she doesn't announce a verdict. She stands, takes the lamp, and is gone for two minutes. Read:

> She comes back carrying a box. Dark wood, salt-stained, a lock older than her office. She sets it on the table like it weighs more than it does.
>
> "Every City Leader gets two things on their first day. The keys to this hall — and this. My predecessor handed it to me the way hers handed it to her. I've read it maybe a hundred times."
>
> One page inside. Water-eaten. Two hundred years old. She doesn't hand it over. She reads it to you — slow, like it's the first time:
>
> *"…to **hold** the great presence, in welcome and peace."*
>
> She stops. Puts one finger on the word. The ink's half gone all around it — the damp ate exactly the part that would settle what it means.
>
> "Twenty-two years, I read that word the kind way."

**Then let her get there. Do not help.** She has every piece now — the party's case plus twenty-two years she's been declining to add up. The click is hers, and she says it staring at the map wall, not at them:

> Her hand goes to the black cord on her wrist. Her grandmother's knot — the outer-boardwalk tie, the one the edge families still use.
>
> "The southwest wall. The relocations. Those families—" [a long pause; she works the knot with her thumb, the way she must have as a girl]
>
> "The water isn't rising, is it. It's *reaching*."

**Then the canon beat, exactly as written in [[adaline-roux|her file]]:** very still. Then —

> "Show me."

Not angry. Not dismissive. The voice of someone who has been deciding not to look at something for twenty-two years and has just been handed what was always going to be there when she did.

- **"Show me" is literal.** She means it. The menu below is *what showing her looks like* — she verifies with her own senses before she decides anything. She is watchful to the last; it's why the city is safe with her.
- Roll 4 (optional): if a PC reads the fragment with her, run [[the-hall-of-the-mire#Features|the Founders' Box]] action as written (Knowledge or Instinct, Diff 12).

## What She Does Next — a menu, never a script

Pick nothing in advance. Weigh Standing plus their answer to Question 4, and let the campaign's hardest lever move the way the table earned. All roads pass through her verifying first: the next Celebration with a tethered PC beside her at open water, or Oswin's wall the morning after — *her* eyes, *her* week.

- **The Address** *(they answered Q4 with her freedom; Standing high)* — She verifies, and then she does the thing only she can do: stands in front of Sallowreach and says *"I need to tell you something about the Celebration."* The truth-and-politics lever ([[resolution-options]]) goes live, at full cost, with consent. This is the hardest ending to reach in the campaign. If the table walks it, they've won something no dice could give them.
- **The Steward's Bargain** *(the middle road — most tables land here)* — She believes, and she asks for **time**. Per house rule, never yes/no: negotiate *terms*. One more Celebration while she reads everything. Delphine and Caspar brought in under seal. The party's silence as collateral. And the cost is the horror of it: **she must keep singing the lock while she decides** — and she knows what the words mean now. Whether the party grants her that is a real decision with a real cost. Charge it.
- **The Legitimate Refusal** *(they demanded; or the cost landed harder than the wrong)* — She believes them. **And she keeps the covenant anyway** — until someone shows her what letting go costs sixty thousand people. *"You've shown me what this is. Nobody's shown me what stopping it does."* She is not a villain; she's the Tallykeeper pattern — a claim the party can't call unjust. She becomes the campaign's last, best argument to answer: bring her a resolution that protects the city ([[resolution-options]] — through-Oswin, the controlled quieting) and she opens the door herself.
- **The Broken Steward** *(Consequence ran high but Progress got there; the case landed cruel)* — She believes, and it takes the floor out. Twenty-two years of devotion recast as jailkeeping in one night. She goes quiet for days; the Hall runs on clerks; the city notices. The party made the strongest person in Sallowreach need help — now that's their job, and [[delphine]] or [[caspar-renne|Caspar]] is where it starts.

**Bank this — stage two, whatever she chose:** the next Celebration, the party watching her. Deep Mire green, gold thread, hands over heart, singing every word perfectly — **eyes open**. Because until the city decides, or she does, the covenant is still hers to keep. Don't narrate her feelings. The table knows what she knows. That's the whole gut-punch.
