# Set Piece — They Give Us the Night *(Isotta at the Hall of the Mire, the day before)*

*Isotta's slot on the S8 town day ([[session-08-run-sheet]]) — the day before the Celebration. She goes to [[the-hall-of-the-mire|the Hall of the Mire]] to ask [[adaline-roux|Adaline]] what the Celebration is for, carrying the [[the-tallykeeper|Tallykeeper]]'s warning from S7 ([[set-piece-she-lets-me-win]] Drop 3: *"On that night, they go."*). Built 2026-09-28. **3 rolls. ~25–30 min.** The rule of the scene: **people give her the frustration, things give her the carrots.***

---

## 🧭 In Plain Words

Isotta is a player character who is secretly a ghost, and the only person in town who can see the town's dead. Last session, a death-spirit told her that every ghost and spirit in the city clears out on festival night, like animals running before a storm. The festival is tomorrow. Today she goes to the town hall to ask the mayor what the festival is actually for. The living people there are warm, busy, and no help at all — they love the festival and have no idea what it does. But three things she finds on her own pay off. She watches the town's dead start walking out of the city, and there are more of them at the town hall than anywhere she has ever counted. She finds an old book where every mayor for two hundred years signed for the same three things — keys, a seal, and a locked box — and the very first mayor got them from nobody. And she watches crates of expensive food arrive for tomorrow's feast with no name on them, sent by the city's quiet crime boss, who pays for the feast in secret.

## 👁️ What You See *(two layers — read the first to the table, the second to Isotta)*

> So the Hall of the Mire's right in the middle of town, up on stilts, and the big doors are wide open today.
>
> There's people going in and out with their arms full — lantern frames, tablecloths, and there's a kid carrying a stack of bowls right up to her chin.
>
> Inside it's just one long room. Benches down both sides, and the floor's worn pale down the middle, like a path through grass.
>
> There's a guy up a ladder stringing lanterns across the rafters, and somebody underneath yelling that they're crooked.
>
> It smells like fresh bread in here. Somebody up in the rafters is laughing.
>
> A clerk with ink on his cuffs waves you in without even looking up. "Guests are welcome. Mind the ladder."

**Then, to Isotta:**

> Isotta — that's what everybody else sees.
>
> *[beat]*
>
> You see the dead.
>
> There's more of them here than anywhere you've ever counted. They're on the steps, they're along the rail, and a couple are just standing between the benches like they showed up early for a meeting.
>
> And they're leaving.
>
> Nobody's running. They're just… going. Like folks clearing out of a house before a storm comes in.
>
> And they're all going the same way. Out. Away from here.
>
> An old man in a work coat comes down the steps right past you. He doesn't look at you. Keeps walking.

⚠ **That much is free — no roll.** "The dead are thickest at the Hall" is pattern one of her count ([[isotta-arc]] §The Standing Count), and the arc rule is that it's *hers to find*, never rolled for. Say what she sees. Let the player say what it means.

🚫 **What NOT to say:** "they're fleeing the Hall" · "something here scares them" · anything about what's under the floor. Direction only.

## 👻 Beat 1 — The Dead Are Leaving *(the centerpiece — only she sees it)*

🎲 **Instinct 13** — counting them properly while they move:

| Roll | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Success w/ Hope** | A clean count, dropping while she writes it. **And then:** the floor-look, below. |
| **Success w/ Fear** | A clean count — but she's been standing on the steps counting at nothing, and the clerk saw. He tells Adaline. **Beat 3 opens with Adaline:** *"Florent says you've been counting my stairs."* |
| **Failure w/ Hope** | The number won't hold still — "a lot, and fewer every minute." And one of them walks straight through her on the way out. **Mark a Stress.** Cold to the bone. |
| **Failure w/ Fear** | Same, **mark the Stress** — and she flinches at nothing in front of a family hauling tables. The clerk: *"You all right, miss? You look like you lost something."* He tells Adaline, as above. |

🔥 **The Hearth-Warm Briquette:** if she closes her hand on it, the cold passes in a minute instead of an hour. **The Stress stays marked** — otherwise the failure costs nothing. It's the first warm thing she's felt all day, and that's what Chauncey gave it to her for.

**The number** — match it to her sheet. Suggested: **38 when she starts, 29 when she's done.** The shape is what matters: about a third of every dead person she's ever counted is standing around this one building, and it's going down while she watches.

**The floor-look** *(Success w/ Hope only — the one image this scene spends)*:

> One of them stops, right in the doorway. A woman.
>
> She looks down at the floor. Not at you — at the floor.
>
> *[let it sit. count three.]*
>
> Then she goes too.

🚫 **What NOT to say:** anything. Not who she was, not why she looked. *(GM only: the Hall sits on the city's first pilings, and the drowned tree is straight down — [[set-piece-the-honored-guest]]. This is a Stage-1-safe pointer: a direction, never a fact.)*

## 📖 Beat 2 — The Handover Book *(her deduction puzzle)*

**The shape, for the GM:** the Hall keeps almost nothing about the Celebration. If she asks for festival records, the clerk — **Florent Guidry**, forties, ink to the elbows, cheerful — says: *"That's the Accord's business. They don't write much down either."* Then, helpful: *"What we've got is the Book."* It's the oldest thing in the Hall.

🎲 **Presence 13** — getting the Book. **The room rewards respect:** if she asks to *understand the tradition so she does tomorrow right*, give her **advantage** and say why out loud ([[the-hall-of-the-mire]] — *Two Hundred Years of Tradition*).

| Roll | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Success w/ Hope** | Florent brings it with both hands and leaves her alone with it. |
| **Success w/ Fear** | He brings it — and mentions to Adaline, in passing, that the guest is reading the Book. Adaline walks into Beat 3 already knowing. |
| **Failure w/ Hope** | *"That's the City Leader's to say."* He goes to ask. Adaline brings it herself, sets it down, and goes back to work. But she saw who asked. |
| **Failure w/ Fear** | Adaline brings it herself **and stays.** She stands at Isotta's shoulder while she reads, and sees which page Isotta stops on. |

> Florent sets it down with both hands. It's big — wooden covers, and the leather's gone soft as an old glove.
>
> Every page is one City Leader. There's a name, a year, and a signature.
>
> And under every name it's the same line. Different handwriting every time, but the same words:
>
> "Received of—" whoever had the job before— "the keys, the seal, and the box."
>
> Adaline's is the last page. Her handwriting's small and neat. Same line.
>
> The front of the book's the oldest. Those pages are going brown.

🎲 **Knowledge 13** — reading it:

| Roll | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Success w/ Hope** | She finds page one herself, alone — **and has time to copy it onto her sheet, word for word, dash and all.** That's the object she carries out. |
| **Success w/ Fear** | She finds page one — but Florent needs the table for tomorrow. No time to copy it. She has to carry it in her head. |
| **Failure w/ Hope** | She gets the repeating line, but page one's ink is too far gone. Florent leans over and reads it out for her — then shuts the Book. *"That's enough of that, we need the table."* No second look. |
| **Failure w/ Fear** | Florent reads it out loud enough that Adaline hears it across the room. She comes over and closes the Book herself: *"That's the office's book. I'll take it."* She carries it off. **Beat 3 starts with Adaline already guarded about the box.** |

**Page one:**

> Page one. The ink's gone brown as tea.
>
> "Received of—"
>
> *[stop.]*
>
> And then nothing. Just a dash, where the name should be.
>
> "—the keys, the seal, and the box."

**Florent, if he reads it** *(the fallback)*: *"Received of… nobody. Huh."* [beat] *"That's the first one. It's always been like that."*

🚫 **What NOT to say:** "founders" · "so the box is older than the city" · what's in it. The dash is a dash. The player does the arithmetic.

### 🧩 GM crib *(the puzzle is hers — don't solve it for her)*

- **Sixteen pages, sixteen City Leaders,** about two hundred years. Adaline is the sixteenth.
- **The keys and the seal are decoys.** The Hall's doors, the city's seal. Ordinary. The box is the only one of the three nobody explains. She has to notice the odd one out herself.
- **The dash:** the first leader signed for the box from *nobody*. The book starts after the thing it records. *(Same shape as the Accord's ledger, which [[sinope|Sinope]] brings up at the Celebration — that's this clue's second pass, free, tomorrow.)*
- **The box is real.** It's the Founders' Box ([[the-hall-of-the-mire]] §The Founders' Box). **Its contents stay gated on standing** — see Guardrails.

## 🏛️ Beat 3 — Adaline and the Crates *(the frustration, then the Marcel clue)*

**The shape, for the GM:** Adaline is warm, unhurried, and satisfied with every answer. That's the frustration, and it's the point — but **keep it short.** Three satisfied answers, then fire the crates. Two things in this beat aren't answers: the pause at the box, and the crates.

> Adaline Roux comes down the room with her sleeves rolled up — list in one hand, pencil behind her ear — and people just step out of her way without being asked.
>
> She sees you, and she stops. And you get her whole attention, like there's nothing else going on today.
>
> "Our guest." *[beat]* "What do you need?"

*(If a roll sent Florent to her first, swap in her opener from that row.)*

🎙️ **Her tic: she finishes every answer.** Full stops. No trailing off, and she **never says "I don't know."** *(Sinope says it tomorrow — "a city that never says 'I don't know'" — and Adaline is the proof.)* The one exception is the box.

### The answer cards *(pull whichever Isotta asks for)*

- **What's it for?** — *"It's a covenant. With what watches over this city. We keep our side. Every week, two hundred years."*
- **What do the old words mean?** — *"Nobody's spoken that language in two hundred years."* [smiles] *"You don't have to understand a thank-you to mean it."*
- **Why can't it change?** — *"Because the form is the point. Care, repeated — that's what makes it real. You don't honor something by deciding you understand it better than everyone who came before you."* *(Save "You just keep faith" for her ten seconds at the Celebration.)*
- **The dead / the spirits leave the city for it** — she's *charmed*, not troubled: *"Of course they give us the night."* [beat] *"It's ours."*
- **Who started it?** — *"The first families. Founding year."*
- ⭐ **The box** — **the pause.** One beat, no more. Then: *"Old papers. An heirloom of the office."*
- ⭐ **Can I see it?** — warm: *"No."* [beat] *"That's not a no forever. It's a no from someone who hasn't known you long."* *(That tells the table what opens it: standing.)*
- **Pushes a second time** — **spend a Fear: Adaline Goes Still** ([[the-hall-of-the-mire]]). She goes very still. *"Not today."* And she's back to her list. The door closes for today, not for good.

🚫 **What NOT to say:** a second sentence after the box pause. The pause *is* the clue; anything she adds explains it.

### ⭐ The crates *(fire these whenever the questions start to grind)*

> Two porters come in the side door with a crate between them. Then another one. Then, honestly, four more.
>
> There's no name on them. No mark, nothing. And nobody signs for anything.
>
> Florent pries one open to check — little paper twists of saffron, bottles of that dark sweet wine. The kind of stuff the poor end of town never sees.
>
> "Outer tables," he says. "Same as always."

- **Asks the porters:** *"Where it always comes from, miss."* They're gone before the next question.
- **Asks Florent:** *"Comes every week. Never a name."*
- **Asks Adaline** — the only thing in the Hall she says quietly: *"Monsieur Dray is generous."* [beat] *"He prefers we don't say so."*

🚫 **What NOT to say:** why he gives · what he runs · anything about the spear. Adaline says his name and stops. *(GM: [[marcel-dray|Marcel Dray]] — the city's banker and back-channel boss, and the man holding the spear the party needs. He feeds the feast tables anonymously; that's canon in his file. **Leavy tastes this same saffron at the gumbo tables tomorrow** — [[session-05-first-celebration]] Beat 2. That's the second pass.)*

## 💡 If They Get Clever

- **Follows the dead:** out to the last boardwalks, then out over the open water. She loses them in the reeds. They don't come back today.
- **Talks to one:** they don't stop. An old woman, kind, hurrying: *"Not now, dear. Not today."* That's all. They don't know why either — they just go.
- ⭐ **Looks for the ones who stay** *(the Tallykeeper told her "Nine stay")*: every ghost she can see is leaving. **She finds none staying.** Let the question hang — the answer is tomorrow night, at the chant ([[session-05-first-celebration]] Beat 5.5b).
- **Uses Basile's Ivory Domino on one:** it works on *spirits*; these are the town's dead ([[cosmology]] — ghosts ≠ spirits). The tile clicks down. Nothing changes. They keep walking.
- **Reaches down through the floor:** far below, something warm. Like a stove through a floorboard. **Nothing else** — no light, no voice. The rest keeps for the dive.
- **Hunts for the box / tries to take it:** it isn't in any room she can reach, and the Hall is packed with families hauling lanterns. Getting caught costs the party's honored-guest welcome — the only thing that ever opens it.
- **Asks Florent about the box:** *"Only the City Leader opens that."* [beat] *"Never asked. Why would I?"*
- **Asks Adaline about Caspar or the museum:** it's settled — the mimic. And one human line: *"I miss him today especially. He'd have been up that ladder."*
- **Asks where the Celebration's records are:** *"The Accord keeps a book. Ask their steward tomorrow."* *(That's Tenson's Find at the Celebration — pointing at it is fine; don't open it here.)*

## 🚫 Guardrails

- **Nobody alive knows.** Adaline is complete and satisfied. One pause, at the box. No other cracks.
- **Describe, don't conclude.** "They're all going the same way. Out." is what she sees. "Something's under the Hall" is what the player says — or doesn't.
- **No eleven today.** If she looks for them, she finds nothing.
- **The box stays shut.** Its existence is the carrot; its contents are earned later.
- **The book never says "founders."** The dash is a dash.
- **Keep the frustration short.** Three satisfied answers, then the crates.

## 🎒 Walking Out

- **Tally sheet:** write in the eve count — or *"a lot, falling."* Stress and Hope from the rolls.
- **The copied line** — if she got it, it's on her sheet now. Log it.
- ⭐ **She knows the box exists.** Log whether she asked to see it, and whether Adaline went still (Front 8, [[world-in-motion]]).
- **Log what Adaline saw** — whether Florent told her about the stairs, whether she stood at Isotta's shoulder, whether she took the Book away.
- **Into tomorrow:** she's watched the dead leave. The empty streets won't surprise her now. The ones who stayed will.
