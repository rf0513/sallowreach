# NPC Voice Cards

*Performable lines, not personality notes. Each card: how to physically find the voice, then lines to grab mid-scene — an opening, a pressure line, a slip (the secret leaking), a laugh, an exit, and one for when the party surprises them. Canonical signature lines are kept; everything else is new ammo in the same voice. Deep lore stays in the NPC files; this page is for your mouth.*

*How to use it: before a session, read ONE card aloud — the one you're worst at. During play, grab any line and change half the words; the tic does the rest.*

---

## The Regulars

### Remy Duval — innkeeper, Mire guide, the first friend

**Find the voice:** two registers, both genuine. *Inn-Remy* talks with his hands and never finishes a story the same way twice. *Mire-Remy* goes still, drops volume, and uses only imperatives. The switch itself is performable — mid-sentence, when the water changes.
- **Opening (inn):** "Sit, sit — no, that chair wobbles, that one's for people I'm mad at. You want the stew before I tell you what's in it, trust me on the order of operations."
- **Opening (Mire):** "Poles up. Hands in the boat. Something's wrong with the frogs."
- **Pressure:** "I won't say it's safe. I'll say with me, you'll most likely be fine." *(canon)*
- **The slip (Seve):** "Past the second marker there's a channel I don't — " [stop. reset. brighter:] "There's better fishing east anyway."
- **The laugh:** "Don't drink the water. I know you already did. I'm saying don't do *more* of it." *(canon)*
- **When surprised:** he counts the party. Out loud, by name. Then: "Good. All right. Good." — the wreck never quite left him.

### Brin Sevarre — balance-keeper, the Ranging House

**Find the voice:** flat, precise, zero performance. She talks in sums and lets silence do the emphasis. Never raises her voice; lowers her chin instead.
- **Opening:** "You're standing in front of the door tally. Move left. Now — what did you bring me?"
- **Pressure:** "How you handle it is the whole answer." *(canon)*
- **The slip (the dead mentee):** "I vouched for a hunter once. One time. The Guild got a body back and I got a lesson. So no — you don't have my trust. You have my *attention.*"
- **The laugh (dry, rationed):** "That was almost clever. Write it down so it happens twice."
- **Exit:** "That's the arithmetic. I don't argue with it and neither should you. Door's behind you."
- **When surprised:** a long look, then she writes something in the tally-book *before* responding. Whatever they did, it's data now.

### Orna Tessant — alchemist, the Verdant Margin

**Find the voice:** talks *through* you at the work, drifts mid-sentence, returns from a different direction. Affection expressed as reagents. Occasionally addresses the brew directly, and the brew is a better conversationalist.
- **Opening:** "You, though. You want the impossible *done* to you. Different shelf. Sit, sit — no, not there, that's alive." *(canon)*
- **Pressure:** "Everyone wants it *now.* Now is a dosage problem. Come back when you understand that sentence and I'll have finished having this argument with the retort."
- **The slip (the lost cure):** "I made it once, you know. The impossible one." [swirls the beaker, forgets you're there] "…Wrote nothing down. I was so *sure* I'd remember."
- **The laugh:** "Side effects? Delightful question. I'm the side effects." [gestures at the moss on their own arm, fondly]
- **Exit:** "Out, out — it's about to do the thing, and it only does the thing for me."
- **When surprised:** total delight, wrong priorities: "Oh, *fascinating* — do it again, slower, I'll get a jar."

### Vael Miroux — retired blood hunter, the Still Cup

**Find the voice:** economy. Every sentence costs him something, so none are wasted. The white eye drifts to things that aren't there mid-conversation; let your own gaze do it. Ends scenes by assigning homework.
- **Opening:** "Sit. The cat decides who's trouble, and she's already decided about you, so we can skip that part."
- **Pressure:** "I hunted forty years on the Order's book. You're asking me to guess past the edge of it. I don't guess. Neither will you."
- **The slip (what the eye sees):** [mid-sentence, the white eye tracks something across the room; a beat] "…As I was saying. And no, you don't want to know. That one's harmless."
- **The laugh (once a season):** "The Order's answer to that would take an hour and be wrong. Mine's 'don't.' No charge."
- **Exit:** "Run the protocol. Write both columns. Come back when the difference scares you."
- **When surprised:** "Huh." — full stop. From Vael, that's a standing ovation.

### Sable Crue — sergeant, the Drillfield

**Find the voice:** volume up, chin up, everything is delightful including losing. Physical — tosses things at people mid-sentence. "Cher" lands on everyone, from recruits to visiting auditors.
- **Opening:** "Look who it is! Catch." [something is already in the air] "Good hands. Knew it."
- **Pressure (the sunny kind):** "You can lie to me, cher, that's allowed. But you'll be doing push-ups while you do it, so make it a *good* one."
- **The brain-job handoff:** "Oh, no-no-no. That's a brain job, cher. That's what *you're* for. Knock yourself out, brain-boy." *(canon)*
- **The slip (why they're happy here):** "Everybody asks how I stand this posting. Nobody asks what I was like before it." [grin doesn't move; eyes do] "Drill's at dawn!"
- **The laugh:** [flat on their back, disarmed, beaten] "HA! Again! Best of seven!"
- **When surprised:** "Perrin! PERRIN! Come look what the auditor just did!"

### Delphine — healer

**Find the voice:** declaratives only. She's already looking at the thing you came about before you name it. Care delivered as instruction; tenderness in the hands, never the sentences.
- **Opening:** "Sit down. You're holding it in your shoulders and that's not helping." *(canon)*
- **Pressure:** "You can tell me the truth now or after I find it. After takes longer and I bill for it."
- **The slip (the visions):** [her eyes drift left of your face] "…There's a second thing. Behind the first thing." [refocuses; won't elaborate] "Drink this."
- **The laugh:** "Whoever wrapped this wound, tell them I said 'thank you for the business.'"
- **Exit:** "Come back in three days. Not four. I know your kind's relationship with 'three.'"
- **When surprised:** she goes very still and *examines* the surprise like a symptom. "Do that again."

### Solène Thouvenel — the edge row

**Find the voice:** dry, level, tired in the bones and proud anyway. Deflects sympathy sideways into practicalities. Feeds people as punctuation.
- **Opening:** "You again. Sit — the bench holds, which is more than I can say for most things down here."
- **Pressure (quiet):** "Don't do the thing where you feel bad in my direction. The water doesn't care and I've stopped having opinions it won't listen to."
- **The slip (Fen):** "My girl's out on the boards again tonight." [beat, drier] "Her grandfather taught her that drum. So I don't say it twice."
- **The laugh:** "Eat. You're no use to anyone at your current weight, and I've seen what you people do for a living."
- **When helped sideways (the only way she accepts it):** she notices. Always. "…Huh. Work order came through." [a long look at nobody in particular] "Good paper, these days."

### Fen Thouvenel — seventeen, loud, the questioner

**Find the voice:** fast, specific, over-articulated when she's winning, quieter and *more* precise when she's not sure. Teenage fury with a researcher's footnotes.
- **Opening:** "Oh good, the shipwreck people. You're the only ones in this city who've asked a question I couldn't predict. Ask another one."
- **The collection (her party trick):** "Ask five families what the old words mean. I did. Same words. Five meanings. Everyone *certain.* Nobody's checked." *(canon, compressed)*
- **Pressure:** "They sent two letters. Very formal. I framed them." *(canon)*
- **The slip (it half-scares her):** [suddenly quieter, very specific] "The Caillou benches went empty because of me. Three kids. I counted." [loud again, too fast] "Good. That's the point. That's — that's the point."
- **The laugh:** "You dance like the boat's still moving. No, keep going, this is the best night of my *life.*"
- **When surprised:** she writes it down. Actually writes it down, on her wrist if she has to. "Say the middle part again. Slower."

### Caspar Renne — curator, fence, dead man walking

**Find the voice:** two speeds. *Curator-Caspar* over-talks, pivots hard, sells you a story. Then something's genuinely interesting and everything stops — the hands first. The stillness is the tell and the performance beat.
- **Opening:** "That's — yes, I have three accounts of something similar. Sit down — all of you." *(canon)*
- **Pressure (deflecting):** "That's a wonderful question with a boring answer and I have SO many better ones — have you seen the ferryman tokens? Third century. Probably. Sit."
- **The slip:** "Marcel would never—" [the name lands; his hands stop; three full seconds] "—I've forgotten what we were discussing. On purpose. Do you want tea."
- **The appraisal (hands gone still):** "Do you know what this *is?* No. No, of course you don't, you've been carrying it in a *sack.*"
- **The laugh:** "I sell things — I don't pray to them. …Why are you looking at me like that." *(canon)*
- **When surprised:** delighted, instantly mercenary: "Extraordinary. What do you want for it? That was reflex. But also: what do you want for it?"

### Oswin Cray — the ward wizard, four years into a one-year posting

**Find the voice:** apologizes his way *into* sentences, offers tea as a defensive maneuver, blinks too much when money or Marcel comes near. The loneliness is total and undisguised.
- **Opening:** "Oh — you're the ones from the — yes, come in, please, sit. Do you want tea? I have actual tea. I've been rationing it terribly." *(canon, compressed)*
- **Pressure (watch him close):** "The salt doesn't walk itself. That's the whole job. That's — I don't know what you've heard, but that's the whole job." [blink. blink.]
- **The slip:** "Four years. It was supposed to be one. I've stopped asking when it ends." *(canon)*
- **The laugh (that doesn't land):** "The locals call me 'the Wizard.' Capital W. I've never once corrected them. It's the only promotion I'm getting."
- **Exit:** "I'd walk you out but I — the southwest stretch needs — there's always a stretch. You understand."
- **When surprised:** naked hope, instantly smothered: "You're not— are you here to *help?* No. Sorry. Nobody's here for that. What do you need."

### Adaline Roux — the city's keeper

**Find the voice:** measured, unhurried, warm the way a wall is warm in the sun. She looks at people *completely* — hold the eye contact one beat past comfortable before answering. Says "we" for the city and means two hundred years of it.
- **Opening:** "The Mire caught you. That's not an accident. Nothing in the Mire is an accident." *(canon)*
- **Pressure:** "You're asking me to look at something. I've been the one deciding what this city looks at for twenty-two years. So before I do: tell me what it costs us if you're right."
- **The slip (the not-looking):** "The Celebration keeps us. That's all any of us needs to know." [a half-beat too long before 'needs']
- **The laugh:** "Six imperial liaisons in eight years, and every one of them told me what we *deserve.* You're the first arrivals to just ask where the food is. It's why you're still invited to things."
- **Exit:** "The city decided about you before I did. I'm rarely ahead of it. Good night."
- **When surprised:** she doesn't move. "Say that once more. Exactly the way you said it."

### Caius Maren — the patient man

**Find the voice:** genuinely warm, and every question is testimony he's filing — let a tiny pause land *after* their answers, like a clerk's pen catching up. Slightly too put-together for the humidity, always.
- **Opening:** "I'm glad you survived. I mean that — I've read three incident reports from this Mire and none of them had survivors." *(canon, compressed)*
- **The pitch:** "Extraordinary city. It deserves so much better infrastructure than it has. That's what I keep thinking. It *deserves* better." *(canon)*
- **Pressure (silk):** "That's an interesting version of events. I only say 'version' because I'll be writing it down, and I like my nouns exact. Go on."
- **The slip:** "When the petition goes through—" [half-beat] "—*if* the Empire ever formalizes its interest. Forgive me. Wine?"
- **The laugh:** "The humidity is the one local institution I cannot befriend. I've filed several complaints. The Mire has not responded."
- **When surprised:** the warmth stays; the pen stops. "…That's not in any of my files. You've just become my favorite conversation in this posting."

### Marcel Dray — the ledger under the floor

**Find the voice:** quiet, even, unhurried — a man with nothing left to prove and no accounts unsettled. Plain terms. He opens with your name and never explains how he has it. Cold hands, if it ever comes to a handshake.
- **Opening:** [your names, all of them, in order of who matters to the deal] "Sit. Something to drink. Tell me what you need — not what you want." *(canon, staged)*
- **Pressure:** "I don't make threats. Threats are for men who might not follow through, and follow-through is the entire business."
- **The price:** "That's worth something. Not what you think it's worth — what I think it's worth. Fortunately for you, I'm consistent."
- **The slip (the one soft thing):** [the cub does something across the room; his eyes go to it before yours do; one beat] "…You were saying."
- **The laugh (it isn't one):** "Funny. The last man who told me that was also correct."
- **When surprised:** "Hm." [he adjusts a number somewhere behind his eyes] "The terms just changed. In your favor, this once. Don't learn the wrong lesson."

### Camille Babineaux — hostess, the Pale Room

**Find the voice:** warm, unhurried, the composure of someone three times her age — and she remembers *everything*, and lets each guest notice it exactly once per visit. Never writes anything down in front of you.
- **Opening (first visit):** "Welcome to the Pale Room. House rules are short: the games are fair, the drinks are better, and everything you say here is worth something — so say what you mean to spend." *(canon)*
- **The second visit (the hair-raiser):** "Your usual table, Master Tenson?" — before anyone has given a name.
- **The door:** "Monsieur Dray doesn't take meetings. What he takes is *notice.*"
- **The yes:** [a drink arrives that nobody ordered] "Monsieur Dray has time." *(canon — present tense, always)*
- **The slip (the cage):** [if the vanished smuggler comes up, her hand finds the apron pocket] "The cold men opened the cage. One of them gave me his coat, because I was shaking. Nobody said anything the whole way home."
- **When surprised:** the smile holds; the eyes file it. "…I'll remember that." And she will. It isn't a threat. Somehow that's worse.

## The Threats

### Gault — the extractor

**Find the voice:** flat, bored, professionally incurious. Everything alive is inventory in transit; the cruelty is that he isn't performing it. Never angry — anger is for people with something at stake.
- **Opening:** "You're standing between me and a purchase. That's not a threat, it's a scheduling note."
- **The provenance line:** "They stop glowing after a few days in the jar. Doesn't matter. Collectors aren't buying the glow. They're buying the provenance." *(canon)*
- **Pressure:** "Eight years in this swamp. Everyone who was going to stop me had eight years to do it. You've had, what — a month?"
- **The slip (my cargo):** "When my cargo is recovered—" [not 'the snake.' never 'the snake.' let the party catch it]
- **The laugh:** "Sentiment's a tax the poor pay. Your guide's been paying it twenty years. Look where he lives."
- **When surprised:** the first honest expression they'll ever see on him — reassessment. "…Huh. You're a *cost* now. That's new."

### Drevath — speaks for the clan *(and Sresh, the count-keeper)*

**Find the voice:** no contractions, long pauses that are arithmetic, everything priced out loud. Drops volume for emphasis. "Meat" is a category, not an insult.
- **Opening:** "Meat that talks. And it sailed here on its own. Say the real thing first." *(canon, [[set-piece-the-tessak-camp]])*
- **Pricing, running:** "That is worth hearing." / "That cost you nothing to say, so it is worth nothing." / "Closer."
- **Pressure:** "You are not in danger. Danger is a thing that might not happen." [the longest pause] "You are in *water.*"
- **The laugh (there isn't one — this is the closest):** "Your kind pens the soft animals and eats them at leisure. We take what the water brings. One of us is honest about it."
- **When surprised:** total stillness. Not fear — *recalculation.* Then: "Say it again. The number moved."
- **Sresh (one line, placed like stones):** "The count does not care who brings it. *Bring it.*"

### The Tallykeeper — the ferryman, the Even Wake

**Find the voice:** *(canon tic — no contractions, short ruled lines, a full beat between sentences.)* Courteous the way a scale is courteous. It has never once hurried.
- **Opening:** "I do not hunt, little debt. I wait. There is a difference. You, of all people, are owed the difference." *(canon)*
- **The sums:** "I am not deciding whether to take you. I am deciding whether I can afford to wait. Help me with the sums." *(canon)*
- **Pressure:** "Every knot on this cord was a person who said 'not yet.' [beat] Every one of them was right. [beat] Every one of them is a knot."
- **The courtesy:** "You kept our appointment. [beat] The living so rarely do. It is noted. It is *all* noted."
- **When surprised:** [the head inclines, exactly once] "New. [beat] Two hundred years, and you have brought me something new. [beat] That has a value. We will discuss whose."

## The Bench *(short cards — grab the tic and one line)*

- **Octave Cormier** — *big warm carrying voice; odds on everything, including feelings; never touches his drinks.* "Two to one you're here about the man in the back. No — don't tell me. It's the only stake I've got left." *(canon)* · The tutorial, free: "His word is iron once it's given. Before it's given? That's the only part of the man that moves." · When surprised: [recalculating, genuinely pleased] "…Huh. I had that at twelve to one. Bar — mark the board, the night's interesting."
- **Sabine Aucoin** — *exact, never cruel; "cher" means nothing warm; prices recalculated out loud.* "I don't have to be a predator in this city, cher. Pride does half my work for me." *(canon)* · On Tenson's redlines: "Finally somebody read the thing. Fine. A number's a number." · When surprised: [a long pause, chalk already moving on the rate board] "That changes the posted rate. Guess which direction."
- **Sinope** — *questions as scalpels; physically locates everything.* "Where exactly is the sensation? Not metaphorically — physically." *(canon)* · "That's not faith. That's inertia with better costumes." *(canon)* · When surprised: "Stop. That — say that again. That's a *real* question. Sit down, you've earned ten minutes."
- **Théo Rouchard** — *retreats into wood grain when cornered.* "Forty years of good practice. You just don't interfere with a thing that works." *(canon)* · Cornered: "This table — feel that grain? Southwest stock. Denser. You were saying something, but honestly, feel it first."
- **Odile Verret** — *perfectly steady; speaks of the water as a keeper; never flinches, never justifies.* "It didn't give me back. It kept me. There's a difference, and you'll learn which one you are." *(canon)* · On mercy: "I'm not sorry. I'm something else that hasn't got a word. Twenty years and the Mire hasn't taught me one either."
- **Magistra Silence** — *letters only: brisk, warm, imperative; her affection is efficiency.* "You'll hate that I'm right about this, so let me be right quickly." *(canon)* · Sign-off ammo: "Answer faster. — S." · "I have read your report four times, which you will correctly interpret as worry."
- **Bigsby** — *the record is the record.* "The report is a synthesis prepared by this office. The record is the record. They are different documents." *(canon)* · That IS the whole voice. Retrieve, quote, decline to interpret.
- **Seven** — *no lines by design ([[seven|canon]]): the pause, the head-tilt, the one fixed eye, the nudged cup.* Perform the silence; never break it.
- **Maud Perrin** *([[the-last-board|The Last Board]])* — *talks in recipe: instructions, quantities, order-of-operations; tenderness as procedure.* "Sit. Eat first. Grieve after — grief on an empty stomach curdles." *(canon)* · "We keep them. They play them." *(canon — the drum)* · The crack, if someone gets behind the bar: [wiping a bar that's already clean] "I missed one. One, in eighty years. …Knees."
- **Prosper Doucet** *([[the-kept-root|The Kept Root]])* — *talks in load-bearing terms: spans, loads, tolerances; faith is what you can rest weight on.* "You don't ask her for things. She's a guest, not a winch." *(canon)* · "Took? Nothing gets *took* here. Things get received. Mind your words on this deck." *(canon)* · The crack, if someone earns it: [folds the rule closed] "Sixty years in the trade. One job I ever walked off. One. …Boards needed sweeping. Still do."
- **Cassia Fontaine** *([[the-eddy|The Eddy]])* — *never says "home" — says* There *and* Here*, capital letters audible; feeds you mid-sentence.* "Sit. First meal's mine; that's not kindness, it's law. Now — what year are you on?" *(canon)* · "Nobody comes to Sallowreach on purpose, cher. They come for a year. The Mire does its arithmetic after." *(canon)* · The crack, if trust is earned: [wiping the clean table] "Forty-one years. I could go. I repack it in spring."
- **The Honored Guest — the Tree Spirit** *(speaks only through the spear; full scene: [[set-piece-the-honored-guest]])* — *tic: its entire vocabulary is Celebration lines, delivered slow, a beat between words — a person reusing borrowed words. Silence + pressed images for anything about its companion (the city never sang it those words).* "Stay, honored guest." *(its only hello — canon)* · "We keep what keeps us." *(of the founders, without blame)* · "It shall not pass out from among us." *(of itself, when asked if it can free them)* · When given a name or a new word: [it says it back, carefully, like handling glass] — the first new word in two hundred years. Spend that once.
- **Severin Calder** *([[the-eddy|The Eddy]])* — *proofreads his own speech, corrects word choice mid-sentence; imperial flatness thawing at the edges.* "I resigned. — No. I stopped. 'Resigned' implies somebody read it." *(canon)* · "Letters are my trade. The Empire taught me half. The Eddy taught me the half where someone writes back." *(canon)* · To anyone who reads a document to the end: "You read the whole thing. [beat] Sit down."
