## Key Decisions Made *(history log — trace a ruling's origin here. The enforceable distillation is CLAUDE.md § The Iron Rules; read that instead on a cold start.)*

- **No ticking clock.** Players should not feel rushed. Urgency comes from personal stakes (where each PC was going), not a countdown. The world still *moves* — see [[world-in-motion]] for the living fronts the GM advances, slows, or redraws based on what players do. Fronts, not a doom timer.
- **Personal tether manifestations** — ~~held in reserve until PCs are known~~ **BUILT 2026-07-11 → [[tether-manifestations]]** (the welcome-not-punishment unifier, per-PC signature/cost/gift, the Comparison scene, the Edge, Fear hooks).

Session 1 gets a **Tessak Scout** (`adversaries/sallowreach-roster.md`) as the bank-beat after the gar swim. **✅ RESOLVED 2026-07-11:** `lantern-rays.md` fully rewritten (the détente, Sresh the count-keeper, the wreck-supply deal, three diplomacy levers, no sick-children path), `gault.md` Encounter 2 recut, and the encounter built table-ready as [[set-piece-the-tessak-camp]] ("Meat That Talks" — the Weighing countdown, performed Fear menu, the fight if it breaks). ## SRD Facts — Verified 2026-07-06 *(official SRD; reuse these when resolving the remaining `[VERIFY]` tags — session-critical files S4/S5 + the-celebration swept earlier; **[[fletcher-arc]] and [[mire-complications]] swept clean 2026-07-17** against this section. Biggest remaining debtors: [[items/loot-ledger|loot-ledger]] (14) · [[set-piece-double-knot]] (6) · session-04-maintenance-house (6))*

- **Difficulty:** GM-set, 5–30 ladder, no fixed bands — our 11 baseline / 13–14 hard / 15 reach house benchmarks are legal Tier-1 texture. Prefer granting advantage/disadvantage over shifting Difficulty (SRD's own guidance).
- **Advantage/disadvantage:** ±1d6 on the roll; they cancel one-for-one.
- **Hope:** gained on ANY roll with Hope (success or failure) and on crits; spend to add an Experience's modifier, Help an Ally (adv die), Tag Team (3 Hope), class Hope features (3).
- **Reaction rolls:** generate no Hope/Fear, trigger no GM move, and can't be Helped. Crit on a reaction = ignore the effects, but no Hope/Stress bonus.
- **Crits (action rolls):** auto-succeed with a bonus, gain a Hope, clear a Stress.
- **Stress:** cleared by crits and downtime moves (features may also grant clears); marking your **last** Stress → Vulnerable; must-mark-but-can't → mark 1 HP instead.
- **Fear:** GM starts with 1/PC, gains when a PC rolls with Fear and on rests, cap 12; spends: interrupt/extra move, adversary or environment Fear feature, add an adversary Experience.
- **Countdowns:** standard (tick per action roll) and dynamic Progress/Consequence (tick 0–3 by outcome: crit 3 · Hope-success 2 · Fear-success 1 · failures 0/2/3 on the consequence side); loop/linked/long-term variants exist.
- **Conditions:** Hidden, Restrained, Vulnerable only — "Overwhelmed" (our celebration/heart files) is homebrew; run as its stated effect (mark Stress, lose spotlight), which is legal.
- **Adversary benchmarks** *(verified 2026-07-16 from official SRD blocks — use these when statting or resolving `[VERIFY]` tags)*:
  - **Tier 1 Solo** (Cave Ogre): Diff 13 · Thr 8/15 · HP 8 · Stress 3 · ATK +1 · 1d10+2
  - **Tier 1 Bruiser** (Bear): Diff 14 · Thr 9/17 · HP 7 · Stress 2 · ATK +1 · 1d8+3
  - **Tier 2 Skulk** (Stonewraith): Diff 13 · Thr 11/22 · HP 6 · Stress 3 · ATK +3 · 2d8+6
  - **Tier 2 Bruiser** (Shark): Diff 14 · Thr 14/28 · HP 7 · Stress 3 · ATK +2 · 2d12+1
  - **Tier 2 Solo** (Gorgon): Diff 15 · Thr 13/25 · HP 9 · Stress 3 · ATK +4
  - **Tier 3 Solo** (Hydra/Young Ice Dragon): Diff 18 · Thr 19/35–21/41 · HP 10 · Stress 5–6
  - **Relentless (X) — Passive** (exact SRD wording): "can be spotlighted X times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight them."
  - **Forced movement idiom:** "knocked back to [Very Close / Far / Very Far] range" — often paired with damage or Stress (Bear, Minotaur Wrecker).
  - **Feature-cost idioms:** Actions cost "Mark a Stress" or "Spend a Fear"; Reactions can cost "mark a Stress"; "Ramp Up — Passive" = must spend Fear to spotlight, then attacks all targets in range (Ogre/Minotaur).
  - *House note: our Tier 1 party means "Tier 2" threats get statted a shade soft of benchmark on purpose (the Mother Owlbear, the Bull Gar) — note it in the block, don't silently re-inflate.*
