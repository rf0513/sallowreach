# The Morning Spar — Drop-In for Session 6

*10–15 minutes. Slot before the museum. Tenson owes this from S4. The Drillfield, first light.*

---

## In Plain Words

Tenson promised Sable a real spar back in Session 4 and it's overdue. Three exchanges with practice staves, the guards keeping score on a post, Sable having the time of his life. The point isn't who wins — it's that Tenson earns respect through the body, not the seal. And between rounds, Tenson's trained eye catches a young guard with bad form — that's the Sweat First seed for later.

**Three rolls, three Experiences named, real Hope/Fear generated before the heavy emotional work starts.**

---

## The Scene

> Morning. The Drillfield. Sable's guards are already running laps in the wet air. The moment Sable sees you he lights up like you just made his whole week.
>
> "There he is! You owe me one, brain-boy. Thought you'd slipped off in the night."
>
> He's already tossing a practice stave. The guards are already drifting into a ring. Somebody's scratching a scoreboard on a post.

**The wager, before they start:**

> "Loser cooks tonight. And I mean *cooks* — none of that dried-ration sadness you call a meal."

---

### Round 1 — "Show Me What You've Got"

> Sable rolls his shoulders and starts circling. Loose grip, easy feet, that grin. He taps your stave twice — *tap, tap* — light, testing. Not trying to hit you. Trying to see how you move.
>
> The guards are watching but nobody's cheering yet. They've seen this before. This is the warm-up.

**🎲 Agility 12.** *No Experience fits — this is just reflexes.*

| Roll                | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Success w/ Hope** | Tenson catches the second tap and turns it — Sable's stave swings wide and he has to reset his whole stance. The guards make a low noise. Sable's grin gets bigger. *"Oh. Alright then."*                                               |
| **Success w/ Fear** | Tenson catches it clean — but Sable was already somewhere else. A tap on the shoulder from behind the parry. *"Just checking."* The guards laugh. He's showing off for his people and they love it.                                     |
| **Failure w/ Hope** | Sable walks through it. Two taps become three and Tenson's stepping back before he means to. But the feet hold — Sable nods, respecting the stance. *"Good legs. Let's see the rest."*                                                  |
| **Failure w/ Fear** | Sable puts him on his back foot in four moves, easy as breathing. The guards cheer for their sergeant. **Spend the Fear:** someone in the ring calls out *"Citadel sends their best!"* — the whole circle laughs. Not cruel. Just loud. |

*Mark the post. Move on.*

---

### Round 2 — "Now I'm Trying"

> He plants his feet. Stops circling. The grin's still there but it's different now — it's the grin of someone who's about to enjoy themselves for real.
>
> The guards go quiet. They've seen this version of Sable before. This is the part where their sergeant stops being fun and starts being good.

**🎲 Strength 13.** ⭐ *Spend a Hope for **+2 from Intense Discipline** — the trained soldier's form, held under real pressure. Say the name out loud.*

| Roll | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Success w/ Hope** | Tenson meets it. Full weight on full weight, clean block, and for one beat they're locked and even — eye to eye, neither giving. Sable's eyebrows go up. *"There it is. There's the soldier."* He means it. |
| **Success w/ Fear** | Tenson holds, barely. The block is good but Sable drove him back three steps and everyone saw it. He's stronger than he looks, which is saying something. *"You can take a hit. Good. Most people can't."* **Spend the Fear:** Sable turns to the ring — *"Somebody get brain-boy some water. He's going to need it for the last one."* |
| **Failure w/ Hope** | Sable puts him down. Not hard — *placed.* Tenson's on one knee looking up at a hand already reaching for him. *"You dropped your shoulder. You know you dropped your shoulder."* Coaching, not mocking. That's a sergeant. |
| **Failure w/ Fear** | Same thing, harder. Tenson hits the dirt and the crowd cheers. **Spend the Fear:** Sable turns to bow to his guards before offering the hand. Showmanship. This is his field and everybody in this ring knows it. |

*Mark the post.*

---

### ⭐ The Breath — Perrin

*Between rounds. No roll. Just hand this to the player:*

> While you're catching your breath, toweling off, you see a kid at the edge of the field. Young guard — nineteen, maybe. He's drilling alone. Stave work, basic strikes.
>
> His left shoulder drops every time he commits. You can see the whole problem from here — old injury, never treated right, compensated around until the compensation became the habit. He's going to blow that shoulder out inside a month and wash out of the guard.

*Pause. Let the player sit with it.*

> Sable follows your eyes. Watches you watch the kid. Says nothing.
>
> Picks up his stave.
>
> "One more."

---

### Round 3 — "The Last One"

> Staves up. Both breathing hard. The ring goes quiet on its own — nobody told them to.
>
> Sable's got his weight low and his eyes on yours. He's not circling, not testing, not showing off. The first two rounds were muscle. This one's about who sees the opening first.

**🎲 Instinct 13.** ⭐ *Spend a Hope for **+2 from Intense Discipline** — reading an opponent is a trained reflex.*

| Roll                | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Success w/ Hope** | Tenson sees it — a quarter-beat where Sable's weight shifts wrong — and takes it clean. Sable goes down on one knee. Dead silence. Then Sable *laughs*, loud, from the dirt. *"HA! Brain-boy's got HANDS!"* The ring breaks into noise.                                                                                                                                               |
| **Success w/ Fear** | Tenson sees it and takes it — but Sable saw it too, half a beat later, and chose to let it land. Tenson knows. A soldier who's sparred a thousand times knows what a good hit looks like, and this was Tenson's best. **Spend the Fear:** from the dirt, the smallest nod. He threw nothing. He just didn't block. That's worse than losing — it's generosity, and they both know it. |
| **Failure w/ Hope** | They both see it at the same time. Sable's faster. Tenson goes down. But it was *close* — and everyone in the ring heard the staves crack. Sable pulls him up one-handed. *"You're rusty. But you used to be something, cher. I can tell."*                                                                                                                                           |
| **Failure w/ Fear** | Sable reads him clean and puts him down before he sees it coming. Quick, decisive, almost gentle. **Spend the Fear:** from the dirt, Tenson hears a voice in the crowd — Perrin, the kid with the bad shoulder, shouting *"Get up!"* before thinking. The whole ring heard it. Sable heard it.                                                                                        |
|                     |                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

*Mark the post. Final score doesn't matter — read the next section either way.*

---

## After

**If Tenson took the best of 3:**

> "Alright, alright." [wiping his face, delighted — completely at ease with losing] "The Citadel sends someone who can fight. First time for everything." [he sits, grabs water, offers some] "Now. You had something behind your eyes the day you walked in here. What do you need?"

**If Sable took it:**

> "Don't make that face. You almost had the last one and you know it." [sitting, tossing a water skin] "Come back tomorrow and I'll show you where you're leaking." [the grin, still there, always there] "But first — what did you actually come here for?"

**Either way — the Perrin beat** *(this is the real payoff, and it's Sable being quietly smart):*

> "The kid." [he tilts his head toward where Perrin was drilling] "You saw it. The shoulder." [beat] "I've been trying to fix that for three months. I can see what wrong looks like — I just don't know what to *do* about it." [looking at Tenson properly, for the first time today without the grin] "You do, though. Don't you."

**That's Sweat First, planted.** Tenson can come back any time to coach Perrin. When he does, Sable is won for keeps — and the whole southwest guard opens up. Today is the seed. Don't resolve it today.

## What This Scene Does (GM)

- **3 rolls** with Hope/Fear before the museum — the economy is stocked before the emotional weight starts
- **Intense Discipline named twice** — teaching the Experience spend to Tenson's player specifically
- **Perrin planted** — the Sweat First payoff is now seeded with a face and a bad shoulder
- **Sable earned** — not through the seal, not through the audit. Through the thing Tenson has been trying to leave behind.
- **⚠ This scene takes the fun register.** The museum takes the heavy one. **Do not reverse them.** Fun → heavy is a session. Heavy → fun is a mood swing.
