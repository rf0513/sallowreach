# Session 8 — Player Handouts *(for Roll20)*

**In plain words:** three short handouts for the players, one for each thing they found on the day before the Celebration: the old man's story about the shrine root, the page they read in the founders' book at the Hall, and Leavy's potion recipe from Orna's notebook. Everything here is something the party actually saw or heard at the table. Nothing is a GM secret. Copy each block into its own Roll20 handout.

*GM note: the folk tale doesn't name the spear. The players connect it themselves. If Prosper said "Tidebreaker" out loud at the table, add the name.*

---

## Handout 1 — *The Old Root* (the shrine story, as Prosper tells it)

> Old story. My grandmother's. Don't hold me to it.
>
> Before there was a city here, she was already here. The root, I mean. Her. Folks say she was a tree once — a whole one — and you can't imagine the size of her.
>
> She got hurt, once. Long before any of us. Lost a piece of herself.
>
> She had a sweetheart. Something out in the deep water, the story goes. And her sweetheart took that piece, and made it into something. Something sharp. A gift — to give back to her.
>
> Never got to give it to her, though. That's where the story stops.
>
> Anyway. She's warm, if you put your hand on her. Always has been. Her roots go down further than anybody's looked. And on Celebration night, she glows.
>
> That part's not a story. Come see.

## Handout 2 — *The Founders' Book* (Hall of the Mire archive)

> A big book. Wooden covers, the leather gone soft as an old glove. One page for every City Leader, going back about two hundred years. Every page is a different hand. Every page says the same thing:
>
> ***Received of*** *[the leader before] —* ***the keys, the seal, and the box.***
>
> ---
>
> **The last page** — small, neat handwriting:
> *Adaline Roux. Received of —* [her predecessor] *— the keys, the seal, and the box.*
>
> **The first page** — the ink gone brown as tea. The first name's washed out by water. You can just make out the last one:
>
> *…Sauvage.*
>
> *Received of —*
>
> And then nothing. A dash, where a name should be.
>
> *— the keys, the seal, and the box.*

## Handout 3 — *Glow-Eye* (Orna's notebook, Leavy's copy)

> **GLOW-EYE** *(see in the dark — mostly)*
>
> - one fist of marsh-lichen. the GREEN crust. not the grey. *(grey = you hear colors. ask me how I know)*
> - three drops of anything that glows on its own
> - water drawn on lantern night. *takes better. don't ask me why.*
>
> steep it. **DO NOT BOIL THE LICHEN** *(unless you want glowing eyes for a week)*
>
> *— tested on myself. saw in the dark two nights running. also my toes glowed.*
>
> *[sketch in the margin: a frog in a hat]*

## Handout 4 — *What We Know* (the curse & the Celebration — Sessions 1–8)

*GM note: facts only, one line each, grouped by where the party found them — **no connections drawn.** Arranging them is the players' job. Every line is confirmed in a recap. If the Tallykeeper's "Nine stay" landed in S7, add it under **The dead & the spirits**.*

> **The night we arrived**
> - We went under in the black water on a Celebration night. As we sank, something took hold of the deepest part of us.
> - We can't leave the Mire. Near the edge we get sick and weak, worse with every stroke, until we can't go on.
>
> **Augury Vane** *(his journal)*
> - Two hundred years ago, a Guild wizard went under on a festival night, in the same black water.
> - He could never leave either. Thirty-one years on a five-year posting. Six recalls, all refused. The Guild commended him for his "devotion."
>
> **The salt wall**
> - The wall threw Isotta — in her ghost form — about two hundred feet.
> - The wall is failing fast. But for two days after every Celebration, it settles. *(Aldric)*
>
> **The dead & the spirits**
> - On Celebration days, the spirits and the dead leave the city, "like animals before a disaster." *(the Tallykeeper; Vael agrees)*
> - The day before, Isotta watched the dead walking out of the city. They were thickest at the Hall of the Mire.
>
> **The Hall of the Mire**
> - For two hundred years, every City Leader has signed for *the keys, the seal, and the box.* The first one received them from nobody.
> - Adaline keeps the box. She says the old words are a blessing.
>
> **The old root**
> - It's warm. It goes down further than anyone's looked — toward something under the Hall.
> - It glows during the Celebration.
> - The shrine's old story: she was hurt once and lost a piece of herself. Her sweetheart made it into something sharp — a gift, never given.
>
> **The spear**
> - The Tidebreaker cuts curses. Marcel Dray has it.
> - Caspar told us a version of the same story: made by one great spirit, as a gift for another.

## Handout 5 — *What We Know* (the snake — Sessions 1–8)

*GM note: same rules — facts only, confirmed in recaps, no connections drawn. **Two to confirm before you upload:** did S7's deck-at-dusk beat fire — **the 2H-3 brand in Fletcher's own journal**? And did Fletcher take **the broken obsidian fang** out of the gar? If yes, add them under **The rookery**.*

> **What it is**
> - It's an amphisbaena — a serpent with two heads. Fletcher has been on its trail for three years.
> - It knows it's being hunted. *"Don't let the hunter become the hunted."* *(Delphine)*
>
> **The rookery** *(Old Plenty)*
> - The deep channels were emptied four years ago.
> - The spirit kept a twelve-foot shed skin, high in the branches, for four years.
> - The snake knocked the Mire out of balance. That's what drove the Bull Gar out of its own water and into the nests.
>
> **The skin** *(Brin)*
> - It's stitched straight down the spine — two different skins, joined. Brin says that's lab work.
> - It's stamped **2H-3.** Brin knows the mark: imperial cargo freight.
>
> **Gault**
> - He sells live creatures in sealed jars. *"We don't do business with Gault."* *(Remy)*
> - He tried to buy Brin's snake maps and shed skins. She threw him out of her own guild hall.
> - He works the deep channels with traps. Fletcher put an arrow across his bow; he ran and left the traps behind. Brin says they're poacher's gear.
>
> **What's next**
> - Brin is writing to old contacts about a hunt.
