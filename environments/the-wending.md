# The Wending

**Tier 1 Traversal**

> ⚙️ **Engine under [[mire-complications]]** *(audit 2026-07-17)* — the travel loop's mechanical home; the complications table is its draw deck. Mechanics swept vs [[STATUS]] SRD Facts. Slimmed to house style same day (content unchanged).

*"The Mire is not lost. You are."* — the network of named, shifting navigable channels locals memorize over generations. The party crosses it every time they leave [[Sallowreach]].

**Impulses:** Reroute the unwary, swallow the road behind them, reach (always, slowly, toward the city), reward those who listen to the water.

**Difficulty:** **11 with a guide** ([[Remy Duval]]) · **13 without** · **15 past the known channels** (the Deep Mire — and consult [[mire-complications]] for what Lost means out there).

**Potential Adversaries:** [[Gault]] working a stripped basin · [[mire-bestiary|Mire beasts]] (ridgeback crawler, glass snake, bloodmidge swarm) · [[sallowreach-roster#Wending Runner|Wending Runners]] (punt-pirates, poachers in Gault's pay).

## Features

**The Shifting Road - Passive:** Crossing = **Progress Countdown (4)**; one PC leads with Instinct rolls, the others read water and call turns.
- Success: +1 (+2 with Hope). **With Hope:** the lead also learns one true thing about the route ahead.
- **With Fear**, GM picks: arrive late and drained (each PC marks 1 Stress), *or* a waterway that existed last season is gone — no tick, and you're somewhere you didn't mean to be.
- With a guide: the guide rolls; the party only handles what surfaces.

**Lost - Reaction:** On a navigation fail with Fear (or GM's call), the party is **Lost**:
- **No Long Rest while Lost** — the one mechanic that makes a wrong turn frightening. Lean on it.
- The crossing's Countdown freezes until a successful navigation roll clears it.
- Each hour Lost, the GM may spend a Fear for a Mire encounter (*Something Surfaces* / a [[mire-complications]] draw).

**The Hinge - Action:** The current grabs the boat at the channel that turns wrong. Each PC: Agility Reaction Roll or the punt slews into the mangroves — 1d6+1 physical, and one carried item goes into the black water unless someone spends their action to catch it. A guide who knows the Hinge grants advantage.

**Something Surfaces - Action:** Spend a Fear — a Mire threat at Close range, spotlight immediately. Inner Wending: curious or territorial. Deep Mire: *drawn*, as if the water pointed them here.

**Root-Choke - Reaction:** Forcing a shortcut, pushing after dark, moving faster than the Mire allows: roots seize the hull — the boat is Restrained until a Strength or Agility roll clears it, or someone goes over the side to cut it free. The water is not empty.

**The Reaching Dark - Action:** *(Fear-gated.)* When the party is Lost, deep, or moving against the grain: the bioluminescence arranges itself into a pattern that tracks nothing in the wind, and every PC feels *examined by something very old.* Each marks 1 Stress; next navigation roll at disadvantage. This is the [[The Mangrove Spirit|Mangrove Spirit's]] attention brushing past — aimed *through* them, toward [[Sallowreach]], never at them. **Do not name it.**

## Running It *(five rules)*

- **Texture, not a tax.** Most crossings: Countdown (4), two or three rolls, party talking the whole time.
- **With a guide it's wondrous** (Diff 11, Remy's master's-eye tour). **Without, it's a cage with teeth** (Diff 13, Lost is real, no rest).
- **Take away a route they've used** at least once — *"the cut you ran last week is roots to the waterline now."* That's Front 1 made tactile.
- **Spend Fear only when a crossing should mean something** — a race against Gault, a panicked return, the first push past the known channels.
- **The Reaching Dark ties to [[world-in-motion]]** — use it sparingly, never explain it, let them carry the unease to town.

## Feature Questions

- *Who leads the navigation, and what do they have to prove — to the water, the locals, or themselves?*
- *What's different since the last time they came this way, now that the grief is rising?*
- *If Remy's aboard, what does he notice and quietly file away?*
- *When the Reaching Dark passes through, what does each PC privately suspect it wants?*
