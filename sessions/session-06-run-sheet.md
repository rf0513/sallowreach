## 🧭 In Plain Words *(the whole session — no jargon)*

You pick up where you stopped: four people in a dead curator's museum, mid-afternoon, a two-hundred-year-old journal still open on the table. The front door opens and the woman who runs the city walks in — she was his friend, she's come to decide what happens to the building, and she finds four foreigners in it. She'll answer anything they ask about the weekly festival, warmly and at length, and tell them nothing they need — including that the next one is in three days.

Then the day splits four ways: the ranger goes to the woodworkers' guild for materials to fix the bow he stole, the witch finally calls her familiar at dusk and gets a bird that already knows her, the auditor writes the false report he's been dreading and carries it to the embassy for a signature, and the half-ghost goes wherever her player takes her.

It ends at the inn with one question. The swamp trip they owe a friend takes two days. The innkeeper flatly refuses to sail across the festival, and the festival is in three. Go now, or spend those three days in town on the crime boss, the imperial officer, and the wall-keeper — and end the session on the choice.

- **Isotta** has had a heron following her for three sessions and has not asked it a direct question.

Don't announce it. When an improvised beat needs a direction, aim it at: *what are you not looking at, and what does the not-looking cost?*

## 🎲 Dice First

*Restored from [[session-03-run-sheet]], where it worked and then quietly fell out of the format. **The reflex:** the moment a PC tries to* get *something — read a person, find a thing, push past a "no," steady their own hands — call the roll* **before** *you narrate the result.*

**Action roll** = 2d12 (Hope die + Fear die) + trait vs **Difficulty.** Hope die high → the PC gains a **Hope.** Fear die high → **you** gain a **Fear.** Matching dice = **Crit** (auto-succeed, gain a Hope, clear a Stress).

- ⭐ **Spend a Hope → +2 from a relevant Experience.** *This is the one that's been missing all campaign.* Every roll below names which Experience applies. **Say the name out loud when you call the roll** — *"that's a Budding Bureaucrat problem, if you want it to be."* The player still has to justify it in the fiction; that justification **is** the roleplay.
- **Help an Ally** → the helper **spends 1 Hope** and rolls **+d6** (several helpers, only the **highest** d6 counts).
- **Tag Team** → **3 Hope**, two PCs on one action. Nobody at this table has ever used it. **Offer it once tonight, out loud.**
- **One PC rolls, the others Help.** Don't gang-roll the same thing.
- ⚠ **Reaction rolls** generate **no Hope/Fear** and **can't be Helped.**
- **Gate the cost, not the fun.** They always learn *something*; the die decides the **price** and the **bonus**.

**⭐ The new habit — every roll has four answers, not two.** Success-with-Hope · success-with-Fear · failure-with-Hope · failure-with-Fear. Every roll below is written out that way, and **the Fear spend lives inside the failure-with-Fear row** — so Fear arrives *with* the bad roll instead of waiting on a list you have to remember. That is the whole trick.

**Diff ladder:** 11 = Tier-1 baseline · 13–14 = hard · 15 = a real reach.

#### 🎙️ Playing Adaline — first meeting, read this before the scene

**Find the voice:** measured, unhurried. **Warm the way a wall is warm in the sun.** She looks at people *completely* — hold eye contact one beat past comfortable before you answer. She says **"we"** for the city and means two hundred years of it.

**The one physical thing to do:** don't fill silences. She's the most important person in every room she's ever been in and stopped needing to announce it decades ago. If a player trails off, let it sit. That single habit *is* the character.

**Grab-lines — you need no others** ([[voice-cards]]):

- **Opening / the honored-guest line** *(canon — use it, it's her first impression)*: "The Mire caught you. That's not an accident. Nothing in the Mire is an accident."
- **The laugh:** "Six imperial liaisons in eight years, and every one of them told me what we *deserve.*" *[dry]* "You four just asked where the food was. It's why you're still invited to things."
- **Pressure, if they push her on anything:** "You're asking me to look at something. I've been the one deciding what this city looks at for twenty-two years." *[beat]* "So before I do — tell me what it costs us if you're right."
- **⭐ The slip — Front 8's tell, land it once:** "The Celebration keeps us. That's all any of us needs to know." *[a half-beat too long before 'needs' — that pause is the whole front. Don't comment on it.]*
- **When surprised:** she doesn't move. "Say that once more. Exactly the way you said it."
- **Exit:** "The city decided about you before I did. I'm rarely ahead of it."

**If they ask about the Celebration and you need an answer fast** — every one is warm, complete, and empty. Deliver *satisfied*, never cagey:

| They ask… | She says |
|---|---|
| What do the old words mean? | Her family's version, with total confidence — and it won't match anyone else's. Nobody has ever compared. |
| Why does the form never change? | The scripted covenant line below. Verbatim. |
| When did it start? | "Founding year." Which one? There's no page where it begins — the oldest ledger already assumes it exists. |
| What is it *for*? | "The city being polite to something bigger than it." She finds the question sweet. |

⚠ **She is never cagey and never evasive.** She answers everything, warmly, at length. **The horror is that she's satisfied.** Nobody in this city finds the question interesting — that's the whole effect.

**The three things she does, in any order:**

1. **She grieves, plainly.** Caspar was a real friend. One short story about arguing with him. **Tenson's knife** ([[twist-the-knife]]) — he refused the funeral and now stands in the room while someone mourns for real. Say nothing. Let him sit there.
2. ⭐ **She walks the room, and things are missing.** The scene's engine — run it off [[set-piece-the-empty-shelves]]. *(⚰️ Retired 2026-07-25: she does **not** ask the party what Caspar would have wanted done with the place. They're strangers who've been in the city a month; she'd never ask them.)*

**Her scripted line — use it verbatim when they ask why the form never changes** ([[adaline-roux]] §Dialogue Hooks):

> "Because the form is the point. Care expressed in repetition is what makes it real. You don't honor something by deciding you understand it better than everyone who came before you." *[a beat, something underneath the certainty]* "You just keep faith. That's what a covenant asks."

**The date, said out loud:**

> "Three days. You'll have been here a month by then, near enough — first one you'll actually stand in." *[she looks at them properly]* "Come. It's not a spectacle. It's the city being polite to something bigger than it."

🎲 **If a PC reads her — Instinct or Presence 13:** she is completely sincere and there is a floor under the sincerity she has never lifted. She *does not know*. On a Fear result, she notices the reading and asks, pleasantly, what they were hoping to find.
